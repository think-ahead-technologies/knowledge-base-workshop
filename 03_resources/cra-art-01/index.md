# CRA Art. 1 - Subject matter (Gegenstand)

## Summary
Article 1 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") states what the regulation lays down: (a) rules for
making "Produkte mit digitalen Elementen" available on the market, (b) essential
cybersecurity requirements ("grundlegende Cybersicherheitsanforderungen") for
their design, development and production plus obligations of economic operators
("Wirtschaftsakteure"), (c) essential requirements for manufacturers'
vulnerability-handling processes ("Verfahren zur Behandlung von Schwachstellen")
over the expected product lifetime ("erwartete Nutzungsdauer"), and (d) rules for
market surveillance and enforcement. The captured page is the German text as
republished by Kunnus, with a vendor FAQ (non-binding) and a list of 7 related
recitals; only the Official Journal text is binding.
