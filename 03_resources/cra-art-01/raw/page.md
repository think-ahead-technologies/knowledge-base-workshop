**Article 1, Subject matter (paraphrased)**
The regulation sets out four things:
- **(a)** Rules for placing products with digital elements on the market to ensure their cybersecurity.
- **(b)** Basic cybersecurity requirements for design, development and production, and economic operators' obligations for those products.
- **(c)** Basic cybersecurity requirements for manufacturers' vulnerability-handling processes over the expected product lifetime, and operators' obligations regarding those processes.
- **(d)** Market surveillance and enforcement rules for the requirements in this article.

**FAQ (summarized)**
- **What does Article 1 regulate?** It defines the regulation's scope: cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements and their manufacturers across the lifecycle, from market placement to end of support.
- **Which actors does the CRA target?** Directly, manufacturers, importers and distributors. Indirectly, suppliers (through SBOM requirements) and end customers (through transparency duties).
- **Is the CRA horizontal or sector-specific?** Horizontal, covering all sectors. Exceptions apply where other EU cybersecurity rules already apply, such as medical devices, motor vehicles and aviation.
