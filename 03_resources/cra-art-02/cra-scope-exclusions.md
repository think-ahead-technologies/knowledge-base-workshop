# CRA scope exclusions - sector law, national security, delegated limitation

Art. 2(2)-(5), (7) and (8) CRA carve products out of the regulation (the spare
parts exclusion of Art. 2(6) has its own note):

- **(2) Sector regulations** - not applicable to products covered by
  Verordnung (EU) 2017/745 (medical devices), (EU) 2017/746 (in-vitro
  diagnostics) and (EU) 2019/2144 (motor vehicle type approval).
- **(3) Aviation** - not applicable to products "die nach der Verordnung (EU)
  2018/1139 zertifiziert worden sind". Note the trigger is certification, not
  mere coverage.
- **(4) Marine equipment** - not applicable to equipment ("Geräte") within the
  scope of Richtlinie 2014/90/EU.
- **(5) Delegated limitation or exclusion** - for products under other Union law
  addressing all or some of the risks covered by the essential cybersecurity
  requirements in Annex I, application can be limited or excluded if (a) this is
  consistent with the general legal framework for those products and (b) the
  sector rules reach the same or a higher protection level. The Commission does
  this by delegated acts under Art. 61.
- **(7) National security and defence** - not applicable to products developed or
  modified "ausschließlich für Zwecke der nationalen Sicherheit oder für
  Verteidigungszwecke", nor to products specifically designed to process
  classified information ("Verschlusssachen").
- **(8) Information disclosure limit** - obligations never require supplying
  information whose disclosure would run against Member States' essential
  interests of national security, public security or defence.

Commission guidance 9.3 (non-binding) on the vehicle exclusion: components are
excluded only if intended and suitable exclusively for integration into such
vehicles (VO (EU) 2019/2144, VO (EU) Nr. 168/2013). Objective distribution
conditions decide - generic components sold via open retail/online channels are
in CRA scope regardless of intended-use statements. EU type-examination
certificates covering cybersecurity under the RED delegated act or the Machinery
Regulation remain usable as conformity evidence for the risks they cover until
11 June 2028 at the latest; uncovered CRA risks (vulnerability handling, data
minimisation, attack surface reduction) must be addressed additionally.
