# CRA spare parts exception

Art. 2(6) CRA: the regulation does not apply to "Ersatzteile, die auf dem Markt
bereitgestellt werden, um identische Komponenten in Produkten mit digitalen
Elementen zu ersetzen, und die nach denselben Spezifikationen hergestellt werden
wie die Bauteile, die sie ersetzen sollen". Recital 29 gives the rationale:
effective repair and longer product life.

Commission guidance 4.1-4.2 (non-binding) as summarised on the page:

- Repair, refurbishment and maintenance are usually not a substantial
  modification while intended purpose and risk level stay unchanged; swapping a
  defective part for a better-performing one is not one by itself (example 35).
- "Identical" is judged by security-relevant properties, not part identity: a new
  chipset with the same protocols and security mechanisms can be identical
  (example 38); a changed crypto implementation or secure-boot mechanism is not
  (example 37).
- The repair purpose must be evident from the supply context (product named in
  order/offer, after-sales channel); keep evidence for market surveillance.
- The exception also covers replacing whole modules or sub-products in larger
  systems (example 39: CPU unit or complete PLC).
