# CRA Art. 2 - Scope (Anwendungsbereich)

## Summary
Article 2 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") defines which products the regulation covers. It
applies to products with digital elements made available on the market whose
intended or reasonably foreseeable use includes a direct or indirect, logical or
physical data connection to a device or network (para. 1). Paragraphs 2-8
exclude products under medical-device, in-vitro-diagnostic and vehicle
type-approval law, aviation-certified products, marine equipment, identical
spare parts and national security / defence / classified-information products,
allow further limitation by delegated act, and cap disclosure obligations at
Member States' security interests. The captured page is the German text as
republished by Kunnus plus summaries of the (non-binding) EU Commission guidance
of 27 July 2026 (C(2026) 5252 final) and a list of related recitals; only the
Official Journal text is binding.
