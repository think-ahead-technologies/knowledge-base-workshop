# CRA conformity assessment, notified bodies, CE marking and standards

Conformity actors and procedure:
- "notifizierende Behörde" (26): the national authority responsible for setting up and carrying out procedures for the assessment, designation and notification of conformity assessment bodies and their monitoring.
- "Konformitätsbewertung" (27): the process verifying whether the essential cybersecurity requirements in Annex I are fulfilled.
- "Konformitätsbewertungsstelle" (28): as defined in Art. 2 point 13 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.
- "notifizierte Stelle" (29): a conformity assessment body designated under Art. 43 of the CRA and other relevant Union harmonisation legislation.
- "CE-Kennzeichnung" (31): marking by which a manufacturer indicates that the product and the manufacturer's processes conform to the essential cybersecurity requirements in Annex I and other applicable Union harmonisation legislation providing for its affixing.

Market framework and standards (mostly by reference):
- "Harmonisierungsrechtsvorschriften der Union" (32): legislation listed in Annex I of Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 plus any other Union legislation harmonising marketing conditions to which that regulation applies.
- "Marktüberwachungsbehörde" (33): as in Art. 3 point 4 of Regulation (EU) 2019/1020.
- "internationale Norm" (34), "europäische Norm" (35), "harmonisierte Norm" (36): as in Art. 2(1)(a), (b), (c) of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012.

## Mentions
- Annex I (essential cybersecurity requirements)
- Art. 43 (designation of notified bodies)
- External: Regulation (EC) No 765/2008, Regulation (EU) 2019/1020, Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012
