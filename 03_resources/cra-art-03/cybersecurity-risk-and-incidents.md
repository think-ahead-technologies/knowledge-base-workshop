# CRA cybersecurity risk, incidents and near misses

Risk terms:
- "Cybersicherheitsrisiko" (37): the potential for loss or disruption caused by an incident, expressed as a combination of the magnitude of such loss or disruption and the likelihood of the incident occurring.
- "erhebliches Cybersicherheitsrisiko" (38): a cybersecurity risk which, based on its technical characteristics, can be assumed to have a high likelihood of an incident that could have a severe negative impact, causing considerable material or non-material loss or disruption.

Incident terms:
- "Sicherheitsvorfall" (43): as defined in Art. 6 point 6 of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS 2).
- "Sicherheitsvorfall mit Auswirkungen auf die Sicherheit des Produkts mit digitalen Elementen" (44): an incident that negatively affects or may affect the product's ability to protect the availability, authenticity, integrity or confidentiality of data or functions.
- "Beinahe-Vorfall" (45): near miss as in Art. 6 point 5 of Directive (EU) 2022/2555.
- "Cyberbedrohung" (46) and "Cybersicherheit" (3): by reference to Art. 2 points 8 and 1 of Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Cybersecurity Act).
- "als Koordinator benanntes CSIRT" (51): a CSIRT designated as coordinator under Art. 12(1) of Directive (EU) 2022/2555.
