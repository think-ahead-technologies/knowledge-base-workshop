# CRA economic operators (Wirtschaftsakteure)

"Wirtschaftsakteur" (point 12) covers the manufacturer, authorised representative, importer, distributor, or any other natural or legal person subject to obligations relating to manufacturing or making available products with digital elements under the regulation.

- "Hersteller" (13): a natural or legal person who develops or manufactures products with digital elements, or has them designed, developed or manufactured, and markets them under its name or trademark - "sei es gegen Bezahlung, zur Monetarisierung oder unentgeltlich" (for payment, monetisation or free of charge).
- "Bevollmächtigter" (15): a person established in the Union with a written mandate from a manufacturer to perform specified tasks on its behalf.
- "Einführer" (16): a person established in the Union who places on the Union market a product bearing the name or trademark of a person established outside the Union.
- "Händler" (17): a person in the supply chain, other than manufacturer or importer, who makes a product available on the Union market without changing its properties.

Related actor definitions: "Verbraucher" (18) is a natural person acting outside trade, business, craft or profession; "Kleinstunternehmen", "kleine Unternehmen", "mittlere Unternehmen" (19) follow the annex of Recommendation 2003/361/EC. The open-source software steward (14) is a separate, non-manufacturer role (see the open-source note).
