# CRA Art. 3 - Definitions (Begriffsbestimmungen)

## Summary
Article 3 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") is the definitions article: 51 numbered terms used
throughout the regulation, from "Produkt mit digitalen Elementen" and
"Datenfernverarbeitung" over the economic operators, placing on the market,
conformity assessment and standards, to risk, vulnerability and incident terms.
Several definitions are made by reference to other EU acts (Regulation (EU)
2019/881, Directive (EU) 2022/2555, Regulations 765/2008, 2019/1020, 1025/2012,
2016/679, Recommendation 2003/361/EC). The captured Kunnus page adds summaries of
the non-binding Commission guidance of 27 July 2026 (C(2026) 5252 final),
sections 2.1-2.5, 3.1-3.2, 4.3, 8.1 and 8.3, and lists related recitals. Only
the Official Journal text is binding.
