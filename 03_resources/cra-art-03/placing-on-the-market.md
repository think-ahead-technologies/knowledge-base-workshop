# CRA placing on the market and making available

"Inverkehrbringen" / "in den Verkehr bringen" (point 21) is the first making available of a product with digital elements on the Union market. "Bereitstellung auf dem Markt" (point 22) is any supply of a product for distribution or use on the Union market in the course of a commercial activity ("im Rahmen einer Geschäftstätigkeit"), whether for payment or free of charge.

Commission guidance (non-binding):
- 2.1: standalone software is placed on the market once the manufacturing phase is complete and it is first offered for distribution or use in the EU. All copies of the same version count as placed on the market at that moment, regardless of individual download dates; later downloads are only "making available". Iterations without substantial modification do not shift the date; a substantial modification counts as new placing on the market. Variants (per-OS builds, feature bundles) are separate products. Example: version 1.0.0 first offered 1 January 2028 - all copies count as placed on 1 January 2028.
- 2.3: source code licensed commercially to customers is placed on the market even if the customer must adapt and compile it; the licensor is not responsible for the customer's downstream modifications. Publicly shared FOSS code, unfinished in-development code, and demo/tutorial code are not placed on the market.
