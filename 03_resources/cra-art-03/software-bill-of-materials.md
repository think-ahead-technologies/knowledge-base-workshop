# CRA software bill of materials (Software-Stückliste)

"Software-Stückliste" (SBOM, point 39) is a formal record of the details and supply-chain relationships of the components included in the software elements of a product with digital elements. Article 3 only defines the term; it does not prescribe a format. Guidance section 2.4 (as summarized on the page) notes that companion apps and drivers belong in the SBOM of the hardware product they form one product with.
