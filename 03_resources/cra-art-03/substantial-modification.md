# CRA substantial modification (wesentliche Änderung)

"Wesentliche Änderung" (point 30) is a change to the product with digital elements after it has been placed on the market that affects its compliance with the essential cybersecurity requirements in Annex I Part I, or results in a modification of the intended purpose for which the product was assessed.

Commission guidance (non-binding) section 4.3: the yardstick is not the size of the change but its effect on the risk profile. An update that changes the assessed intended purpose or creates new risks not covered by the risk assessment is a substantial modification; security updates generally are not. Features already anticipated and assessed in the original risk assessment (including later activation of existing assessed functions) do not trigger it; even a small feature can (e.g. a "Remember me" login with locally stored tokens creates token-theft/session-hijacking risk). Security updates are substantial only exceptionally, e.g. when they fundamentally change intended purpose, data flows or dependency structure (local encryption becomes a cloud service; external key management replaces internal). Four test criteria (Rn. 110): new threat vectors, new attack scenarios, changed likelihood, changed impact of known scenarios. Regardless of classification, risk assessment and technical documentation must be kept up to date. A substantial modification counts as a new placing on the market (guidance 2.1).
