# CRA support period (Unterstützungszeitraum)

"Unterstützungszeitraum" (point 20) is the period during which the manufacturer must ensure that vulnerabilities of the product with digital elements are handled effectively and in accordance with the essential cybersecurity requirements in Annex I Part II. The definition ties the time dimension of manufacturer obligations to vulnerability handling rather than to general product maintenance.
