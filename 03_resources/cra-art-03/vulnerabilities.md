# CRA vulnerability, exploitable and actively exploited vulnerability

Three graded terms:
- "Schwachstelle" (40): a weakness, susceptibility or flaw of a product with digital elements that can be exploited by a cyber threat.
- "ausnutzbare Schwachstelle" (41): a vulnerability that can effectively be used by an unauthorised third party under practical operational conditions.
- "aktiv ausgenutzte Schwachstelle" (42): a vulnerability for which there is reliable evidence that a malicious actor has exploited it in a system without the system owner's permission.

The grading matters because the bar rises from theoretical weakness to practical exploitability to proven exploitation in the wild. "Cyberbedrohung" (46), used in point 40, is defined by reference to Art. 2 point 8 of Regulation (EU) 2019/881.
