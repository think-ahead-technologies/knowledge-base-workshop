# CRA trade fair and prototype exception

Art. 4 (2) CRA: Member States do not prevent the "Präsentation oder Verwendung"
of a product with digital elements that does not comply with the CRA "bei Messen,
Ausstellungen, Vorführungen oder ähnlichen Veranstaltungen, einschließlich
Prototypen".

Condition: the product carries a "sichtbare Kennzeichnung" that clearly states it
does not comply with the regulation and may only be made available on the market
once it does.

Takeaway: non-compliant products and prototypes may be shown and demonstrated at
events, but only with a visible non-conformity label; this is presentation, not
making available on the market.
