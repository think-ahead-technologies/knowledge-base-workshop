# CRA unfinished software testing exception

Art. 4 (3) CRA: Member States do not prevent making available on the market
"unfertiger Software, die dieser Verordnung nicht entspricht", provided that:

- it is made available only "für einen begrenzten Zeitraum ..., der für
  Testzwecke erforderlich ist", and
- it carries a "sichtbare Kennzeichnung" clearly stating that it does not comply
  with the regulation and will not be available on the market except for testing.

Art. 4 (4): para. 3 does not apply to "Sicherheitsbauteile im Sinne von anderen
Harmonisierungsrechtsvorschriften der Union als dieser Verordnung" - unfinished
software that is a safety component under other harmonisation law cannot use the
testing exception.

Commission guidance C(2026) 5252 final, section 2.3 (non-binding, as quoted on the
page): unfinished software ("Alpha, Beta, Release Candidate") may be provided for
a limited time for test and feedback purposes under Art. 4 (3); unfinished code in
development is not considered placed on the market.

Takeaway: public betas / release candidates are possible without full CRA
conformity, but time-limited, test-only and visibly labelled - and never for
safety components.
