# CRA Art. 4 - Free movement (Freier Verkehr)

## Summary
Article 4 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") guarantees free movement in the internal market:
Member States must not impede making compliant "Produkte mit digitalen Elementen"
available on the market (para. 1). It also sets two tolerated exceptions for
non-compliant products: presentation at "Messen, Ausstellungen, Vorführungen oder
ähnlichen Veranstaltungen", including prototypes, with a visible non-conformity
marking (para. 2), and making "unfertige Software" available for a limited
testing period, again with a visible marking (para. 3). The testing exception does
not apply to "Sicherheitsbauteile" under other Union harmonisation legislation
(para. 4). The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus, with
an excerpt of the (non-binding) Commission guidance C(2026) 5252 final; only the
Official Journal text is binding.
