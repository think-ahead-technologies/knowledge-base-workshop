# Additional national cybersecurity requirements for procurement or use

Art. 5(1) CRA keeps a door open for Member States: the regulation does not
prevent them from subjecting products with digital elements to additional
cybersecurity requirements ("zusätzlichen Cybersicherheitsanforderungen") when
those products are procured or used for specific purposes. This expressly
includes procurement or use for national security or defence ("für Zwecke der
nationalen Sicherheit oder Verteidigung").

Two conditions apply: the requirements must be consistent with the Member
States' obligations under Union law, and they must be necessary and
proportionate ("notwendig und verhältnismäßig") to achieve those purposes.

Practical consequence: a CRA-compliant product may still face stricter
cybersecurity demands in specific national tenders or deployments; CRA
conformity is a floor, not a ceiling, for such buyers.
