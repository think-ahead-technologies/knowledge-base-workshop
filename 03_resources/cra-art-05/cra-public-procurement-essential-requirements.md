# Essential cybersecurity requirements in public procurement

Art. 5(2) CRA makes CRA compliance a public procurement criterion. When Member
States procure products with digital elements that fall within the scope of the
regulation, they must ensure that compliance with the essential cybersecurity
requirements of Annex I ("grundlegenden Cybersicherheitsanforderungen gemäß
Anhang I") is taken into account in the award procedure ("im Vergabeverfahren
berücksichtigt wird").

This explicitly covers the manufacturer's ability to handle vulnerabilities
effectively ("Fähigkeit der Hersteller, Schwachstellen wirksam zu bewältigen") -
so tenders can weigh not only the product's security properties but also the
vendor's vulnerability-handling process.

The obligation applies without prejudice to ("Unbeschadet der") the EU public
procurement Directives 2014/24/EU and 2014/25/EU, i.e. it sits on top of the
existing procurement framework rather than replacing it.
