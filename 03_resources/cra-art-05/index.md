# CRA Art. 5 - Procurement or use of products with digital elements (Beschaffung oder Nutzung von Produkten mit digitalen Elementen)

## Summary
Article 5 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") governs how Member States buy and use "Produkte mit
digitalen Elementen". Paragraph 1 lets Member States impose additional
cybersecurity requirements ("zusätzlichen Cybersicherheitsanforderungen") when
procuring or using such products for specific purposes - including national
security or defence - provided the requirements are consistent with Union law
and "notwendig und verhältnismäßig". Paragraph 2 obliges Member States, without
prejudice to the procurement Directives 2014/24/EU and 2014/25/EU, to take
compliance with the essential cybersecurity requirements of Annex I - including
the manufacturer's ability to handle vulnerabilities effectively - into account
in the award procedure ("Vergabeverfahren"). The captured page is the German
text as republished by Kunnus with one related recital (34, truncated); only the
Official Journal text is binding.
