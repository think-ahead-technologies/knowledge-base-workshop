# CRA market-availability conditions - Annex I Part I and Part II

Art. 6 sets two cumulative conditions ("und") under which "Produkte mit digitalen
Elementen werden nur dann auf dem Markt bereitgestellt":

- **(a) Product properties** - the product meets the "grundlegenden
  Cybersicherheitsanforderungen in Anhang I Teil I". This holds under the
  condition that it is "ordnungsgemäß installiert, gewartet und bestimmungsgemäß
  oder unter vernünftigerweise vorhersehbaren Umständen verwendet" and, where
  applicable, the "erforderlichen Sicherheitsaktualisierungen installiert wurden".
  Compliance is therefore judged against intended use and reasonably
  foreseeable use, with any security updates installed.
- **(b) Manufacturer processes** - the "vom Hersteller festgelegten Verfahren"
  meet the essential cybersecurity requirements in Annex I Part II (the
  vulnerability-handling requirements).

So the article regulates both the product itself (Part I) and the manufacturer's
processes (Part II). Market availability needs both.

Kunnus' non-binding FAQ adds the following points:
- Core Part I themes: secure default configuration ("Sichere Standard-Konfiguration"),
  protection against unauthorised access, confidentiality and integrity of data,
  availability of critical functions, a minimised attack surface, and a secure
  update mechanism.
- Not every Annex I requirement has to apply to every product. If one does not
  apply, the manufacturer must justify that in the cybersecurity risk assessment
  in the technical documentation (Recital 55).
- The risk assessment is mandatory and decides which requirements are
  implemented and how. It is part of the technical documentation under Annex VII.
