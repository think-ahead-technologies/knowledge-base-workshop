# CRA Art. 6 - Requirements for products with digital elements (Anforderungen an Produkte mit digitalen Elementen)

## Summary
Article 6 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") links market availability to the essential
cybersecurity requirements. A product with digital elements may be made
available on the market only if (a) it meets Annex I Part I, assuming correct
installation, maintenance, intended or reasonably foreseeable use and installed
security updates, and (b) the manufacturer's processes meet Annex I Part II.
The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus. It also carries a
vendor quick answer and FAQ (non-binding) and lists recitals 35-40 as related.
Only the Official Journal text is binding.
