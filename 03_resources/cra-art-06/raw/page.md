**Article 6: Requirements for products with digital elements** (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I)

**Legal text (excerpt).** The page states that products with digital elements may be placed on the market only if:
- a) they meet the basic cybersecurity requirements in Annex I, Part I, provided they are properly installed, maintained, and used, and any necessary security updates have been installed. The page's wording is "sie den grundlegenden Cybersicherheitsanforderungen in Anhang I Teil I genügen."
- b) the manufacturer's procedures meet the basic cybersecurity requirements in Annex I, Part II. The page's wording is "die vom Hersteller festgelegten Verfahren den grundlegenden Cybersicherheitsanforderungen in Anhang I Teil II entsprechen."

**Quick answer for manufacturers.** Article 6 refers to the Annex I requirements, which must be met across the product lifecycle through risk assessment, secure development, and ongoing vulnerability handling.

**FAQ summary**
- **Core requirements:** Secure default configuration, protection against unauthorized access, confidentiality and integrity of data, availability of critical functions, minimized attack surface, and a secure update mechanism. The page refers readers to Annex I for details.
- **Applicability:** Not every Annex I requirement necessarily applies to every product. If one doesn't, the manufacturer should justify this in the cybersecurity risk assessment within the technical documentation (see Recital 55).
- **Risk assessment:** It is mandatory and guides how each requirement is implemented. It forms part of the technical documentation under Annex VII.

**Related recitals.** The page lists Recitals 35 to 40 by title only, with truncated previews. They cover topics such as transitional periods, CE marking, software released for testing, and substantial modifications through software updates. The full text is not shown.
