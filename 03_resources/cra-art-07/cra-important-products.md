# CRA important products - Class I and Class II

**Definition (Art. 7(1)).** A product with digital elements that has the core
functions of a product category listed in Annex III is a "wichtiges Produkt mit
digitalen Elementen" and is subject to the conformity assessment procedures of
Art. 32(2) and (3). Integrating such a product into another product does not, by
itself, make the host product subject to those procedures.

**Criteria (Art. 7(2)).** Annex III divides the categories into Class I and
Class II. Each category meets at least one of:
- a) the product primarily performs functions critical for the cybersecurity of
  other products, networks or services - including securing authentication and
  access, intrusion prevention and detection, endpoint security or network
  protection;
- b) the product performs a function carrying a significant risk of adverse
  effects (intensity, ability to disrupt, control or damage many other products or
  the health, safety or security of its users through direct manipulation), such
  as a central system function - network management, configuration control,
  virtualisation or processing of personal data.

**Changing the list (Art. 7(3)).** The Commission may amend Annex III by
delegated acts (Art. 61): add a category and specify its definition, move a
category between classes, or remove one, judged against the par. 2 criteria.
Delegated acts provide, where appropriate, a transition period of at least 12
months before the Art. 32(2)/(3) procedures apply - especially when a category is
added or moved from Class I to Class II - unless extreme urgency justifies a
shorter one.

**Technical descriptions (Art. 7(4)).** By 11 December 2025 the Commission adopts
an implementing act (examination procedure, Art. 62(2)) with the technical
description of the Annex III Class I/II categories and the Annex IV categories.
The page's guidance section names it: Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392.

**Practical consequence (vendor FAQ, non-binding).** Class I may use
self-assessment under Module A when fully harmonised standards are applied;
Class II always requires a notified body.
