# CRA Art. 7 - Important products with digital elements (Wichtige Produkte mit digitalen Elementen)

## Summary
Article 7 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") defines "wichtige Produkte mit digitalen Elementen":
products whose core functions ("Kernfunktionen") match a product category listed
in Annex III. These are split into Class I and Class II and are subject to the
conformity assessment procedures of Art. 32(2) and (3). Integrating such a
product into another product does not by itself pull the host product into those
procedures. Par. 2 gives the two criteria a category must meet (cybersecurity-
critical function, or significant risk of adverse impact through direct
manipulation); par. 3 empowers the Commission to amend Annex III via delegated
acts (Art. 61), normally with a transition period of at least 12 months; par. 4
required an implementing act with technical descriptions of the Annex III and
Annex IV categories by 11 December 2025 (examination procedure, Art. 62(2)). The
captured page also carries Commission guidance section 6.1 on core functionality,
a vendor FAQ and three related recitals; only the Official Journal text is
binding.

- **Vendor FAQ (Kunnus, non-binding)** - Class I (e.g. password managers, home routers, PLCs / "SPSen") may self-assess under Module A when fully harmonised standards are applied; Class II (e.g. hypervisors, firewalls, smartcards) always needs a notified body ("notifizierte Stelle"); full conformity deadline 11 December 2027, Class II manufacturers should approach a notified body 12+ months ahead.
- **Related recitals** - page lists recitals 41 (substantial modification), 42 (refurbishment, maintenance, repair per Regulation (EU) 2024/1781) and 43 (products are important when exploitation impact can be severe); FAQ also points to recital 45 (core functionality logic).
- **Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392** - technical descriptions of the Annex III / IV categories (cited by the guidance section).
