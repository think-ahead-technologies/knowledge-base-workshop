**Article 7: Important products with digital elements**

- **Classification:** Products whose core functions match a category in Annex III count as "important" and fall into Class I or Class II. The class determines the conformity assessment procedure, including whether a notified body is required.
- **Integration:** Including such a component in a larger product does not, by itself, subject the larger product to those procedures.
- **Criteria:** A category qualifies if its products mainly perform cybersecurity-critical functions for other products, networks, or services, or if they carry a significant risk of adverse impact through direct manipulation.
- **Amendments:** The Commission can amend Annex III through delegated acts, generally with a transition period of at least 12 months.
- **Technical descriptions:** An implementing act was due by 11 December 2025.

**Commission guidance, section 6.1 (core functionality)**

- Each product has one core functionality, which should be documented in its technical documentation. Additional features don't change the classification.
- Marketing, user manuals, and technical documentation must tell a consistent story.
- Separately marketed modules are classified individually.
- The technical descriptions of the categories are in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392.
- The page notes that the guidance is not legally binding. Only the Court of Justice of the EU can give binding interpretations.

**FAQ**

- **Class I vs. Class II:** Class I products (e.g., password managers, home routers, PLCs) can use self-assessment under Module A if fully harmonised standards are applied. Class II products (e.g., hypervisors, firewalls, smart cards) always require a notified body.
- **Deadline:** The full conformity deadline is 11 December 2027. The page advises Class II manufacturers to contact a notified body at least 12 months ahead because of capacity constraints.

**Related recitals:** 41 (substantial modification), 42 (overhaul, maintenance, and repair), and 43 (importance based on the severity of exploitation).
