# Criteria for amending Annex IV (critical product categories)

Art. 8(2) CRA empowers the Commission to adopt delegated acts (under Art. 61)
amending Annex IV to add or remove categories of "kritische Produkte mit
digitalen Elementen". When defining such categories and the required assurance
level of Art. 8(1), the Commission takes into account the criteria of Art. 7(2)
and must ensure the category meets at least one of:

- (a) essential entities per Art. 3 Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2) have a
  critical dependency ("kritische Abhängigkeit") on the product category;
- (b) incidents and exploited vulnerabilities concerning the category could
  lead to serious disruptions of critical supply chains across the internal
  market ("schwerwiegenden Störungen kritischer Lieferketten im gesamten
  Binnenmarkt").

Before adopting, the Commission carries out an assessment of the kind described
in Art. 8(1) (market impact, stakeholder consultation). The delegated acts must
provide a transition period of at least six months unless extreme urgency
justifies a shorter one.

Classification into Annex IV follows the product's core functionality; the
Commission guidance of 27 July 2026 (section 6.1, non-binding) explains how
core functionality is determined - see the resource index.
