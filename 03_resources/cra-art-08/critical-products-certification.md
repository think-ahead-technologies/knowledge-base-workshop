# Mandatory European cybersecurity certification for critical products

Art. 8(1) CRA empowers the Commission to adopt delegated acts (under Art. 61)
specifying which products with digital elements that have the core functions of
an Annex IV category must obtain a "europäisches Cybersicherheitszertifikat
mindestens der Vertrauenswürdigkeitsstufe 'mittel'" under a European
cybersecurity certification scheme adopted per Regulation (EU) 2019/881. The
certificate demonstrates conformity with the essential cybersecurity
requirements of Annex I, or parts of them.

Conditions and safeguards in the text:
- Only possible once a European certification scheme for that product category
  has been adopted under Regulation (EU) 2019/881 and is available to
  manufacturers.
- The delegated act sets the required assurance level, proportionate to the
  cybersecurity risk and the intended purpose, including critical dependency of
  essential entities ("wesentliche Einrichtungen") per Art. 3(1) Directive (EU)
  2022/2555 (NIS2).
- Before adoption the Commission assesses market impact and consults
  stakeholders, including the European Cybersecurity Certification Group; the
  assessment considers Member States' readiness and capacity to implement the
  scheme.
- Transition period of at least six months, unless a shorter one is justified
  by extreme urgency ("Gründe äußerster Dringlichkeit").

Fallback: where no such delegated act has been adopted, products with core
functions of an Annex IV category are subject to the conformity assessment
procedures of Art. 32(3).
