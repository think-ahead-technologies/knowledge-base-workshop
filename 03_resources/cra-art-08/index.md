# CRA Art. 8 - Critical products with digital elements (Kritische Produkte mit digitalen Elementen)

## Summary
Article 8 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") governs "kritische Produkte mit digitalen Elementen" -
products whose core function falls into a category listed in Annex IV.
Paragraph 1 empowers the Commission to require, by delegated act, a European
cybersecurity certificate of at least assurance level "mittel" (substantial)
under a scheme adopted per Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Cybersecurity Act); absent
such a delegated act, these products go through the conformity assessment
procedures of Art. 32(3). Paragraph 2 empowers the Commission to add or remove
Annex IV categories, subject to criteria (critical dependency of NIS2 essential
entities, or risk of serious supply-chain disruption). The captured page is the
German text republished by Kunnus, plus a non-binding summary of the Commission
guidance of 27 July 2026 (C(2026) 5252 final) on core functionality, and two
related recitals; only the Official Journal text is binding.

- **Core functionality (Kernfunktionalität), Commission guidance section 6.1 (non-binding)** - classification as important (Annex III) or critical (Annex IV) depends only on the product's single core functionality, not on add-on features or integrated components (smartphone with OS is not an OS, example 58; SOAR is not SIEM, example 59; log collectors without correlation are not SIEM, example 60; separately marketed modules are classified individually, example 61); marketing, user instructions and technical documentation must not contradict each other to escape the stricter procedure; category technical descriptions are in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392.
- **Related recitals** - page lists recitals 44 (stricter conformity assessment for certain categories, proportionality) and 45 (important products understood as those with the core function of a listed category), excerpts only.
- **Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Cybersecurity Act)** - framework for European cybersecurity certification schemes -> <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/881/oj>
- **Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2)** - defines "wesentliche Einrichtungen" (essential entities, Art. 3) -> <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj>
