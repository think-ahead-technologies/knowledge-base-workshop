# CRA stakeholder consultation

Art. 9 CRA ("Konsultation der Interessenträger") sets how the Commission
involves stakeholders when it prepares implementing measures
("Maßnahmen für die Durchführung dieser Verordnung").

**Who is consulted (Abs. 1)** - "einschlägige Interessenträger", listed as:
relevant authorities of the Member States, private-sector companies including
micro, small and medium enterprises ("Kleinstunternehmen und kleiner und
mittlerer Unternehmen"), the "Open-Source-Software-Gemeinschaft", consumer
associations ("Verbraucherverbände"), universities ("Hochschulen"), relevant
Union agencies and bodies, and expert groups set up at Union level. The
Commission must take their views into account ("berücksichtigt deren
Ansichten").

**Structured consultation cases (Abs. 1, points a-c)** - "gegebenenfalls in
folgender strukturierter Weise":

- **(a)** when preparing the guidance ("Leitlinien") referred to in Art. 26;
- **(b)** without prejudice to Art. 61: when drafting the technical
  descriptions of the product categories in Annex III under Art. 7(4), when
  assessing whether the list of product categories under Art. 7(3) and
  Art. 8(2) needs updating, or when assessing the potential market impact
  under Art. 8(1);
- **(c)** when carrying out preparatory work for the evaluation and review of
  the regulation ("Bewertung und Überprüfung dieser Verordnung").

**Regular sessions (Abs. 2)** - the Commission organises consultation and
information sessions ("Konsultations- und Informationssitzungen") regularly,
at least once a year, to gather the stakeholders' views on implementation.

Practical reading: manufacturers, SMEs and open-source communities have a
formal channel to influence CRA guidance and the scope of important/critical
product lists.
