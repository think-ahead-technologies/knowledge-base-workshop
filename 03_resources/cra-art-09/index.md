# CRA Art. 9 - Stakeholder consultation (Konsultation der Interessenträger)

## Summary
Article 9 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") obliges the Commission to consult relevant
stakeholders ("einschlägige Interessenträger") when preparing measures to
implement the regulation and to take their views into account. Structured
consultation is named for three cases: the guidance under Art. 26, the
technical descriptions and list updates for important/critical product
categories (Art. 7, Art. 8, Annex III) including the market-impact assessment,
and preparatory work for evaluating and reviewing the regulation. Paragraph 2
requires consultation and information sessions at least once a year. The
captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus, with one related
recital (46); only the Official Journal text is binding.
