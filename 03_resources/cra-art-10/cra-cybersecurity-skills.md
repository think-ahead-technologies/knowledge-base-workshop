# CRA cybersecurity skills development by Member States

Art. 10 CRA ("Ausbau der Kompetenzen in einem digitalen Umfeld mit
Cyberabwehrfähigkeit") puts no obligation on manufacturers or other economic
operators. It addresses the Member States ("die Mitgliedstaaten"): they must
promote measures and strategies ("fördern ... Maßnahmen und Strategien") so that
enough skilled professionals exist to support the application of the regulation.
They may be supported, where appropriate, by the Commission, the European
Cybersecurity Competence Centre ("Europäisches Kompetenzzentrum für
Cybersicherheit") and ENISA, with full respect for the Member States'
responsibility in education ("unter uneingeschränkter Achtung der Verantwortung
der Mitgliedstaaten im Bildungsbereich").

The measures aim at:

- **(a) Staffing the enforcement side** - developing cybersecurity skills and
  creating organisational and technological tools so that sufficient qualified
  professionals ("qualifizierter Fachkräfte") are available to support the
  activities of market surveillance authorities ("Marktüberwachungsbehörden")
  and conformity assessment bodies ("Konformitätsbewertungsstellen").
- **(b) Public-private cooperation and training** - strengthening cooperation
  between the private sector and economic operators ("Wirtschaftsakteure"),
  including reskilling or upskilling ("Umschulung oder Weiterbildung") of
  manufacturers' employees, consumers, training providers and public
  administrations, to open more access to cybersecurity jobs for young people.

Practical relevance for a manufacturer: no direct duty, but national
programmes under this article may offer training or funding for staff.

## Mentions
- No other CRA article or annex is referenced in the legal text of Art. 10.
- Actors named: Commission, European Cybersecurity Competence Centre, ENISA,
  market surveillance authorities, conformity assessment bodies.
