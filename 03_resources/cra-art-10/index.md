# CRA Art. 10 - Enhancing skills in a cyber-resilient digital environment

## Summary
Article 10 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") obliges the Member States to promote measures and
strategies that build cybersecurity skills ("Cybersicherheitskompetenzen"), with
optional support from the Commission, the European Cybersecurity Competence
Centre and ENISA, while respecting national competence in education. Goals:
(a) enough qualified professionals for market surveillance authorities and
conformity assessment bodies, and (b) stronger public-private cooperation,
including reskilling and upskilling of manufacturers' employees, consumers,
training providers and public administrations, opening cybersecurity jobs to
young people. It creates no direct obligation for manufacturers. The captured
page is the German text as republished by Kunnus; only the Official Journal text
is binding.
