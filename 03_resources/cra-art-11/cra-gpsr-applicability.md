# CRA and the General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) - residual applicability

Art. 11 CRA ("Allgemeine Produktsicherheit") is a single-paragraph conflict rule
between the CRA and the General Product Safety Regulation, Regulation (EU)
2023/988. It derogates ("Abweichend von") from Art. 2(1) subparagraph 3 point (b)
of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 - the GPSR clause that would otherwise limit the
GPSR where other Union law covers a product.

Effect: for "Produkte mit digitalen Elementen", the following parts of the GPSR
still apply - "Kapitel III Abschnitt 1, Kapitel V und VII sowie die Kapitel IX
bis XI" - but only:

- for aspects and risks or risk categories not covered by the CRA ("Aspekte und
  Risiken oder Risikokategorien, die nicht unter die vorliegende Verordnung
  fallen"), and
- provided the products are not subject to specific safety requirements laid
  down in other "Harmonisierungsrechtsvorschriften der Union" within the meaning
  of Art. 3 point 27 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988.

Practical reading: the CRA covers cybersecurity risks; non-cyber safety risks of
a product with digital elements remain under the GPSR's general safety regime
unless sector-specific Union harmonisation law already sets safety requirements.
