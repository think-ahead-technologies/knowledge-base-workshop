# CRA Art. 11 - General product safety (Allgemeine Produktsicherheit)

## Summary
Article 11 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") governs the interplay with the General Product Safety
Regulation (EU) 2023/988. By derogation from Art. 2(1) subpara. 3 point (b) GPSR,
GPSR Chapter III Section 1, Chapters V and VII and Chapters IX to XI apply to
"Produkte mit digitalen Elementen" for aspects and risks not covered by the CRA,
unless the products are subject to specific safety requirements in other
"Harmonisierungsrechtsvorschriften der Union" (Art. 3 point 27 GPSR). The
captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus, with no FAQ and one
related recital (48, truncated); only the Official Journal text is binding.
