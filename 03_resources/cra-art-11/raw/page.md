**Substance of the provision (paraphrased)**
The article's single paragraph modifies how Article 2(1), subparagraph 3, point (b) of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (General Product Safety Regulation) applies to products with digital elements. Specific chapters of that regulation apply to risks not covered by the CRA, but only where no sector-specific EU harmonisation rules already set safety requirements for those products. One excerpt: "Kapitel III Abschnitt 1, Kapitel V und VII sowie die Kapitel IX bis XI".
