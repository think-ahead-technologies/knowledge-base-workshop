# CRA-AI Act presumption of conformity for high-risk AI systems

Art. 12(1) CRA links the two regulations: a product with digital elements in CRA
scope that is classified as "Hochrisiko-KI-System" under Art. 6 of the AI Act
(Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) "gilt ... als mit den Cybersicherheitsanforderungen
gemäß Artikel 15 der genannten Verordnung konform", if all three hold:

- **(a)** the product meets the essential cybersecurity requirements of CRA
  Annex I Part I (product properties);
- **(b)** the manufacturer's processes meet CRA Annex I Part II
  (vulnerability handling);
- **(c)** achieving the cybersecurity level required by Art. 15 AI Act is
  demonstrated in the EU declaration of conformity ("EU-Konformitätserklärung")
  issued under the CRA.

This is "unbeschadet" (without prejudice to) the AI Act requirements on accuracy
and robustness ("Genauigkeit und Robustheit") in Art. 15 AI Act - those still
need their own evidence. Takeaway: one CRA compliance effort plus a statement in
the CRA declaration of conformity covers the AI Act cybersecurity requirement.
