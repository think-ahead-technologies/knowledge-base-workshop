# Conformity assessment of CRA products that are high-risk AI systems

Art. 12(2)-(3) CRA decide which conformity assessment procedure applies to a
product that is both a CRA product and a high-risk AI system.

- **Default (para 2):** the relevant procedure of Art. 43 AI Act applies
  ("das einschlägige in Artikel 43 der Verordnung (EU) 2024/1689 vorgesehene
  Konformitätsbewertungsverfahren"). The notified bodies ("notifizierte Stellen")
  competent under the AI Act also check conformity with CRA Annex I - provided
  the AI Act notification procedure verified that they meet the requirements of
  CRA Art. 39.
- **Exception (para 3, "Abweichend von Absatz 2"):** the CRA's own conformity
  assessment procedures apply, as far as the CRA essential cybersecurity
  requirements are concerned, to
  - important products ("wichtige Produkte mit digitalen Elementen") listed in
    CRA Annex III that are subject to the procedures of Art. 32(2)(a)-(b) and
    Art. 32(3), and
  - critical products ("kritische Produkte mit digitalen Elementen") listed in
    CRA Annex IV that need a European cybersecurity certificate under Art. 8(1)
    or, lacking one, are subject to Art. 32(3),

  when they are also high-risk AI systems under Art. 6 AI Act and the AI Act
  would only require internal control ("interne Kontrolle") per Annex VI AI Act.

Takeaway: AI Act internal control must not let important or critical CRA
products escape the stricter CRA assessment for their cybersecurity part.
