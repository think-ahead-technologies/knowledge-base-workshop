# CRA Art. 12 - High-risk AI systems (Hochrisiko-KI-Systeme)

## Summary
Article 12 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter I
"Allgemeine Bestimmungen") is the interface between the CRA and the AI Act
(Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). Products with digital elements that are also
"Hochrisiko-KI-Systeme" under Art. 6 AI Act are presumed to meet the
cybersecurity requirements of Art. 15 AI Act if they fulfil CRA Annex I Part I
and Part II and the EU declaration of conformity shows the required
cybersecurity level (para 1). Conformity assessment normally runs through the
AI Act procedure (Art. 43 AI Act), with AI Act notified bodies also checking CRA
Annex I if they meet CRA Art. 39 (para 2); important and critical products
(Annex III / Annex IV) that would only get internal control under AI Act Annex VI
fall back to the CRA procedures instead (para 3). Manufacturers may join AI
regulatory sandboxes ("KI-Reallabore", Art. 57 AI Act) (para 4). The captured
page is the German text as republished by Kunnus; only the Official Journal text
is binding.
