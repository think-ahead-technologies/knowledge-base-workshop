# Technical documentation, conformity assessment and CE marking

Art. 13(12): before placing a product on the market, manufacturers draw up the technical documentation ("technische Dokumentation", Art. 31) and carry out, or have carried out, the chosen conformity assessment procedure ("Konformitätsbewertungsverfahren", Art. 32). Where conformity with Annex I Part I (product) and Annex I Part II (manufacturer's processes) has been demonstrated, they draw up the EU declaration of conformity ("EU-Konformitätserklärung", Art. 28) and affix the CE marking ("CE-Kennzeichnung", Art. 30).

Art. 13(13): technical documentation and the EU declaration of conformity are kept at the disposal of market surveillance authorities for at least ten years after placing on the market or for the support period, whichever is longer.

Art. 13(14): procedures ensure series production remains in conformity, taking into account changes in development/production process, design or characteristics, and changes to harmonised standards, European cybersecurity certification schemes or common specifications (Art. 27) relied upon.

Art. 13(20): the product is accompanied by a copy of the EU declaration of conformity or a simplified one; a simplified declaration must give the exact internet address of the full declaration.

Guidance (non-binding): conformity assessment, declaration and CE marking remain mandatory even for products designed before CRA applicability (2.7); product families may share one declaration if it identifies the covered variants unambiguously (7.4). Page FAQ: Art. 13 applies to each individual unit placed on the market from 11 December 2027 - stock produced earlier but sold after that date is covered.
