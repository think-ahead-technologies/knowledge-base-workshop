# Corrective action, cooperation with authorities and cessation of operations

Art. 13(21): from placing on the market and during the support period, manufacturers who know or have reason to believe that the product or their processes do not conform with Annex I immediately take the necessary corrective measures ("Korrekturmaßnahmen") to bring them into conformity, or to withdraw or recall the product, as appropriate.

Art. 13(22): upon a reasoned request of a market surveillance authority ("Marktüberwachungsbehörde"), manufacturers provide, on paper or electronically and in an easily understood language, all information and documentation needed to demonstrate conformity of the product and processes with Annex I, and cooperate on measures to eliminate cybersecurity risks of the product.

Art. 13(23): a manufacturer that ceases operations and as a result cannot comply with the regulation informs the relevant market surveillance authorities and, by all available means and where possible, the users of the products concerned before the cessation takes effect.

Page FAQ (vendor, non-binding): violations of Art. 13 can lead to fines up to EUR 15 million or 2.5% of worldwide annual turnover (whichever is higher), recall and a ban on placing on the market; penalties are regulated in Art. 64.
