# CRA Art. 13 - Obligations of manufacturers (Pflichten der Hersteller)

## Summary
Article 13 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") is the core catalogue of manufacturer duties in 25
paragraphs: design products per Annex I Part I; perform, document and maintain a
cybersecurity risk assessment; exercise due diligence on third-party and
open-source components and report their vulnerabilities upstream; set a support
period of at least five years and handle vulnerabilities per Annex I Part II;
keep security updates available; draw up technical documentation, run
conformity assessment, issue the EU declaration of conformity and affix CE
marking; identify the product, give contact details and a single point of
contact; supply user information; take corrective action and cooperate with
market surveillance; and SBOM-related implementing powers. The captured page is
the German text as republished by Kunnus, plus summaries of the non-binding
Commission guidance C(2026) 5252 final (27 July 2026), a vendor FAQ and a list
of related recitals 50-64. Only the Official Journal text is binding.
