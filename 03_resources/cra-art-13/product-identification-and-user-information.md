# Product identification, contact details, single point of contact and user information

Art. 13(15): products bear a type, batch or serial number or other identification element, or, if not possible, this is given on the packaging or accompanying documents.

Art. 13(16): the manufacturer's name, registered trade name or trade mark, postal address, email address or other digital contact and, where available, website are indicated on the product or, if not possible, on packaging or accompanying documents. They are also included in the user information and instructions per Annex II, in a language easily understood by users and market surveillance authorities.

Art. 13(17): manufacturers designate a single point of contact ("zentrale Anlaufstelle") enabling users to communicate directly and rapidly, including to facilitate reporting of vulnerabilities. It must be easily identifiable, included in the Annex II user information, and let users choose their preferred means of communication, not limited to automated tools.

Art. 13(18): products are accompanied by the Annex II information and instructions for the user ("Informationen und Anleitungen für den Nutzer"), on paper or electronically, in an easily understood language; clear, understandable, intelligible and legible; enabling secure installation, operation and use. They stay available for at least ten years after placing on the market or for the support period, whichever is longer; online versions must be accessible, user-friendly and available for the same period.

Guidance (non-binding, 2.6): interoperability constraints, risks and mitigations belong transparently in the user information (Annex II); 2.7: Art. 13(18) applies fully to pre-existing designs.
