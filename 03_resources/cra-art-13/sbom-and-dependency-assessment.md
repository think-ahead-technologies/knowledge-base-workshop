# Software bill of materials and Union-wide dependency assessment (Software-Stückliste)

Art. 13(24): the Commission may, by implementing acts and taking into account European or international standards and best practice, specify the format and elements of the software bill of materials ("Software-Stückliste") required by Annex I Part II point 1. These are adopted under the examination procedure of Art. 62(2).

Art. 13(25): to assess the dependency of Member States and the Union on software components, in particular free and open-source software, ADCO may decide to carry out a Union-wide dependency assessment for certain product categories. Market surveillance authorities may then request manufacturers of such categories to submit their SBOMs, and may pass anonymised and aggregated dependency information to ADCO. ADCO reports the results to the Cooperation Group established under Article 14 of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2).

Page FAQ (vendor, non-binding): the SBOM need not be published; it is part of the technical documentation and is provided to market surveillance authorities on request. Publication to customers is optional but may be demanded in B2B procurement.
