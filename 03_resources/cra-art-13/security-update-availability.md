# Security update availability and older versions (Sicherheitsaktualisierungen)

Art. 13(9): every security update ("Sicherheitsaktualisierung") per Annex I Part II point 8 made available during the support period stays available for at least ten years after it is issued, or for the remainder of the support period, whichever is longer.

Art. 13(10): where a manufacturer has placed subsequent substantially modified versions of a software product on the market, it may limit compliance with the essential requirement of Annex I Part II point 2 to the latest version, provided users of earlier versions get free access to the latest version and incur no additional cost to adjust their hardware and software environment.

Art. 13(11): manufacturers may maintain public software archives for historical versions; users are then informed clearly and accessibly about the risks of using unsupported software.

Guidance (non-binding): "Zusatzkosten" in Abs. 10 means buying new hardware or changing infrastructure - not normal update effort such as staff time, testing or configuration. Manufacturers may voluntarily keep patching older versions, also for a fee.
