# Support period (Unterstützungszeitraum)

Art. 13(8): when placing a product on the market and during the expected product lifetime and the support period ("Unterstützungszeitraum"), manufacturers ensure vulnerabilities, including in components, are handled effectively per Annex I Part II.

The support period must reflect the expected time of use, considering reasonable user expectations, the nature and intended purpose of the product, and relevant Union law on product lifetime. Manufacturers may also consider support periods of similar products, availability of the operating environment, support periods of integrated third-party core components, and ADCO (Art. 52(15)) and Commission guidance - applied proportionately. The support period is at least five years; if the product is expected to be in use for less than five years, it matches the expected use time. The Commission may adopt delegated acts (Art. 61), considering ADCO recommendations (Art. 52(16)), fixing minimum support periods for product categories where market surveillance data shows inadequate periods. The information used to determine the period goes into the technical documentation (Annex VII). Manufacturers need policies and procedures, including a coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy per Annex I Part II point 5.

Art. 13(19): the end date of the support period is stated clearly at the time of purchase, at least month and year, where applicable on the product, its packaging or by digital means. Where technically feasible, users are notified when the support period has ended.

Guidance (non-binding, section 5-5.1): five years is a floor, not a default; each substantially modified version needs its own declared support period; a substantial modification does not reset the period automatically - if unchanged factors (e.g. hardware lifetime) still drive the use time, the original period stays; if the modification changes those factors, recalculate. Page FAQ: industrial machines with 15-year lifecycles need correspondingly longer support.
