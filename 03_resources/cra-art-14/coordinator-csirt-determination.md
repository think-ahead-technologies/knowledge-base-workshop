# CRA reporting channel - which coordinator CSIRT receives the report

Art. 14(7) fixes where Art. 14(1) and (3) notifications go: through the single
reporting platform of Art. 16, using one of the electronic notification
endpoints of Art. 16(1). The report goes via the endpoint of the CSIRT
designated as coordinator in the Member State of the manufacturer's main
establishment in the Union ("Hauptniederlassung in der Union") and is
simultaneously accessible to ENISA.

**Main establishment** is the Member State where cybersecurity decisions about
the manufacturer's products are predominantly taken; if that cannot be
determined, the Member State with the establishment having the highest number of
employees in the Union.

**No main establishment in the Union** - fallback order, based on information
available to the manufacturer:

1. (a) Member State of the authorised representative ("Bevollmächtigter")
   acting for the largest number of the manufacturer's products;
2. (b) Member State of the importer ("Einführer") placing the largest number of
   its products on the market;
3. (c) Member State of the distributor ("Händler") making the largest number of
   its products available;
4. (d) Member State with the largest number of users.

For case (d), later reports may be sent to the same coordinator CSIRT first
notified.

Art. 14(9)-(10): the Commission adopts a delegated act (Art. 61), due by
11 December 2025, on conditions for delaying dissemination of notifications on
cybersecurity grounds (Art. 16(2)), cooperating with the CSIRTs network
(Art. 15 Directive (EU) 2022/2555) and ENISA; and may specify format and
procedures of notifications under Arts. 14, 15 and 16 by implementing acts
(examination procedure, Art. 62(2)).
