# CRA Art. 14 - Reporting obligations of manufacturers (Meldepflichten der Hersteller)

## Summary
Article 14 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") is the central reporting duty: manufacturers report every
actively exploited vulnerability ("aktiv ausgenutzte Schwachstelle") and every
severe incident ("schwerwiegender Sicherheitsvorfall") affecting their product's
security simultaneously to the coordinator CSIRT and ENISA via the Art. 16 single
reporting platform - 24h early warning, 72h notification, final report after
14 days (from fix availability) resp. one month. It also defines the severity
test, the CSIRT-determination rule, interim reports, user information, and
Commission delegated / implementing acts. The page is the German text as
republished by Kunnus, plus non-binding Commission guidance excerpts
(C(2026) 5252 final, 27 July 2026), a vendor FAQ and recitals 65-74; only the
Official Journal text is binding. Per the page, Art. 14 applies since
11 September 2026.
