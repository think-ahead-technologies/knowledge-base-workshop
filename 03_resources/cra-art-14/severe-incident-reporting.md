# CRA severe security incident reporting and severity criteria

Art. 14(3) obliges a manufacturer to report every "schwerwiegenden
Sicherheitsvorfall, der sich auf die Sicherheit des Produkts mit digitalen
Elementen auswirkt" it becomes aware of, simultaneously to the coordinator CSIRT
and ENISA via the Art. 16 single reporting platform.

Art. 14(4) staged submissions:

- **(a) Early warning ("Frühwarnung")** - without undue delay, at the latest
  within 24 hours of awareness; states at least whether the incident is suspected
  to be caused by unlawful or malicious acts ("rechtswidrige oder böswillige
  Handlungen"), and where applicable the Member States where the product was
  made available.
- **(b) Incident notification ("Meldung des Sicherheitsvorfalls")** - within
  72 hours: nature of the incident, an initial assessment, corrective or
  mitigating measures taken and measures users can take, optionally sensitivity
  of the information.
- **(c) Final report ("Abschlussbericht")** - within one month after the (b)
  notification: (i) detailed description incl. severity and impact, (ii) type of
  threat or root cause likely to have triggered it, (iii) applied and ongoing
  mitigation measures.

**Severity test, Art. 14(5).** An incident affecting the product's security is
"schwerwiegend" if it

- (a) negatively affects, or is capable of affecting, the product's ability to
  protect the availability, authenticity, integrity or confidentiality of
  sensitive or important data or functions, or
- (b) has led or is capable of leading to the introduction or execution of
  malicious code ("böswilliger Code") in the product or in the network and
  information systems of a user.

Art. 14(6): the coordinator CSIRT may request an interim report.

Guidance on the page (non-binding): infrastructure open-source stewards (repos,
version control, signing keys) must report severe incidents under Art. 14(3).
