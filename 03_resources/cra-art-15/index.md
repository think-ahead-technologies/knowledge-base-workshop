# CRA Art. 15 - Voluntary reporting (Freiwillige Meldungen)

## Summary
Article 15 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") lets manufacturers and any other natural or legal person
voluntarily report vulnerabilities, cyber threats, security incidents and near
misses ("Beinahe-Vorfälle") to a coordinating CSIRT or ENISA. Reports follow the
Art. 16 procedure, mandatory reports may take priority, the manufacturer is
informed of third-party reports of actively exploited vulnerabilities or severe
incidents, and reporters get confidentiality and no additional obligations from
reporting. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus, with a
vendor quick answer and FAQ, excerpts of the Commission guidance of 27 July 2026
(C(2026) 5252 final, sections 3.3, 9.1, 9.2) and related recital 75; only the
Official Journal text is binding.

- **Commission guidance excerpts (non-binding)** - 3.3 open-source stewards (non-technical support: no mandatory reporting, consider voluntary Art. 15 report); 9.1 Art. 14 reporting since 11 September 2026, "Bewusstwerden", third-party components only voluntary unless exploitable in own product; 9.2 upstream reporting (Art. 13(6)), known vulnerabilities, regular testing.
- **Vendor FAQ (Kunnus, non-binding)** - anyone may report (researchers, bug-bounty participants, importers, end customers); claims good-faith reporters are protected from liability (not stated in the article text); Art. 15 complements, not replaces, bug-bounty programmes.
- **Related recitals** - recital 75 (Member States should address challenges faced by vulnerability researchers).
