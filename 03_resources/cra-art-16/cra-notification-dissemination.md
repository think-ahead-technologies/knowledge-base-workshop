# CRA notification dissemination and delay by coordinator CSIRTs

How a CRA notification travels once a manufacturer has submitted it
(Art. 16(2), (3), (6)):

- **Forwarding.** The CSIRT designated as coordinator ("als Koordinator
  benanntes CSIRT") that first receives the notification forwards it without
  undue delay via the single reporting platform to the coordinator CSIRTs of
  every Member State where, per the manufacturer, the product with digital
  elements was made available.
- **Delay on cybersecurity grounds.** In exceptional circumstances - in
  particular on the manufacturer's request and given the sensitivity the
  manufacturer indicated under Art. 14(2)(a) - dissemination may be delayed for
  justified cybersecurity reasons for as long as strictly necessary, also when
  the vulnerability is under coordinated vulnerability disclosure per Art. 12(1)
  of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS 2). A CSIRT that withholds a notification
  informs ENISA without undue delay with a justification and an indication of
  when it will disseminate. ENISA may support the CSIRT.
- **Exceptional-exceptional case (Art. 16(2) points a-c).** If the manufacturer
  states in the early warning under Art. 14(2)(b) that (a) the vulnerability is
  actively exploited by a malicious actor only in the receiving CSIRT's Member
  State, (b) immediate further dissemination would likely reveal information
  contrary to that Member State's essential interests, or (c) further
  dissemination itself poses an imminent high cybersecurity risk, then only
  minimal information goes to ENISA simultaneously: that a notification was made,
  general product information, the general nature of the exploit, and that
  security reasons were raised. If ENISA sees a systemic risk to the internal
  market ("Systemrisiko für die Sicherheit des Binnenmarkts"), it recommends that
  the receiving CSIRT forward the full notification to the other coordinator
  CSIRTs and ENISA.
- **Market surveillance.** For actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe
  incidents ("schwerwiegender Sicherheitsvorfall"), coordinator CSIRTs give their
  national market surveillance authorities ("Marktüberwachungsbehörden") the
  information they need for their CRA obligations (Art. 16(3)).
- **CVD deferral.** If a coordinator CSIRT learns of an actively exploited
  vulnerability in a coordinated vulnerability disclosure process (NIS 2
  Art. 12(1)), it may defer dissemination via the platform until the CVD parties
  consent to disclosure, no longer than strictly necessary. Manufacturers may
  still report such a vulnerability voluntarily (Art. 16(6)).
