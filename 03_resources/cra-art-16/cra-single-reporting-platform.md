# CRA single reporting platform (einheitliche Meldeplattform)

Art. 16(1) obliges ENISA to set up a single reporting platform ("einheitliche
Meldeplattform") for the notifications under Art. 14(1) and (3) (mandatory
reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents) and
Art. 15(1) and (2) (voluntary reporting), to simplify manufacturers' reporting.
ENISA manages and maintains its day-to-day operation. Its architecture must let
Member States and ENISA set up their own electronic notification endpoints
("Endpunkte für die elektronische Meldung").

Security of the platform (Art. 16(4)-(5)):
- ENISA takes appropriate and proportionate technical, operational and
  organisational measures to manage risks to the platform and to the information
  submitted or disseminated through it.
- ENISA notifies the CSIRTs network ("CSIRTs-Netzwerk") and the Commission
  without undue delay of any security incident affecting the platform.
- ENISA, in cooperation with the CSIRTs network, provides and implements
  specifications for setting up, maintaining and securely operating the platform
  and the national/Union endpoints, including procedural aspects so that
  information on vulnerabilities without available fix or mitigation is shared
  under strict security protocols and on a need-to-know basis ("Grundsatz
  'Kenntnis nur, wenn nötig'").
