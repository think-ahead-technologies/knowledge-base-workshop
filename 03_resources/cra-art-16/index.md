# CRA Art. 16 - Establishment of a single reporting platform (Einrichtung einer einheitlichen Meldeplattform)

## Summary
Article 16 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") makes ENISA set up and run a single reporting platform
("einheitliche Meldeplattform") through which manufacturers' mandatory (Art. 14)
and voluntary (Art. 15) notifications flow. It sets how the receiving coordinator
CSIRT forwards notifications to other Member States' CSIRTs, when and how
dissemination may be delayed on cybersecurity grounds, what goes to market
surveillance authorities, and how ENISA secures the platform (need-to-know
sharing of unpatched vulnerabilities). The captured page is the German text as
republished by Kunnus, plus a non-binding summary of Commission guidance section
9.1 and two related recitals; only the Official Journal text is binding.

- **Commission guidance C(2026) 5252 final, section 9.1 (non-binding, as summarised by Kunnus)** - Art. 14 reporting obligations apply since 11 September 2026 to all in-scope products, including legacy and end-of-support products; "Bewusstwerden" means reasonable certainty after prompt initial assessment, starting the deadlines (24 h early warning, 72 h notification, 14 days / 1 month final report); no retroactive reporting for exploitation known before 11.09.2026; third-party components: only exploitable-in-own-product vulnerabilities are mandatory, otherwise voluntary (Art. 15) plus upstream report (Art. 13(6)); user information (Art. 14(8)) is risk-based; reporting continues after the support period.
- **Related recitals** - page lists recitals 76 (coordinated vulnerability disclosure policies) and 77 (component documentation, e.g. SBOM) as previews only.
