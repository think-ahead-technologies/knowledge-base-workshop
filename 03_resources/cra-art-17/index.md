# CRA Art. 17 - Other provisions related to reporting (Sonstige Bestimmungen im Zusammenhang mit der Berichterstattung)

## Summary
Article 17 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") governs what happens to the notifications made under
Art. 14(1) and (3) and Art. 15(1) and (2) after they are filed. ENISA may pass
relevant information to EU-CyCLONe (para. 1), must publish a technical trend
report every 24 months (para. 3) and enters publicly known, fixed
vulnerabilities into the European vulnerability database (para. 5). The
coordinator CSIRT may inform the public about an incident after consulting the
manufacturer (para. 2). For manufacturers, two points matter most: the mere act
of notifying does not create higher liability (para. 4), and coordinator CSIRTs
offer a helpdesk for the Art. 14 reporting obligations, especially for micro and
SMEs (para. 6). The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus;
only the Official Journal text is binding.
