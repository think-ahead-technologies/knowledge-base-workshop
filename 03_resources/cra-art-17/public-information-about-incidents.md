# Public information about security incidents

Art. 17(2) CRA lets the CSIRT designated as coordinator ("das als Koordinator des
betreffenden Mitgliedstaats benannte CSIRT") inform the public about a security
incident, or require the manufacturer to do so, when:

- public awareness is needed to prevent or mitigate a severe incident
  ("schwerwiegenden Sicherheitsvorfall") affecting the security of the product
  with digital elements, or to handle an ongoing incident, or
- disclosure of the incident is otherwise in the public interest
  ("im öffentlichen Interesse").

Procedure: the CSIRT must first consult the manufacturer ("nach Konsultation des
betreffenden Herstellers") and acts, where appropriate, in cooperation with
ENISA. The manufacturer is consulted but has no veto; the CSIRT may also order
the manufacturer to make the announcement itself.
