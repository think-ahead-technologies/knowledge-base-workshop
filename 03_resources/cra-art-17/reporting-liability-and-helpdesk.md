# Reporting does not increase liability; CSIRT helpdesk

Two manufacturer-facing safeguards around the CRA reporting duties:

- **No higher liability from notifying (para. 4)** - "Die bloße Meldung gemäß
  Artikel 14 Absätze 1 und 3 und Artikel 15 Absätze 1 und 2 begründet keine
  höhere Haftung der meldenden natürlichen oder juristischen Person." The act of
  filing a mandatory or voluntary report is not itself a basis for increased
  liability. Interpretation (not stated in the text): it does not exempt the reporter from liability for the underlying
  vulnerability or incident; it only removes the report as an aggravating factor.
- **Helpdesk support (para. 6)** - the CSIRTs designated as coordinators offer
  "Helpdesk-Unterstützung" to manufacturers on the Art. 14 reporting obligations,
  in particular to micro, small and medium-sized enterprises ("Kleinstunternehmen
  oder ... kleine oder mittlere Unternehmen").

Takeaway: fear of liability is not a reason to delay a report, and SMEs can turn
to their national coordinator CSIRT for help with how to report.
