# CRA authorised representative mandate

Art. 18 CRA ("Bevollmächtigte") regulates the authorised representative, an
economic operator acting on behalf of the manufacturer:

- **Appointment is optional and written** - "Ein Hersteller kann schriftlich
  einen Bevollmächtigten benennen." (para 1)
- **Non-delegable obligations** - the obligations in "Artikel 13 Absatz 1 bis
  Absatz 11, Artikel 13 Absatz 12 Unterabsatz 1 und Artikel 13 Absatz 14" are
  "nicht Teil des Auftrags des Bevollmächtigten" (para 2). The manufacturer keeps
  responsibility for these core duties (design/development obligations,
  vulnerability handling etc. under Art. 13).
- **Mandate ("Auftrag")** - the representative performs the tasks the mandate
  sets and must show a copy of it to market surveillance authorities
  ("Marktüberwachungsbehörden") on request (para 3). The mandate must allow at
  least:
  - (a) keeping the EU declaration of conformity ("EU-Konformitätserklärung",
    Art. 28) and the technical documentation ("technische Dokumentation",
    Art. 31) available for at least ten years from placing on the market
    ("Inverkehrbringen") or for the support period ("Unterstützungszeitraum"),
    whichever is longer;
  - (b) handing over all information and documents needed to prove conformity
    upon a reasoned request ("begründetes Verlangen");
  - (c) cooperating with the authorities, on request, on any measures to avert
    risks from a product within the representative's remit.

Takeaway: a representative is a documentation and authority-liaison role; it
does not take over the manufacturer's substantive security obligations.
