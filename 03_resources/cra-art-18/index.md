# CRA Art. 18 - Authorised representatives (Bevollmächtigte)

## Summary
Article 18 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") lets a manufacturer appoint an authorised representative
("Bevollmächtigter") by written mandate ("schriftlich"). Core manufacturer
obligations from Art. 13 (paras 1-11, para 12 first subparagraph, para 14)
cannot be delegated to the representative. The mandate must at minimum let the
representative keep the EU declaration of conformity and technical documentation
available for 10 years (or the support period, if longer), hand over conformity
evidence on a reasoned request, and cooperate with market surveillance
authorities on risk measures. The captured page is the German text as
republished by Kunnus, with one related recital (79); only the Official Journal
text is binding.
