**Article 18: Bevollmächtigte (Authorised representatives)**
*Chapter II, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847. Published 10 December 2024. Last reviewed by Kunnus in March 2026.*

**Paragraph 1:** A manufacturer may appoint an authorised representative in writing. The text reads: "Ein Hersteller kann schriftlich einen Bevollmächtigten benennen."

**Paragraph 2:** The obligations in Article 13(1) to (11), Article 13(12) first subparagraph, and Article 13(14) are not part of the representative's mandate.

**Paragraph 3:** The representative performs the tasks set out in the manufacturer's mandate and must provide a copy of that mandate to market surveillance authorities on request. The mandate must allow the representative to perform at least these tasks:

- **(a)** Keep the EU declaration of conformity (Article 28) and the technical documentation (Article 31) available to market surveillance authorities for at least 10 years from placing the product on the market, or for the support period if longer.
- **(b)** Provide all information and documents needed to demonstrate product conformity to a market surveillance authority on a reasoned request.
- **(c)** Cooperate with market surveillance authorities, on their request, on measures to address risks from products within the representative's mandate.
