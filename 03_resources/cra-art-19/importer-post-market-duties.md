# Importer post-market duties (CRA Art. 19(4)-(8))

- **Para 4 - contact details** - the importer states its name, registered trade name
  or trade mark, postal address, e-mail or other digital contact and, where
  applicable, website, on the product, its packaging or accompanying documents, in a
  language easily understood by users and market surveillance authorities.
- **Para 5 - corrective action and notification** - an importer that knows or has
  reason to believe a product it placed is non-compliant takes the necessary
  corrective measures ("Korrekturmaßnahmen") without undue delay: bring it into
  conformity, withdraw ("vom Markt nehmen") or recall ("zurückrufen"). On becoming
  aware of a vulnerability ("Schwachstelle") it informs the manufacturer without
  undue delay. If the product presents a significant cybersecurity risk it also
  informs, without undue delay, the market surveillance authorities of the Member
  States where it made the product available, with details on the non-compliance
  and corrective measures taken.
- **Para 6 - record keeping** - keeps a copy of the EU declaration of conformity at
  the disposal of market surveillance authorities for at least ten years after
  placing, or for the support period ("Unterstützungszeitraum") if longer, and
  ensures the technical documentation can be provided on request.
- **Para 7 - cooperation** - on reasoned request, provides the authority (paper or
  electronic, in an easily understood language) all information and documents to
  demonstrate conformity with Annex I Part I and of the manufacturer's processes
  with Annex I Part II, and cooperates on measures to eliminate cybersecurity risks.
- **Para 8 - manufacturer exit** - if the importer learns the manufacturer has
  ceased operations ("Betriebstätigkeit eingestellt") and so cannot meet its
  obligations, it informs the relevant market surveillance authorities and, by any
  means available and where possible, the users of the products placed.
