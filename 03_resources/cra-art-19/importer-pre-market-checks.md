# Importer pre-market checks (CRA Art. 19(1)-(3))

- **Para 1** - an importer ("Einführer") may only place on the market products
  with digital elements that meet the essential cybersecurity requirements of
  Annex I Part I, and whose manufacturer-defined processes ("vom Hersteller
  festgelegten Verfahren") meet Annex I Part II.
- **Para 2** - before placing, the importer ensures that (a) the manufacturer ran
  the appropriate conformity assessment procedures ("Konformitätsbewertungsverfahren")
  under Art. 32; (b) the manufacturer drew up the technical documentation; (c) the
  product bears the CE marking (Art. 30) and is accompanied by the EU declaration of
  conformity (Art. 13(20)) and the user information and instructions of Annex II, in
  a language easily understood by users and market surveillance authorities; (d) the
  manufacturer met Art. 13(15), (16) and (19). The importer must be able to produce
  the documents proving this.
- **Para 3** - if the importer considers or has reason to believe the product or the
  manufacturer's processes do not comply, it must not place the product until
  conformity is established. If the product presents a significant cybersecurity
  risk ("erhebliches Cybersicherheitsrisiko"), it also informs the manufacturer and
  the market surveillance authorities. If it has reason to believe the product could
  present a significant risk in view of non-technical risk factors
  ("nichttechnischer Risikofaktoren"), it informs the market surveillance
  authorities, which then follow Art. 54(2).
