# CRA Art. 19 - Obligations of importers (Pflichten der Einführer)

## Summary
Article 19 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") sets the obligations of importers ("Einführer") of
"Produkte mit digitalen Elementen". Before placing a product on the market
("Inverkehrbringen") the importer must verify the manufacturer's conformity work
(conformity assessment, technical documentation, CE marking, EU declaration of
conformity, user information, Art. 13 duties) and must not place products it
believes non-compliant. After placing, it must label its contact details, take
corrective action (bring into conformity, withdraw, recall), pass known
vulnerabilities to the manufacturer, notify market surveillance authorities of
significant cybersecurity risks, keep the declaration of conformity for 10 years
or the support period, cooperate with authorities, and notify authorities and
users if the manufacturer ceases operations. The captured page is the German text
as republished by Kunnus; only the Official Journal text is binding.
