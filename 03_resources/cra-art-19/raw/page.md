I can't reproduce the full article verbatim, but here is a concise summary of Article 19 (Pflichten der Einführer) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, the Cyber Resilience Act:

1. **Conformity before placing on the market:** Importers may only place products with digital elements on the market if they meet the essential cybersecurity requirements in Annex I, Part I, and the manufacturer's processes meet Annex I, Part II.
2. **Checks before importing:** Importers must confirm that the manufacturer has carried out the conformity assessment (Art. 32), prepared technical documentation, and affixed the CE marking. The product must also come with the EU declaration of conformity and user information in an easily understood language.
3. **Doubts about compliance:** If an importer suspects non-compliance, it must not place the product on the market until conformity is established. For significant cybersecurity risks, it must inform the manufacturer and market surveillance authorities. Non-technical risk factors also require notifying the authorities.
4. **Contact details:** Importers must state their name, trademark, postal address, electronic contact, and website, if applicable, on the product, its packaging, or accompanying documents.
5. **Corrective action:** Importers must act immediately to restore conformity, or withdraw or recall the product if needed. They must promptly report vulnerabilities to the manufacturer, and for significant risks, notify market surveillance authorities with specific details.
6. **Record keeping:** Importers must keep a copy of the EU declaration of conformity available for at least ten years from placing on the market, or for the support period if longer, and provide technical documentation on request.
7. **Cooperation:** On a reasoned request, importers must give authorities the information needed to demonstrate conformity and cooperate on measures to address cybersecurity risks.
8. **Manufacturer ceases operations:** If an importer learns that the manufacturer has stopped trading and cannot meet its obligations, it must inform the relevant authorities and, where possible, the users.
