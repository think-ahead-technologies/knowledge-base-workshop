# CRA distributor obligations (Pflichten der Händler)

Art. 20 sets the duties of distributors ("Händler") when they make a "Produkt
mit digitalen Elementen" available on the market ("auf dem Markt
bereitstellen"):

1. **Due care** - comply with the regulation "mit der gebührenden Sorgfalt".
2. **Pre-market verification** - before making the product available, check
   that it bears the CE marking ("CE-Kennzeichnung") and that manufacturer and
   importer met Art. 13(15), (16), (18), (19), (20) and Art. 19(4) and handed
   over all required documents.
3. **Hold back non-conforming products** - if the distributor believes (or has
   reason to believe) the product or the manufacturer's processes fail the
   essential cybersecurity requirements of Annex I, it must not make it
   available until conformity is established; with a significant
   cybersecurity risk ("erhebliches Cybersicherheitsrisiko") it also informs
   manufacturer and market surveillance authorities without undue delay
   ("unverzüglich").
4. **Products already on the market** - ensure corrective measures
   ("Korrekturmaßnahmen") are taken: restore conformity, or withdraw
   ("vom Markt nehmen") or recall ("zurückrufen"). Report any vulnerability
   ("Schwachstelle") it learns of to the manufacturer without undue delay; with
   a significant risk, inform the market surveillance authorities of the
   Member States where it sold the product, detailing the non-conformity and
   corrective measures taken.
5. **Cooperation with authorities** - on reasoned request, supply all
   information and documentation proving conformity (paper or electronic, in a
   language easily understood by the authority) and cooperate on measures to
   avert cybersecurity risks.
6. **Manufacturer ceased operations** - if it learns the manufacturer has
   ceased its business ("seine Betriebstätigkeit eingestellt") and can no
   longer meet its obligations, inform the relevant market surveillance
   authorities and, by all available means and where possible, the users.
