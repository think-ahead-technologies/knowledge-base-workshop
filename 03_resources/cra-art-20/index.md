# CRA Art. 20 - Obligations of distributors (Pflichten der Händler)

## Summary
Article 20 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") lists the obligations of distributors ("Händler"): act
with due care, verify CE marking and manufacturer/importer documentation before
making a product available, hold back or correct, withdraw or recall
non-conforming products, report vulnerabilities to the manufacturer and
significant risks to market surveillance authorities, cooperate with
authorities on request, and alert authorities and users if the manufacturer
has ceased operations. The captured page is the German text as republished by
Kunnus, with 2 related recitals (82, 83); only the Official Journal text is
binding.
