Summary of Article 20 (Obligations of distributors) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, the Cyber Resilience Act.

- **Due care (para. 1):** When distributors place a product with digital elements on the market, they must comply with the Regulation "mit der gebührenden Sorgfalt" (with due care).
- **Pre-market checks (para. 2):** Before making a product available, distributors must verify that it bears the CE marking and that the manufacturer and importer have met their obligations and provided the necessary documents.
- **Non-conformity (para. 3):** If a distributor has reason to believe the product or the manufacturer's processes don't meet the essential cybersecurity requirements in Annex I, it must not make the product available until conformity is established. If the product poses a significant cybersecurity risk, the distributor must also promptly inform the manufacturer and market surveillance authorities.
- **Corrective action (para. 4):** Distributors must ensure corrective measures are taken, which may include withdrawing or recalling the product. They must promptly inform the manufacturer of any vulnerability they become aware of, and notify the relevant market surveillance authorities if there is a significant risk.
- **Cooperation (para. 5):** On a reasoned request, distributors must provide market surveillance authorities with the information and documentation needed to demonstrate conformity, in a language the authority can easily understand, and cooperate on measures to address cybersecurity risks.
- **Manufacturer ceases operations (para. 6):** If a distributor learns that the manufacturer has ceased operations and therefore cannot meet its obligations, it must promptly inform the relevant market surveillance authorities and, as far as possible, the users.
