# Importer or distributor deemed manufacturer (CRA Art. 21)

An importer ("Einführer") or distributor ("Händler") "gilt für die Zwecke dieser
Verordnung als Hersteller" and takes on the manufacturer obligations of Art. 13 and
Art. 14 in two cases:

1. it places a product with digital elements on the market under its own name or
   trademark ("unter seinem eigenen Namen oder seiner eigenen Marke"), or
2. it carries out a substantial modification ("wesentliche Änderung") of a product
   with digital elements already placed on the market.

Commission guidance of 27 July 2026 (C(2026) 5252 final, section 4.4, as
summarised by the vendor Kunnus on the page - vendor interpretation of a
non-legally-binding guidance, not legal text):

- A substantially modified product counts as newly placed on the market when made
  available. A third party that substantially modifies becomes manufacturer
  (Art. 21/22); for component-related changes only for the modified part, provided
  overall cybersecurity is not affected.
- Integration is distinct: whoever assembles components into a new own product is
  the regular manufacturer of the whole product, not a substantial modifier of
  someone else's product (guidance example 51).
- Legacy products: a substantial modification after 11 December 2027 of a product
  placed on the market before that date makes the modifier a manufacturer
  (Art. 69(2)), again limited to the modified parts while overall cybersecurity is
  unaffected.
- The original manufacturer's obligations for the original product (vulnerability
  handling, conformity of unmodified parts) continue.
- Conformity assessment focuses on the modified parts; existing tests and
  documentation for unmodified parts may be reused.
