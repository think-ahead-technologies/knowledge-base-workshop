# Third party substantially modifying a product becomes manufacturer

Art. 22(1) CRA: a natural or legal person that is not the manufacturer, importer
or distributor ("nicht um den Hersteller, Einführer oder Händler handelt"), and
that carries out a "wesentliche Änderung an dem Produkt mit digitalen Elementen"
and makes that product available on the market, "gilt für die Zwecke dieser
Verordnung als Hersteller".

Art. 22(2) limits the scope: the person is subject to the obligations of
Art. 13 and 14

- for the part of the product affected by the substantial modification
  ("für den Teil des Produkts ..., der von der wesentlichen Änderung betroffen
  ist"), or
- for the entire product, if the substantial modification affects the
  cybersecurity of the product as a whole ("wenn sich die wesentliche Änderung
  auf die Cybersicherheit des Produkts mit digitalen Elementen insgesamt
  auswirkt").

Both conditions of para. 1 are needed: the substantial modification and making
the product available on the market.

Commission guidance section 4.4 (non-binding, as summarised on the page):
the modified product counts as newly placed on the market; assembling
components into an own new product is regular manufacturing, not a substantial
modification of someone else's product (example 51); a substantial
modification after 11 December 2027 of a product placed on the market before
that date makes the modifier a manufacturer (Art. 69(2)); the original
manufacturer's obligations for the original product continue; conformity
assessment focuses on modified parts and may reuse existing tests and
documentation for unchanged parts.
