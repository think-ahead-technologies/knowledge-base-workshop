# Economic operator identification and 10-year traceability

CRA Art. 23 makes the supply chain of products with digital elements traceable
for market surveillance:

- **Who is obliged:** all economic operators ("Wirtschaftsakteure") - not only
  manufacturers.
- **Trigger:** a request ("auf Anfrage") from a market surveillance authority
  ("Marktüberwachungsbehörden"). There is no proactive reporting duty in this article.
- **What (Art. 23(1)):**
  - (a) name and address of every economic operator from which they obtained
    ("bezogen") products with digital elements - unconditional;
  - (b) "sofern verfügbar" (where available), name and address of every economic
    operator to which they supplied ("abgegeben") such products.
- **How long (Art. 23(2)):** the information must be producible for ten years
  after obtaining the product and ten years after supplying it ("zehn Jahre nach
  dem Bezug ... sowie zehn Jahre nach der Abgabe").

Practical implication (inferred, not legal text): operators need to keep
upstream and downstream business-partner records per product for at least ten
years from the respective transaction. Downstream records are limited to economic
operators, so sales to end users are not covered by point (b).
