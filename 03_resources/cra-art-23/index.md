# CRA Art. 23 - Identification of economic operators (Identifizierung der Wirtschaftsakteure)

## Summary
Article 23 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") obliges every economic operator ("Wirtschaftsakteure") to
tell market surveillance authorities ("Marktüberwachungsbehörden"), on request,
from whom it obtained products with digital elements and - where available - to
whom it supplied them (name and address). Operators must be able to provide this
information for ten years after obtaining and ten years after supplying the
product. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus, with one
related recital listed; only the Official Journal text is binding.
