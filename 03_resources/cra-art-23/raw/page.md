**Article 23 - Identification of economic operators (EU Cyber Resilience Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847)**

- **Paragraph 1:** On request, economic operators must give market surveillance authorities the names and addresses of:
  - the economic operators they obtained products with digital elements from, and
  - where available, the economic operators they supplied those products to.
- **Paragraph 2:** Operators must be able to produce this information for ten years after obtaining a product and ten years after supplying it.
