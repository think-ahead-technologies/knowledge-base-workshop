# CRA Art. 24 - Obligations of open-source software stewards (Pflichten der Verwalter quelloffener Software)

## Summary
Article 24 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") sets the light-touch obligations of "Verwalter
quelloffener Software" (open-source software stewards): (1) develop and document
"auf überprüfbare Weise" a cybersecurity strategy that fosters secure development
and effective vulnerability handling by the developers, encourages voluntary
vulnerability reporting under Art. 15 and promotes sharing of vulnerability
information in the open-source community; (2) cooperate with market surveillance
authorities on request and hand over that documentation on reasoned request;
(3) the Art. 14(1) reporting duty for actively exploited vulnerabilities applies
to the extent they are involved in development, and Art. 14(3) and (8) apply to
severe incidents affecting network and information systems they provide for
development. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus,
plus a summary of the Commission guidance (non-binding), a vendor quick answer
and FAQ (non-binding) and a list of related recitals; only the Official Journal
text is binding.

- **Commission guidance C(2026) 5252 final, section 3.3 (non-binding)** - as summarised on the page: steward role is per FOSS project; duties tiered by non-technical support, infrastructure stewardship, engineering stewardship; not-for-profit entities with statutory charitable purpose remain stewards even when monetising (example 24: browser with search-engine partnership). Cites Art. 3 Nr. 14 for the definition.
- **Vendor quick answer and FAQ (Kunnus interpretation, not legal text)** - stewards are legal persons systematically supporting commercially used OSS (examples named: Apache Software Foundation, Eclipse Foundation, Linux Foundation); reduced duties, no full Annex I requirements; non-commercial OSS is out of scope and obligations fall on the integrating manufacturer.
- **Related recitals** - page lists recitals 87-90 (harmonised standards, EU declaration of conformity, CE marking, conformity evidence). These look unrelated to stewards; possibly a vendor mapping error.
