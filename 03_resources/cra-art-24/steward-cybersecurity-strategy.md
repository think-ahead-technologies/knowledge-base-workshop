# Open-source software steward cybersecurity strategy

Art. 24(1) CRA obliges "Verwalter quelloffener Software" to "entwickeln und
dokumentieren auf überprüfbare Weise eine Cybersicherheitsstrategie". The
strategy must:

- foster the development of a secure product with digital elements and an
  effective handling of vulnerabilities ("wirksamen Umgang mit Schwachstellen")
  by the product's developers;
- foster voluntary vulnerability reporting ("freiwillige Meldung von
  Schwachstellen gemäß Artikel 15") by those developers;
- take account of the steward's specific nature and its legal and
  organisational arrangements;
- cover in particular documentation, remediation and elimination of
  vulnerabilities ("Dokumentation, Behebung und Beseitigung von Schwachstellen"),
  and promote sharing of information on discovered vulnerabilities within the
  open-source community.

Art. 24(2): stewards cooperate with market surveillance authorities at their
request to mitigate cybersecurity risks of a product with digital elements that
qualifies as free and open-source software. On a reasoned request ("begründetes
Verlangen") they hand the para. 1 documentation to the authority, on paper or
electronically, in a language easily understood by that authority.

Takeaway: the steward regime is a documented-policy plus cooperation duty, not
the full manufacturer regime. The vendor FAQ (non-binding) adds that stewards do
not have to meet the full Annex I requirements.
