# Voluntary security attestation programmes for FOSS

Art. 25 CRA ("Sicherheitsbescheinigung für freie und quelloffene Software")
empowers the Commission to adopt delegated acts that supplement the regulation
with "freiwilliger Programme zur Bescheinigung der Sicherheit" (voluntary
security attestation programmes) for products with digital elements that
qualify as free and open-source software ("freie und quelloffene Software").

Key points of the legal text:

- **Purpose** - to facilitate the due-diligence obligation ("Sorgfaltspflicht")
  of Art. 13(5), especially for manufacturers that integrate free and
  open-source software components into their own products with digital
  elements.
- **Instrument** - delegated acts ("delegierte Rechtsakte") adopted in
  accordance with Art. 61. Art. 25 itself creates no programme; it only
  empowers the Commission to create one.
- **Voluntary** - participation is voluntary ("freiwillig").
- **Who may attest** - the developers or users of the FOSS product, and other
  third parties ("anderen Dritten").
- **Scope of the assessment** - conformity of these products with all or
  specific essential cybersecurity requirements ("grundlegenden
  Cybersicherheitsanforderungen") or other obligations laid down in the
  regulation.

Practical reading: an attestation of a FOSS component would give an
integrating manufacturer evidence for its Art. 13(5) due diligence on that
component. This is an inference from the stated purpose, not a statement of
the legal text.
