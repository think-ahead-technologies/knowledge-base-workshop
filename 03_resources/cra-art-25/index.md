# CRA Art. 25 - Security attestation of free and open-source software (Sicherheitsbescheinigung für freie und quelloffene Software)

## Summary
Article 25 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") empowers the Commission to adopt delegated acts under
Art. 61 that introduce voluntary security attestation programmes
("freiwilliger Programme zur Bescheinigung der Sicherheit") for products with
digital elements that qualify as free and open-source software. The goal is to
facilitate the due-diligence obligation of Art. 13(5) for manufacturers that
integrate FOSS components. Such programmes let the developers or users of the
FOSS, and other third parties, assess its conformity with all or specific
essential cybersecurity requirements or other obligations of the regulation.

Official Journal text is binding.
