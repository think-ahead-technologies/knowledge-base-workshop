# CRA Commission guidelines (Leitlinien) - mandate and minimum content

Art. 26 obliges the European Commission to publish guidelines ("Leitlinien")
that help economic operators ("Wirtschaftsakteure") apply the CRA, to ease
implementation and ensure its coherence. Special emphasis lies on easing
compliance for micro, small and medium-sized enterprises ("Kleinstunternehmen
und kleine und mittlere Unternehmen").

When the Commission issues guidelines under para. 1, they must cover at least
(para. 2):

- a) the scope of the regulation, with a focus on remote data processing
  solutions ("Datenfernverarbeitungslösungen") and free and open-source
  software ("freier und quelloffener Software");
- b) the application of support periods ("Unterstützungszeiträumen") for
  certain categories of products with digital elements;
- c) guidance for manufacturers that are also subject to other Union
  harmonisation legislation or related Union acts;
- d) the concept of substantial modification ("Begriff der wesentlichen
  Änderung").

The Commission must also maintain an easily accessible list of the delegated
and implementing acts adopted under the CRA. Para. 3 requires it to consult
relevant stakeholders ("einschlägigen Interessenträger") when drafting the
guidelines.

## Commission guidance as summarised by the page (vendor rendering, not legal text)
The page summarises the Commission guidance of 27 July 2026, C(2026) 5252
final, sections 1.1-1.2. This is Kunnus's summary of a non-binding Commission
document, not part of the regulation:

- Fulfils the Art. 26(1) mandate, SME focus; legally non-binding, no substitute
  for case-by-case assessment; only the CJEU interprets the CRA bindingly.
- Covers exactly the four Art. 26(2) topics; complements the Commission FAQ of
  3 December 2025; preceded by consultation of the Expert Group on
  Cybersecurity of Products with Digital Elements and a public consultation
  (3 March - 13 April 2026).
- Addressed to economic operators and authorities (market surveillance,
  notifying authorities, notified bodies).
- Further guidance announced on interplay with the AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689) and
  DORA (Reg. 2022/2554).
- Background: CRA follows the New Legislative Framework (Reg. 765/2008,
  Decision 768/2008/EC); market surveillance per Reg. 2019/1020; a planned
  "European Product Act" would modernise the NLF.
- Vendor practice tip: cite guidance by section and margin number toward
  auditors / notified bodies / authorities, but document your own assessment.
