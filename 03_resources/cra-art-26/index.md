# CRA Art. 26 - Guidelines (Leitlinien)

## Summary
Article 26 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter II
"Pflichten der Wirtschaftsakteure und Bestimmungen in Bezug auf freie und
quelloffene Software") mandates the European Commission to publish guidelines
("Leitlinien") helping economic operators apply the regulation, with a focus on
micro, small and medium-sized enterprises. The guidelines must at least cover
scope (remote data processing, FOSS), support periods, interplay with other
Union harmonisation law, and the concept of substantial modification; the
Commission also keeps a list of delegated and implementing acts and consults
stakeholders. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus,
plus a Kunnus summary of the Commission guidance C(2026) 5252 final (27 July
2026) and one related recital; only the Official Journal text is binding.
