# CRA common specifications - fallback to harmonised standards

Art. 27(2)-(7) CRA lets the Commission adopt implementing acts establishing
"gemeinsame Spezifikationen zu technischen Anforderungen" whose application
satisfies the essential cybersecurity requirements of Annex I. They are a
fallback, allowed only when both conditions hold:

- **(a)** the Commission has requested a harmonised standard under Art. 10(1)
  of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 and (i) the request was not accepted, (ii) the
  standards are not delivered within the set deadline, or (iii) the standards
  do not comply with the request; **and**
- **(b)** no reference to a harmonised standard meeting the relevant Annex I
  requirements is published in the Official Journal, and none is expected
  within a reasonable time.

**Procedure.** Adopted under the examination procedure of Art. 62(2). Before
drafting, the Commission informs the committee of Art. 22 of Regulation (EU)
No 1025/2012 that it considers the conditions met (para 3); while drafting it
takes views of relevant bodies into account and consults stakeholders (para 4).

**Effect and lifecycle.** Conformity with a common specification gives a
presumption of conformity insofar as it covers the requirements (para 5). Once
a harmonised standard covering the same requirements is published in the
Official Journal, the Commission repeals the overlapping implementing acts or
parts of them (para 6). A Member State that finds a common specification does
not fully meet Annex I informs the Commission with a detailed explanation; the
Commission may amend the implementing act (para 7).
