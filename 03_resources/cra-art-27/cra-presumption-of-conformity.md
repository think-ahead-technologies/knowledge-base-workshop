# CRA presumption of conformity - three routes

Art. 27 CRA ("Konformitätsvermutung") gives a manufacturer three ways to have
its product with digital elements and its own processes ("vom Hersteller
festgelegten Verfahren", e.g. vulnerability handling) presumed compliant with
the essential cybersecurity requirements of Annex I:

- **Harmonised standards (para 1)** - conformity with "harmonisierten Normen oder
  Teilen davon", whose references are published in the Official Journal of the
  EU. The Commission requests European standardisation organisations to draft
  them under Art. 10(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012, taking existing European
  and international cybersecurity standards into account.
- **Common specifications (para 5)** - conformity with "gemeinsamen
  Spezifikationen" set by Commission implementing act under para 2 (fallback
  route, see the common specifications note).
- **EU certification schemes (para 8)** - an EU declaration of conformity or a
  cybersecurity certificate issued under a European cybersecurity certification
  scheme adopted per Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Cybersecurity Act). Para 9: the
  Commission designates usable schemes by delegated act (Art. 61). A European
  cybersecurity certificate of at least assurance level "mittel" waives the
  manufacturer's obligation to have a third-party conformity assessment under
  Art. 32(2)(a)-(b) and Art. 32(3)(a)-(b) for the requirements concerned.

**Coverage limit.** Every route applies only "soweit" (insofar as) the standard,
specification or certificate covers the requirements - partial coverage yields
partial presumption.

**Commission guidance (interpretation, not legal text; non-binding).** Guidance
of 27 July 2026, section 6.3, as quoted on the page: add-on features outside a
standard's scope do not benefit; the manufacturer proves their conformity
separately and keeps overall responsibility; the full risk assessment under
Art. 13(2) remains mandatory even when a standard is applied. Kunnus adds a
coverage-matrix practice tip (vendor interpretation).
