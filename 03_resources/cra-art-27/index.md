# CRA Art. 27 - Presumption of conformity (Konformitätsvermutung)

## Summary
Article 27 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter III
"Konformität des Produkts mit digitalen Elementen") defines three routes by which
products with digital elements and the manufacturer's processes are presumed to
conform ("wird eine Konformität ... vermutet") with the essential cybersecurity
requirements of Annex I: (1) harmonised standards ("harmonisierte Normen")
published in the Official Journal, (5) common specifications ("gemeinsame
Spezifikationen") set by Commission implementing acts as a fallback when
standardisation fails (paras 2-4, 6-7), and (8) an EU declaration of conformity
or cybersecurity certificate under a European certification scheme per
Regulation (EU) 2019/881. In every route the presumption reaches only as far as
the standard / specification / certificate covers the requirements. Para 9 lets
the Commission designate such schemes by delegated act; a certificate of at
least assurance level "mittel" waives the third-party conformity assessment
obligation of Art. 32(2)(a)-(b) and 32(3)(a)-(b) for the covered requirements.
The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus; only the
Official Journal text is binding.
