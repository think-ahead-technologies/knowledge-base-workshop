# EU declaration of conformity under the CRA (EU-Konformitätserklärung)

Art. 28 CRA regulates the "EU-Konformitätserklärung" - the manufacturer's formal
statement that a product with digital elements meets the regulation.

- **Issuer and claim (para. 1)** - issued by the manufacturer under Art. 13(12);
  it states that fulfilment of the "grundlegenden Cybersicherheitsanforderungen
  in Anhang I" has been demonstrated ("nachgewiesen worden ist").
- **Structure and content (para. 2)** - follows the model in Annex V ("Muster in
  Anhang V") and contains the elements named in the applicable conformity
  assessment procedures of Annex VIII. It is updated as needed ("nach Bedarf
  aktualisiert").
- **Simplified declaration (para. 2)** - the "vereinfachte
  EU-Konformitätserklärung" under Art. 13(20) follows the model in Annex VI.
- **Language (para. 2)** - both the full and the simplified declaration are
  drawn up in the language(s) required by the Member State where the product is
  placed on the market or made available.
- **One declaration for several Union acts (para. 3)** - if the product is
  subject to several Union acts that each require a declaration of conformity, a
  single declaration ("eine einzige EU-Konformitätserklärung") covers all of
  them and lists those acts with their Official Journal references
  ("Fundstellen im Amtsblatt").
- **Responsibility (para. 4)** - by issuing it, the manufacturer assumes
  responsibility ("übernimmt ... die Verantwortung") for the product's
  conformity.
- **Delegated acts (para. 5)** - the Commission may, per Art. 61, add new
  elements to the minimum content in Annex V in light of technical developments.

Commission interpretation shown on the page (Guidance C(2026) 5252 final of
2026-07-27, section 7.4, **not legally binding**; summarized by the vendor):
product variants sharing architecture, security-relevant design, intended
purpose and risk profile may share one risk assessment, technical
documentation, conformity assessment and declaration. Colour, form factor or
storage size do not separate variants; differing communication interfaces,
software stacks, update mechanisms or remote connectivity do. The shared
declaration must identify the covered variants unambiguously, and new variants
with new risks require an update.
