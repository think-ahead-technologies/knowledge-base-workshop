# CRA Art. 28 - EU declaration of conformity (EU-Konformitätserklärung)

## Summary
Article 28 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter III
"Konformität des Produkts mit digitalen Elementen") governs the EU declaration
of conformity: the manufacturer issues it to state that compliance with the
essential cybersecurity requirements of Annex I has been demonstrated; it
follows the Annex V model (simplified version: Annex VI), is written in the
language(s) of the Member State of market placement, is updated as needed, is
combined into one declaration when several Union acts apply, and makes the
manufacturer responsible for conformity. The captured page is the German text as
republished by Kunnus, plus a section of the Commission guidance on product
families and one related recital; only the Official Journal text is binding.

- **Commission guidance section 7.4 - product families (non-binding interpretation, C(2026) 5252 final of 2026-07-27, as summarized by the vendor)** - variants with the same architecture, security-relevant design, intended purpose and risk profile may share one risk assessment, documentation, conformity assessment and declaration; the declaration must name the covered variants.
- **Vendor practice note (Kunnus, non-binding)** - group variants by security-relevant differences to reduce per-variant effort. The page has no separate FAQ.
- **Related recitals** - page lists recital 96 (proportionality of conformity assessment body fees).
