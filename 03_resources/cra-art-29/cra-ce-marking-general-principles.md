# CE marking under the CRA - general principles

Art. 29 CRA ("Allgemeine Grundsätze der CE-Kennzeichnung") states in full:
"Für die CE-Kennzeichnung gelten die allgemeinen Grundsätze gemäß Artikel 30 der
Verordnung (EG) Nr. 765/2008."

Takeaway: the CE marking ("CE-Kennzeichnung") of a product with digital elements
under the CRA is not a CRA-specific mark. It follows the same general principles
that apply to CE marking across EU product legislation, as laid down in Art. 30
of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 (New Legislative Framework). Anyone assessing CRA
CE marking must read that external article; the CRA itself adds only the
affixing rules of the next article (Art. 30 CRA, per page navigation). The page
offers no vendor guidance on this article.
