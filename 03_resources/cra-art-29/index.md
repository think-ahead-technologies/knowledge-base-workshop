# CRA Art. 29 - General principles of the CE marking (Allgemeine Grundsätze der CE-Kennzeichnung)

## Summary
Article 29 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter III
"Konformität des Produkts mit digitalen Elementen") is a single-sentence
reference provision: "Für die CE-Kennzeichnung gelten die allgemeinen Grundsätze
gemäß Artikel 30 der Verordnung (EG) Nr. 765/2008." The CRA does not define its
own CE-marking principles; it imports the horizontal ones of the New Legislative
Framework regulation 765/2008. The concrete CRA rules for affixing the marking
follow in Art. 30. The captured page is the German text as republished by
Kunnus; it carries no vendor FAQ, only one related recital (97, on regulatory
sandboxes). Only the Official Journal text is binding.
