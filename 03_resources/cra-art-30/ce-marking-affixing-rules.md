# CE marking affixing rules under the CRA

Art. 30 CRA fixes where, how and when the CE marking ("CE-Kennzeichnung") is
affixed to a product with digital elements ("Produkt mit digitalen Elementen").

- **Where (para. 1):** "gut sichtbar, leserlich und dauerhaft" on the product
  itself. If the nature of the product does not allow or warrant that, on the
  packaging and on the accompanying EU declaration of conformity
  ("EU-Konformitätserklärung", Art. 28).
- **Software (para. 1):** either on the EU declaration of conformity (Art. 28) or
  on the website accompanying the software product ("der das Softwareprodukt
  begleitenden Website"); in the website case the relevant section must be
  "für Verbraucher leicht und direkt zugänglich".
- **Size (para. 2):** may be smaller than 5 mm where the nature of the product
  requires, provided it stays visible and legible.
- **When (para. 3):** before placing on the market ("vor dem Inverkehrbringen").
  It may be followed by a pictogram or other mark indicating a special
  cybersecurity risk or use, as laid down in implementing acts under para. 6.
- **Notified body number (para. 4):** follows the CE marking when a notified body
  ("notifizierte Stelle") takes part in the conformity assessment based on full
  quality assurance (Module H, Art. 32). Affixed by the body itself or, on its
  instructions, by the manufacturer or its authorised representative
  ("Bevollmächtigter").
- **Enforcement (para. 5):** Member States build on existing mechanisms to ensure
  correct application and take action against improper use ("missbräuchliche
  Verwendung"). Where other Union harmonisation legislation also requires the CE
  marking, the marking means the product also meets those requirements.
- **Implementing acts (para. 6):** the Commission may set technical specifications
  for labels, pictograms or other marks on product security and support periods
  ("Unterstützungszeiträume"), plus mechanisms promoting their use and public
  awareness; it consults stakeholders and the ADCO (if set up under Art. 52(15));
  examination procedure of Art. 62(2).

## Mentions
- Art. 28 (EU declaration of conformity)
- Art. 32 (conformity assessment procedures; Module H, full quality assurance)
- Art. 52(15) (ADCO)
- Art. 62(2) (examination procedure / committee)
