# CRA Art. 30 - Rules and conditions for affixing the CE marking

## Summary
Article 30 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847), Chapter III
("KONFORMITÄT DES PRODUKTS MIT DIGITALEN ELEMENTEN"). It sets the practical rules
for affixing the CE marking ("CE-Kennzeichnung"): visible, legible and indelible on
the product, or else on packaging and the EU declaration of conformity; for
software on the declaration or the accompanying website; may be under 5 mm; must
be affixed before placing on the market; followed by the notified body's number
under Module H; Member State enforcement against misuse; and Commission
implementing acts for security labels, pictograms and support-period marks.
