**Article 30 - Rules and conditions for affixing the CE marking**
*Chapter III - Conformity of products with digital elements*
*Regulation (EU) 2024/2847*

1. **Placement:** The CE marking must be visible, legible, and permanent on the product. If the product type doesn't allow this, it goes on the packaging and the EU declaration of conformity. For software, it can go on the declaration or on an accompanying website that consumers can easily reach. Quote: "Die CE-Kennzeichnung ist gut sichtbar, leserlich und dauerhaft auf dem Produkt mit digitalen Elementen anzubringen."
2. **Size:** The mark may be smaller than 5 mm if it remains visible and legible.
3. **Timing:** It must be affixed before the product is placed on the market. A pictogram or other sign may follow it to flag a specific cybersecurity risk or use.
4. **Notified body number:** If a notified body is involved in the full quality assurance procedure (Module H, Article 32), its identification number follows the CE mark. The body or the manufacturer (or its authorised representative) affixes it.
5. **Enforcement and other laws:** Member States enforce correct use and act on misuse. If other EU harmonisation laws also require the CE mark, affixing it signals compliance with those laws too.
6. **Implementing acts:** The Commission may adopt technical specifications for labels, pictograms, and support periods, along with measures to promote their use and raise public awareness. It consults stakeholders and, where established, the ADCO (Article 52(15)), using the examination procedure in Article 62(2).
