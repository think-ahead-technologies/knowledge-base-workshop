# CRA Art. 31 - Technical documentation (Technische Dokumentation)

## Summary
Article 31 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter III
"Konformität des Produkts mit digitalen Elementen") regulates the manufacturer's
technical documentation: what it must show (conformity with Annex I, at least the
Annex VII content), when it is drawn up and kept up to date (before placing on
the market, then during the support period), a single combined documentation
where other Union acts also require one, the language rule tied to the notified
body, and the Commission's power to extend Annex VII by delegated act. The
captured page is the German text republished by Kunnus, plus a vendor quick
answer, a summary of Commission guidance section 8.2 (RDPS) and a vendor FAQ -
all non-binding; only the Official Journal text is binding.
