# CRA technical documentation (Technische Dokumentation)

Art. 31 of the Cyber Resilience Act sets the rules for the manufacturer's
technical documentation ("technische Dokumentation") of a "Produkt mit digitalen
Elementen". From the legal text (German, as captured):

- **Content (para. 1)** - it holds "alle einschlägigen Daten oder Einzelheiten"
  on how the manufacturer ensures that the product and the processes it set up
  ("die vom Hersteller festgelegten Verfahren") meet the essential cybersecurity
  requirements of Annex I. Minimum content: "zumindest die in Anhang VII
  genannten Angaben".
- **Timing (para. 2)** - drawn up before placing on the market
  ("vor dem Inverkehrbringen") and, where appropriate, continuously updated at
  least during the support period ("zumindest während des
  Unterstützungszeitraums, laufend aktualisiert").
- **Single documentation (para. 3)** - for products under Art. 12 that are also
  subject to other Union acts requiring technical documentation, one single
  technical documentation is drawn up covering Annex VII plus the other acts'
  requirements.
- **Language (para. 4)** - documentation and correspondence on conformity
  assessment procedures are written in an official language of the Member State
  where the notified body ("notifizierte Stelle") is established, or in a
  language that body accepts.
- **Delegated acts (para. 5)** - the Commission may, per Art. 61, add elements to
  Annex VII to follow technical developments, while trying to keep the
  administrative burden proportionate for micro, small and medium-sized
  enterprises.

The article itself sets no retention period; the "10 years" stated on the page
is vendor interpretation (see below).

## Commission guidance and vendor interpretation (not legal text)

- Commission guidance C(2026) 5252 final of 27 July 2026, section 8.2, as
  summarised by Kunnus (non-binding; only the CJEU interprets bindingly): remote
  data processing solutions (RDPS) belong in risk assessment, technical
  documentation and conformity assessment, limited to the software modules that
  carry product functionality and their interfaces; backends without direct
  product interaction are not RDPS but remain external dependencies; an RDPS
  serving several products is declared in each product's documentation and its
  documentation may be reused; reusable evidence includes NIS-2 evidence
  (Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2690), DORA evidence, EU cybersecurity
  certificates and ISO/IEC 27001/27017; changes to third-party services are not
  a substantial modification, but SLAs and the risk assessment should follow.
- Kunnus FAQ (vendor interpretation): keep the documentation 10 years after
  placing on the market (linked by the vendor to the revised Product Liability
  Directive); language at least one EU official language accepted by the
  authorities (note: para. 4 actually ties the language to the notified body's
  Member State); incomplete documentation in an audit can lead to a deadline to
  supply it, fines, sales ban or recall.
