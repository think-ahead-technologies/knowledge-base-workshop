# Conformity assessment procedures by product category (CRA Art. 32)

Art. 32 requires the manufacturer to assess the product with digital elements and
its own processes against the essential cybersecurity requirements of Annex I, and
restricts the allowed procedure ("Verfahren", all per Annex VIII) by category:

| Category | Allowed procedures (legal text) |
|----------|---------------------------------|
| Default (para. 1) | (a) "internes Kontrollverfahren" (Module A); (b) "EU-Baumusterprüfverfahren" (Module B) followed by conformity based on internal production control (Module C); (c) "umfassende Qualitätssicherung" (Module H); (d) where available and applicable, a European cybersecurity certification scheme under Art. 27(9) |
| Important, Class I of Annex III (para. 2) | If harmonised standards, common specifications or European certification schemes of at least assurance level "mittel" under Art. 27 were not or only partly applied, or do not exist: Module B+C or Module H. (Read together with para. 1, full application of such instruments keeps the para. 1 routes open.) |
| Important, Class II of Annex III (para. 3) | Module B+C; Module H; or, where available and applicable, a certification scheme under Art. 27(9) at least at assurance level "mittel" under Regulation (EU) 2019/881 |
| Critical, Annex IV (para. 4) | A European certification scheme under Art. 8(1); if the conditions of Art. 8(1) are not met, one of the para. 3 procedures |

Module A is self-assessment; Modules B+C and H involve a notified body. Vendor
interpretation (Kunnus rendering of non-binding Commission guidance C(2026) 5252
final, section 6.2, not legal text): Class II and critical products always need a
third-party assessment; for Class I, Module A is possible when a harmonised
standard is fully applied that covers at least all risks of the core
functionality, extra risks being handled by documented additional measures; the
core functionality of the overall product decides the category, not an integrated
critical component (Example 63).
