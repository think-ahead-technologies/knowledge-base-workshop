# FOSS conformity assessment route (CRA Art. 32(5))

Manufacturers of products with digital elements that qualify as free and
open-source software ("freie und quelloffene Software") and fall into the
categories of Annex III (important products) may demonstrate conformity with
Annex I using any of the para. 1 procedures - including internal control
(Module A) - instead of the stricter Class I/II procedures, on condition that the
technical documentation referred to in Art. 31 is made publicly available
("der Öffentlichkeit zugänglich gemacht") at the time the product is placed on
the market.

The legal text names Annex III only; critical products of Annex IV are not covered
by this paragraph. Vendor interpretation (Kunnus rendering of non-binding
Commission guidance, section 6.2, not legal text): FOSS products of Class I/II
"dürfen immer selbst bewerten".
