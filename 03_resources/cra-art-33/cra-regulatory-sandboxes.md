# CRA cyber resilience regulatory sandboxes

Art. 33(2) lets Member States, where necessary, set up "Reallabore für
Cyberresilienz" (cyber resilience regulatory sandboxes):

- **Purpose** - controlled test environments ("kontrollierte Prüfumgebungen")
  for innovative products with digital elements, to facilitate their
  development, design, validation and testing for compliance with the regulation
  for a limited period before placing on the market ("vor dem Inverkehrbringen").
- **Support** - the Commission and, where appropriate, ENISA may provide
  technical support, advice and tools for setting up and running sandboxes.
- **Supervision** - sandboxes are set up under the direct supervision, guidance
  and support of the market surveillance authorities.
- **Notification** - Member States inform the Commission and the other market
  surveillance authorities of a new sandbox via the ADCO (administrative
  cooperation group).
- **No immunity** - sandboxes leave the supervisory and corrective powers of
  competent authorities unaffected.
- **Access** - open, fair and transparent; access is to be facilitated in
  particular for micro and small enterprises, including start-ups.

Setting up sandboxes is optional for Member States ("können").
