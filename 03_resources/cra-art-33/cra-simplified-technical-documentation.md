# CRA simplified technical documentation for micro and small enterprises

Art. 33(5) allows micro and small enterprises ("Kleinst- und Kleinunternehmen" -
not medium-sized ones) to provide all elements of the technical documentation
listed in Annex VII in a simplified format:

- The Commission lays down the simplified form ("vereinfachtes Formular") by
  implementing acts, tailored to micro and small enterprises, including how the
  Annex VII elements are to be presented.
- The implementing acts follow the examination procedure of Art. 62(2).
- Using it is optional; but an enterprise that opts for simplified provision of
  the Annex VII information must use that form.
- Notified bodies ("notifizierte Stellen") must accept the form for conformity
  assessment.

The simplification concerns the format of the documentation, not its content:
all Annex VII elements still have to be provided.
