# CRA SME support measures by Member States and Commission

Art. 33(1) obliges Member States to take, "gegebenenfalls" (where appropriate),
measures tailored to the needs of micro and small enterprises:

- (a) specific awareness-raising and training measures ("Sensibilisierungs- und
  Schulungsmaßnahmen") on applying the regulation;
- (b) a dedicated communication channel ("spezieller Kommunikationskanal") for
  micro and small enterprises and, where appropriate, local public authorities,
  to give advice and answer questions on implementation;
- (c) support for testing and conformity assessment activities
  ("Prüf- und Konformitätsbewertungstätigkeiten"), where needed with the support
  of the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre.

On the Union side, the Commission provides guidelines for micro, small and
medium-sized enterprises on implementing the regulation in line with Art. 26
(para. 3), and informs about available financial support within existing Union
programmes, in particular to ease the financial burden on micro and small
enterprises (para. 4). Art. 33 does not itself create a funding programme.
