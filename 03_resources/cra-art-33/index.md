# CRA Art. 33 - Support measures for micro, small and medium-sized enterprises, including start-ups

## Summary
Article 33 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter III
"Konformität des Produkts mit digitalen Elementen") sets out relief for
"Kleinst- und Kleinunternehmen" (micro and small enterprises) and SMEs. Member
States take tailored measures where appropriate: awareness and training, a
dedicated communication channel for advice, and support for testing and
conformity assessment (para. 1). They may set up cyber resilience regulatory
sandboxes ("Reallabore für Cyberresilienz", para. 2). The Commission provides SME
guidance in line with Art. 26 (para. 3) and informs about available Union funding
(para. 4). Micro and small enterprises may submit the Annex VII technical
documentation in a simplified format laid down by implementing act, which
notified bodies must accept (para. 5). The page is the German text republished
by Kunnus; only the Official Journal text is binding.
