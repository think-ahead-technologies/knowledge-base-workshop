**Title:** Artikel 33: Unterstützungsmaßnahmen für Kleinstunternehmen sowie kleine und mittlere Unternehmen, einschließlich Start-up-Unternehmen

**Chapter:** Kapitel III, Konformität des Produkts mit digitalen Elementen

**Source:** Verordnung (EU) 2024/2847, published 10 December 2024; page last reviewed by Kunnus in March 2026

**Article 33 summary:**

1. **Member State measures** tailored to micro and small enterprises:
   - (a) specific awareness and training measures for applying the regulation
   - (b) a dedicated communication channel for advice and questions
   - (c) support for testing and conformity assessment, possibly with the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre
2. **Cyber resilience regulatory sandboxes:** Member States may set up controlled testing environments for innovative products, for a limited period before market placement. Access must be open, fair, and transparent, and easier for micro and small enterprises, including start-ups.
3. **Guidelines:** The Commission provides guidance for micro and small enterprises, in line with Article 26.
4. **Financial support:** The Commission informs about available funding under existing Union programmes.
5. **Simplified technical documentation:** Micro and small enterprises may provide the Annex VII elements in a simplified format. The Commission sets this format by implementing act, and notified bodies must accept it.
