# CRA Art. 34 - Mutual recognition agreements (Abkommen über die gegenseitige Anerkennung)

## Summary
Article 34 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter III
"Konformität des Produkts mit digitalen Elementen") is a single sentence: taking
into account a third country's level of technical development and its approach to
conformity assessment, the Union may, in accordance with Article 218 TFEU
("Artikel 218 AEUV"), conclude "Abkommen über die gegenseitige Anerkennung" with
third countries to promote and facilitate international trade. It grants the
Union a power and imposes no direct obligation on economic operators. The captured
page is the German text as republished by Kunnus, with one related recital
(106, truncated) and no FAQ; only the Official Journal text is binding.
