# CRA mutual recognition agreements with third countries

Art. 34 CRA lets the Union conclude "Abkommen über die gegenseitige Anerkennung"
(mutual recognition agreements, MRAs) with third countries ("Drittländern").
The legal text sets:

- **Who and how** - "die Union", acting "im Einklang mit Artikel 218 AEUV"
  (the TFEU procedure for negotiating and concluding international agreements).
- **What the Union must take into account** - "das Niveau der technischen
  Entwicklung" (the level of technical development) and "das Konzept für die
  Konformitätsbewertung eines Drittlands" (the third country's approach to
  conformity assessment).
- **Purpose** - "um den internationalen Handel zu fördern und zu erleichtern"
  (to promote and facilitate international trade).

It is a power ("kann"), not an obligation: the article creates no direct duty
for manufacturers or other economic operators. In practice an MRA would let
conformity assessment performed under a third country's regime be recognised
for the CRA (inferred from the general meaning of MRAs; the article itself does
not spell out the effect).
