# CRA Art. 35 - Notification (Notifizierung)

## Summary
Article 35 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847) opens
Chapter IV "Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen" (notification of
conformity assessment bodies). Para. 1 obliges Member States to notify the
Commission and the other Member States of the bodies authorised to carry out
conformity assessments under the regulation. Para. 2 obliges Member States to
ensure, by 11 December 2026, a sufficient number of notified bodies in the Union
to avoid bottlenecks and obstacles to market access. The captured page is the
German text as republished by Kunnus; it carries no FAQ, guidance or recital
list for this article, only a vendor disclaimer that the Official Journal text
alone is binding.
