# CRA notification of conformity assessment bodies and notified-body capacity

Art. 35 is the entry point of CRA Chapter IV ("Notifizierung von
Konformitätsbewertungsstellen"). It sets two duties for Member States:

- **Notification (para. 1)** - Member States notify the Commission and the other
  Member States of the bodies authorised to carry out conformity assessments
  under the CRA ("die Stellen, die befugt sind, Konformitätsbewertungen gemäß
  dieser Verordnung durchzuführen"). A body becomes a "notifizierte Stelle"
  (notified body) through this notification.
- **Sufficient capacity by a deadline (para. 2)** - by **11 December 2026**
  Member States must ensure there is a sufficient number of notified bodies in
  the Union ("eine ausreichende Zahl notifizierter Stellen") able to carry out
  conformity assessments, so as to avoid bottlenecks and obstacles to market
  access ("Engpässe und Hindernisse mit Blick auf den Marktzugang").

Why it matters: products whose conformity assessment route requires a notified
body depend on enough such bodies existing; para. 2 is the Union's safeguard
against a certification bottleneck. The 11 December 2026 date is a Member State
obligation, not a manufacturer obligation.
