# CRA Art. 36 - Notifying authorities

## Summary
Article 36 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847), Chapter IV
("Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen"). It obliges each Member State to
designate a notifying authority ("notifizierende Behörde") responsible for the
procedures to assess, designate and notify conformity assessment bodies
("Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") and to monitor them, including compliance with
Art. 41. Member States may let a national accreditation body under Regulation (EC)
No 765/2008 do the assessment and monitoring. If the authority delegates
assessment, notification or monitoring to a non-governmental body, that body must
be a legal person, meet Art. 37 accordingly and cover liability arising from its
activities; the notifying authority keeps full responsibility for that body's work.
