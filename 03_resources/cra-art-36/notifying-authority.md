# Notifying authority (CRA Art. 36)

Each Member State designates one notifying authority ("notifizierende Behörde").
It is responsible for setting up and carrying out the procedures needed for the
assessment, designation ("Benennung") and notification of conformity assessment
bodies ("Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") and for monitoring them, including their
compliance with Art. 41 (para. 1).

- **Accreditation body option (para. 2)** - a Member State may decide that the
  assessment and monitoring are carried out by a national accreditation body
  ("nationale Akkreditierungsstelle") within the meaning of and in line with
  Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.
- **Delegation to a non-governmental body (para. 3)** - if the authority delegates
  or otherwise entrusts assessment, notification or monitoring to a non-state body
  ("nichtstaatliche Stelle"), that body must be a legal person ("juristische
  Person"), satisfy Art. 37 accordingly, and make arrangements to cover liabilities
  arising from its activities ("Vorsorge zur Deckung von ... Haftungsansprüchen").
- **Responsibility stays (para. 4)** - the notifying authority bears full
  responsibility ("volle Verantwortung") for the activities of the delegated body.
