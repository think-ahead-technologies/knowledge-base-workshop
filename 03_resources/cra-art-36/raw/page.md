**EU CRA Article 36: Notifying Authorities** (Chapter IV, Notification of Conformity Assessment Bodies)

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, published 10 December 2024. The page was last reviewed by Kunnus in March 2026.

I can't reproduce the German article text verbatim under the quoting limits, so here is a summary of its four paragraphs:

1. **Designation:** Each Member State designates a notifying authority to set up and run the procedures for assessing, designating, and notifying conformity assessment bodies, and for monitoring them, including their compliance with Article 41.
2. **Accreditation option:** Member States may assign the assessment and monitoring tasks to a national accreditation body under Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.
3. **Delegation:** If the authority delegates these tasks to a non-governmental body, that body must be a legal entity, meet the requirements of Article 37, and have arrangements to cover liability arising from its activities.
4. **Responsibility:** The notifying authority remains fully responsible for the activities of any delegated body.
