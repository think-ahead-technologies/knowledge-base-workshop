# CRA Art. 37 - Requirements relating to notifying authorities (Anforderungen an notifizierende Behörden)

## Summary
Article 37 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") sets six organisational
requirements for the national "notifizierende Behörden" that notify conformity
assessment bodies ("Konformitätsbewertungsstellen"): no conflict of interest with
those bodies, objectivity and impartiality ("Objektivität und Unparteilichkeit"),
separation of the person deciding on a notification from the person who did the
assessment, a ban on offering conformity assessment activities or consultancy on
a commercial or competitive basis, confidentiality of information obtained, and
sufficient competent staff. The captured page is the German text as republished
by Kunnus; it carries no FAQ or recital list for this article. Only the Official
Journal text is binding.
