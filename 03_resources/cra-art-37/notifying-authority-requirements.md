# Notifying authority requirements - independence, impartiality, competence

Under CRA Art. 37, a "notifizierende Behörde" (the national authority that
assesses and notifies conformity assessment bodies) must meet six requirements:

1. **No conflict of interest** - it is set up so that there is no
   "Interessenkonflikt" with the "Konformitätsbewertungsstellen".
2. **Objectivity and impartiality** - its organisation and operation safeguard
   "Objektivität und Unparteilichkeit" of its activities.
3. **Separation of assessment and decision** - each decision on notifying a
   conformity assessment body is taken by competent persons who are not the
   same persons who carried out the assessment.
4. **No competing services** - it must not offer or provide any activities that
   conformity assessment bodies perform, nor consultancy services, "auf einer
   gewerblichen oder wettbewerblichen Basis".
5. **Confidentiality** - it safeguards the "Vertraulichkeit" of the information
   it obtains.
6. **Adequate staffing** - it has a sufficient number of competent staff
   ("kompetente Mitarbeiter in ausreichender Zahl") to perform its tasks properly.

These are requirements on public authorities, not on manufacturers; for product
teams they matter only as the basis for trusting a notified body's
third-party conformity assessment.
