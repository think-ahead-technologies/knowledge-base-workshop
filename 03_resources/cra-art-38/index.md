# CRA Art. 38 - Information obligation on notifying authorities (Informationspflichten der notifizierenden Behörden)

## Summary
Article 38 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") is a short transparency
provision. (1) Member States inform the Commission of their procedures for
assessing and notifying conformity assessment bodies
("Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") and for monitoring notified bodies
("notifizierte Stellen"), and of any changes to them. (2) The Commission makes
that information public. The captured page is the German text as republished
by Kunnus; it carries no FAQ and no recitals list for this article, only a
generic disclaimer that the Official Journal text alone is binding.
