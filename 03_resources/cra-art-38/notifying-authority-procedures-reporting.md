# Notifying-authority procedures reporting

Art. 38 CRA ("Informationspflichten der notifizierenden Behörden") puts a
reporting duty on Member States and a publication duty on the Commission:

- **Para. 1 - Member States report** to the Commission their procedures for
  - assessing and notifying conformity assessment bodies ("Verfahren zur
    Bewertung und Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen"), and
  - monitoring notified bodies ("Überwachung notifizierter Stellen"),
  - plus any changes to these procedures ("sowie über diesbezügliche Änderungen").
- **Para. 2 - Commission publishes** that information ("macht die in Absatz 1
  genannte Information der Öffentlichkeit zugänglich").

Takeaway: how each Member State vets and supervises the bodies that perform
third-party CRA conformity assessments is meant to be publicly visible. For a
manufacturer that needs a notified body, this is where the national procedures
behind a body's notification can be looked up. The duty is on public
authorities; it creates no obligation for economic operators.
