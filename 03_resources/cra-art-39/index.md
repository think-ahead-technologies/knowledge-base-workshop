# CRA Art. 39 - Requirements relating to notified bodies (Anforderungen an notifizierte Stellen)

## Summary
Article 39 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") lists the requirements a
conformity assessment body ("Konformitätsbewertungsstelle") must meet in
paragraphs 2 to 12 to be notified: legal personality under national law,
third-party independence, no involvement in or consultancy for the assessed
products, professionalism free from (financial) influence, capacity for all
Annex VIII tasks (staff, documented procedures, means and equipment), staff
competence incl. knowledge of Annex I, guaranteed impartiality with
remuneration not tied to number or outcome of assessments, liability
insurance, professional secrecy ("berufliche Schweigepflicht"), participation
in standardisation and the Art. 51 coordination group, and fair, proportionate
terms with attention to fees for micro, small and medium-sized enterprises.
The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus; it carries no
article FAQ or recitals list. Only the Official Journal text is binding.
