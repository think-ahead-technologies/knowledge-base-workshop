# Notified body independence and impartiality (CRA Art. 39)

- **Third party (para 3)** - the body is an "unabhängiger Dritter", independent of the organisation or product it assesses. A body belonging to a business or professional association ("Wirtschaftsverband oder einem Fachverband") assessing products of companies the association represents may count as such, provided its independence and absence of any conflict of interest are demonstrated.
- **No role in the product (para 4)** - the body, its top management ("oberste Leitungsebene") and assessment staff must not be designer, developer, manufacturer, supplier, importer, distributor, installer, purchaser, owner, user or maintainer of the assessed products, nor authorised representative of any of them, and must not be directly involved in or represent parties in those activities. Using already-assessed products needed for the body's work, or for personal use, is allowed. No activities that could compromise independence or integrity - "Dies gilt besonders für Beratungsdienstleistungen" (consultancy in particular). Subsidiaries and subcontractors must not affect confidentiality, objectivity or impartiality.
- **No undue influence (para 5)** - highest professional integrity and technical competence; free from all pressure and inducements, especially financial, that could influence judgement or results, in particular from persons interested in the outcome.
- **Impartiality and pay (para 8)** - impartiality of the body, top management and assessment staff must be guaranteed; their remuneration must not depend on the number of assessments carried out or their results.
