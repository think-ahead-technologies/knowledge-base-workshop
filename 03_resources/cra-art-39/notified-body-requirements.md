# Notified body requirements (CRA Art. 39)

Under Art. 39(1) a "Konformitätsbewertungsstelle" must fulfil paragraphs 2 to
12 to be notified. Beyond independence and impartiality (separate note):

- **Legal form (para 2)** - established under national law, with legal personality ("Rechtspersönlichkeit").
- **Capacity (para 6)** - able to perform all conformity assessment tasks assigned by Annex VIII for which it is notified, whether done by itself, on its behalf or under its responsibility. At all times, per procedure and per type/category of product, it has: (a) qualified, experienced staff; (b) procedure descriptions ensuring transparency and reproducibility, separating notified-body tasks from other activities; (c) procedures that take account of company size, sector, structure, product-technology complexity and mass/serial production; plus the means for technical and administrative tasks and access to equipment/facilities.
- **Staff competence (para 7)** - sound technical and vocational training; sufficient knowledge of assessment requirements and authority to perform them; adequate knowledge of the essential cybersecurity requirements of Annex I, applicable harmonised standards and common specifications, and Union harmonisation law; ability to draw up certificates, records and reports.
- **Liability insurance (para 9)** - "Haftpflichtversicherung" required unless liability is assumed by the Member State under national law or the Member State is directly responsible for the assessment.
- **Professional secrecy (para 10)** - information obtained in Annex VIII tasks is covered by "berufliche Schweigepflicht", except towards the market surveillance authorities of the Member State of activity; proprietary rights are protected; documented procedures ensure compliance.
- **Standardisation and coordination (para 11)** - participate in (or keep staff informed of) relevant standardisation and the Art. 51 coordination group of notified bodies, and apply its administrative decisions and documents as general guidelines.
- **Fair terms (para 12)** - consistent, fair, proportionate and reasonable terms and conditions, avoiding unnecessary burden on economic operators, and taking account of micro, small and medium-sized enterprises' interests, in particular on fees.
