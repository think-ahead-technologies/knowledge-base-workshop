**Article 39 (Requirements for notified bodies), in summary:**

1. Conformity assessment bodies must meet paragraphs 2 to 12 to be notified.
2. They must be established under national law and have legal personality.
3. They must be independent of the organisation or product they assess. Industry association members may qualify if their independence and freedom from conflicts of interest are demonstrated.
4. Neither the body, its top management, nor its assessment staff may be involved in designing, developing, making, supplying, installing, using, or maintaining the products they assess, or represent parties doing so. Consultancy that could compromise independence is excluded.
5. They must act with professional competence and be free from undue influence, especially financial influence from parties interested in the outcome.
6. They must be able to perform all tasks in Annex VIII, with suitable staff, documented procedures proportionate to company size and product complexity, and the necessary equipment.
7. Assessment staff need solid training, sufficient knowledge of the requirements and standards, and the ability to produce certificates, records, and reports.
8. Impartiality must be guaranteed, and pay must not depend on the number or results of assessments.
9. They must hold professional liability insurance, unless national law or direct state responsibility covers liability.
10. They must keep information confidential, except toward market surveillance authorities, protect intellectual property, and have documented procedures to ensure this.
11. They must take part in relevant standardisation work and the coordination group under Article 51, or ensure their staff are informed, and apply its outputs as general guidance.
12. They must operate on fair, proportionate, and consistent terms, avoid unnecessary burden, and consider the interests of micro and small enterprises, particularly on fees.

For the authoritative wording, see the Official Journal text of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 on EUR-Lex. This summary is informational and not legal advice.
