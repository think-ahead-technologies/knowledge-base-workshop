# CRA Art. 40 - Presumption of conformity of notified bodies (Vermutung der Konformität von notifizierten Stellen)

## Summary
Article 40 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") establishes a presumption of
conformity for conformity assessment bodies: a body that demonstrates it meets
the criteria of relevant harmonised standards (or parts of them) published in
the Official Journal is presumed to meet the requirements of Art. 39, to the
extent those standards cover them. The captured page is the German text as
republished by Kunnus; it carries no FAQ or recitals, only navigation and a
non-binding disclaimer. Only the Official Journal text is binding.
