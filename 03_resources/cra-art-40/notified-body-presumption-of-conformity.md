# Presumption of conformity for notified bodies via harmonised standards

Art. 40 CRA gives conformity assessment bodies ("Konformitätsbewertungsstellen")
a shortcut to show they meet the requirements for notified bodies. If a body
demonstrates ("nachweist") that it fulfils the criteria of the relevant
harmonised standards ("einschlägigen harmonisierten Normen") - or parts of them -
whose references have been published in the Official Journal of the EU, it is
presumed ("wird davon ausgegangen") to meet the requirements of Art. 39.

Key points from the legal text:
- Only harmonised standards with references published in the Official Journal
  ("im Amtsblatt der Europäischen Union veröffentlicht") trigger the presumption.
- Partial compliance ("oder von Teilen davon") is allowed.
- The presumption is limited: it applies only "soweit die geltenden Normen diese
  Anforderungen abdecken" - requirements not covered by the standards must be
  shown by other means.
- It is a rebuttable presumption about the body (notified body requirements),
  not about the products it assesses.
