**Chapter IV: Notification of conformity assessment bodies**
**Article 40: Presumption of conformity of notified bodies**

The article sets out a presumption of conformity. If a conformity assessment body demonstrates that it meets the criteria of the relevant harmonised standards (or parts of them), whose references are published in the Official Journal of the EU, it is presumed to meet the requirements of Article 39, to the extent those standards cover them. The key clause begins "Weist eine Konformitätsbewertungsstelle nach,".
