# CRA Art. 41 - Branches of and subcontracting by notified bodies (Zweigstellen notifizierter Stellen und Vergabe von Unteraufträgen durch notifizierte Stellen)

## Summary
Article 41 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") governs how a notified body
may pass conformity-assessment tasks to subcontractors ("Unterauftragnehmer")
or branches ("Zweigstellen"): they must meet the Art. 39 requirements and the
notifying authority must be informed; the notified body keeps full
responsibility wherever they are established; the manufacturer must consent;
and qualification and work records must be kept available for the notifying
authority. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus; it
carries no FAQ or recital list for this article. Only the Official Journal
text is binding.
