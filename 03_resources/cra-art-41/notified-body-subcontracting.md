# CRA notified bodies - subcontracting and branches

Art. 41 CRA ("Zweigstellen notifizierter Stellen und Vergabe von Unteraufträgen
durch notifizierte Stellen") sets four conditions when a notified body
("notifizierte Stelle") hands conformity-assessment tasks to a subcontractor
("Unterauftragnehmer") or a branch ("Zweigstelle"):

1. **Same requirements** - the notified body must ensure the subcontractor or
   branch meets the requirements of Art. 39 (requirements for notified bodies)
   and must inform the notifying authority ("notifizierende Behörde").
2. **Full responsibility stays** - the notified body bears "die volle
   Verantwortung" for work done by subcontractors or branches, "unabhängig
   davon, wo diese niedergelassen sind" (regardless of where they are
   established).
3. **Manufacturer consent** - work may be subcontracted or assigned to a branch
   only "mit Zustimmung des Herstellers".
4. **Records for the authority** - the notified body keeps available for the
   notifying authority the relevant documents on the assessment of the
   subcontractor's or branch's qualifications and on the work they carried out
   under the regulation.

Practical reading for a manufacturer: outsourcing by the notified body never
shifts accountability away from it, and the manufacturer has a veto (consent
is a precondition).
