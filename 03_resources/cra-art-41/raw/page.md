**EU CRA Article 41: Branches of notified bodies and subcontracting by notified bodies**
*Chapter IV: Notification of conformity assessment bodies*
Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, published 10 December 2024. The page was last reviewed by Kunnus in March 2026.

1. **Subcontractors and branches:** When a notified body assigns conformity assessment tasks to a subcontractor or a branch, it must ensure they meet the requirements of Article 39 and inform the notifying authority.
2. **Full responsibility:** The notified body remains fully responsible for work done by subcontractors or branches, wherever they are established.
3. **Manufacturer consent:** Work may only be subcontracted or transferred to a branch with the manufacturer's consent.
4. **Documentation:** The notified body must keep relevant documents available for the notifying authority, including its assessment of the subcontractor's or branch's qualifications and the work performed.
