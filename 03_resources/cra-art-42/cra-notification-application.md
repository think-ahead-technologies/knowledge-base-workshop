# CRA notification application of a conformity assessment body (Antrag auf Notifizierung)

Art. 42 sets out how a conformity assessment body ("Konformitätsbewertungsstelle")
applies to become a notified body under the CRA:

- **Where to apply (para. 1)** - to the notifying authority ("notifizierende
  Behörde") of the Member State in which the body is established
  ("niedergelassen").
- **What the application contains (para. 2)** - a description of the conformity
  assessment activities, of the conformity assessment procedure(s), and of the
  product(s) with digital elements ("Produkte mit digitalen Elementen") for which
  the body claims competence; plus, where applicable, an accreditation
  certificate ("Akkreditierungsurkunde") issued by a national accreditation body
  ("nationale Akkreditierungsstelle") attesting that the body meets the
  requirements of Art. 39.
- **Without accreditation (para. 3)** - the body must give the notifying
  authority all documentary evidence needed to verify, recognise and regularly
  monitor ("zu überprüfen, festzustellen und regelmäßig zu überwachen") its
  compliance with Art. 39.

So accreditation is the default route to prove competence, but not mandatory;
a non-accredited body carries the full evidence burden and accepts ongoing
monitoring by the notifying authority.
