# CRA Art. 42 - Application for notification (Antrag auf Notifizierung)

## Summary
Article 42 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") governs how a conformity
assessment body applies for notification: it applies to the notifying authority
of its Member State of establishment, describes its activities, procedures and
the products with digital elements it claims competence for, and attaches an
accreditation certificate attesting compliance with Art. 39 where applicable -
or, lacking one, all documentary evidence needed to verify and regularly monitor
that compliance. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus;
it carries no FAQ and no recitals. Only the Official Journal text is binding.
