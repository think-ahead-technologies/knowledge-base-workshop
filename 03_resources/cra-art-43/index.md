# CRA Art. 43 - Notification procedure (Notifizierungsverfahren)

## Summary
Article 43 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") defines the procedure by which a
notifying authority notifies a conformity assessment body to the Commission and the
other Member States: only bodies meeting Art. 39 may be notified, the notification goes
through the Commission's New Approach information system, it must detail activities,
modules, products and competence, extra evidence is needed without an accreditation
certificate (Art. 42(2)), and the body may only act as a notified body after an
objection-free period of two weeks (accredited) or two months (not accredited). The
captured page is the German text republished by Kunnus; it carries no FAQ, guidance or
recital list for this article. Only the Official Journal text is binding.
