# CRA notification procedure for conformity assessment bodies

Art. 43 sets out how a "notifizierende Behörde" (notifying authority) turns a
"Konformitätsbewertungsstelle" (conformity assessment body) into a
"notifizierte Stelle" (notified body) under the CRA:

1. **Eligibility** - only bodies meeting the requirements of Art. 39 may be notified (para. 1).
2. **Channel** - the authority notifies the Commission and the other Member States via the
   Commission's information system for bodies notified under the New Approach
   ("Informationssystem für die nach dem neuen Konzept notifizierten und benannten Organisationen", known as NANDO) (para. 2).
3. **Content** - full details of the conformity assessment activities, the conformity
   assessment module(s) ("Konformitätsbewertungsmodule"), the products with digital
   elements concerned, and the relevant attestation of competence
   ("Bestätigung der Kompetenz") (para. 3).
4. **No accreditation certificate** - if the notification is not based on an
   accreditation certificate ("Akkreditierungsurkunde") per Art. 42(2), the authority must
   supply documentary evidence of competence plus the arrangements ensuring the body is
   monitored regularly and keeps meeting Art. 39 (para. 4).
5. **Objection period** - the body may act as a notified body only if neither the Commission
   nor other Member States object within **two weeks** of notification (with accreditation
   certificate) or **two months** (without accreditation). Only such a body counts as a
   notified body for the purposes of the regulation (para. 5).
6. **Changes** - the Commission and other Member States are informed of all later relevant
   changes to the notification (para. 6).

Practical consequence (inferred, not stated on the page): accredited bodies get a much
shorter path to notified-body status than non-accredited ones.
