# CRA Art. 44 - Identification numbers and lists of notified bodies (Kennnummern und Verzeichnisse notifizierter Stellen)

## Summary
Article 44 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") has two paragraphs. (1) The
Commission assigns each notified body ("notifizierte Stelle") an identification
number ("Kennnummer"); a body notified under several Union legal acts still gets
only one single number. (2) The Commission publishes the list ("Verzeichnis") of
bodies notified under the CRA, with their numbers and the activities they are
notified for, and keeps it "stets auf dem neuesten Stand". The captured page is
the German text as republished by Kunnus; it carries no FAQ or recital list for
this article. Only the Official Journal text is binding.
