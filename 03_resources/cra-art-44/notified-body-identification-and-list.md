# Notified body identification number and list

Art. 44 CRA makes the Commission the registrar of notified bodies:

- **One identification number per body** - "Die Kommission weist jeder
  notifizierten Stelle eine Kennnummer zu." Even when a body is notified under
  several Union legal acts ("gemäß mehreren Rechtsakten der Union"), it gets
  "nur eine einzige Kennnummer". The number is thus body-wide, not per
  regulation.
- **Public, current list** - the Commission publishes "das Verzeichnis der nach
  dieser Verordnung notifizierten Stellen" with their numbers and the
  activities ("Tätigkeiten") for which they were notified, and keeps it always
  up to date.

Takeaway for manufacturers: to check whether a conformity assessment body may
act under the CRA, and for which activities, consult the Commission's list; the
body's single identification number identifies it across all EU harmonisation
acts. (Inferred, not stated in the article: in practice this list is the
Commission's NANDO database.)
