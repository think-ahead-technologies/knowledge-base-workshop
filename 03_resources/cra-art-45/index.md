# CRA Art. 45 - Changes to notifications

## Summary
Article 45 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847), Chapter IV
("Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen"). Paragraph 1 obliges the
notifying authority to restrict, suspend or withdraw the notification of a notified
body that no longer meets the requirements of Art. 39 or fails its obligations,
proportionate to the extent of the shortfall, and to inform the Commission and the
other Member States without delay. Paragraph 2 obliges the notifying Member State,
on restriction, suspension or withdrawal or when the body ceases its activity, to
ensure the body's files are processed by another notified body or kept available to
notifying and market surveillance authorities on request.
