# Restriction, suspension and withdrawal of a notified body's notification

Under CRA Art. 45(1), when a notifying authority ("notifizierende Behörde") finds,
or is informed, that a notified body ("notifizierte Stelle") no longer meets the
requirements of Art. 39 or fails to fulfil its obligations, it restricts, suspends
or withdraws the notification as appropriate ("schränkt sie die Notifizierung
gegebenenfalls ein, setzt sie aus oder widerruft sie"). The measure is proportionate:
it takes into account the extent of the non-compliance. The authority must inform
the Commission and the other Member States without delay ("unverzüglich").

Art. 45(2) secures continuity of the body's work. In case of restriction, suspension
or withdrawal ("Einschränkung, Aussetzung oder Aufhebung") of the notification, or
when the notified body ceases its activity, the notifying Member State takes
appropriate measures so that the body's files ("Akten") are either processed further
by another notified body or kept available, on request, for the competent notifying
authorities and market surveillance authorities ("Marktüberwachungsbehörden").

Practical reading (inferred, not stated in the text): manufacturers relying on a
notified body for third-party conformity assessment should expect that their
assessment files may be transferred to another notified body if the original body
loses or limits its notification.
