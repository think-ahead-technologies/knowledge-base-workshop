# CRA Art. 46 - Challenge to the competence of notified bodies (Anfechtung der Kompetenz notifizierter Stellen)

## Summary
Article 46 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") sets out how the European
Commission investigates doubts about a notified body's competence or its
continued compliance with its requirements: the notifying Member State must
supply information on request, sensitive information stays confidential, and if
the body falls short the Commission asks the Member State to take corrective
measures, up to withdrawing the notification. The captured page is the German
text as republished by Kunnus; it carries no FAQ and no recitals list. Only the
Official Journal text is binding.
