# Commission challenge of notified body competence

Art. 46 CRA gives the European Commission a Union-level check on notified
bodies ("notifizierte Stellen"), on top of the national oversight by the
notifying Member State ("notifizierender Mitgliedstaat"). The procedure:

1. **Trigger and investigation (para. 1)** - the Commission investigates every
   case where it doubts, or is made aware of doubts about, either the competence
   ("Kompetenz") of a notified body or its continued fulfilment of the
   requirements and obligations that apply to it ("dauerhafte Erfüllung der für
   die Stelle geltenden Anforderungen und Pflichten"). Doubts can come from the
   Commission itself or from third parties.
2. **Duty to inform (para. 2)** - on request, the notifying Member State gives
   the Commission all information on the basis of the notification ("Grundlage
   der Notifizierung") or on how the body's competence is maintained
   ("Erhaltung der Kompetenz").
3. **Confidentiality (para. 3)** - the Commission ensures that all sensitive
   information obtained during its investigations is treated confidentially.
4. **Outcome (para. 4)** - if the Commission finds that the body does not, or no
   longer, meets the requirements for its notification, it informs the
   notifying Member State and requests it to take the necessary corrective
   measures ("Korrekturmaßnahmen"), including withdrawal of the notification
   ("Widerruf der Notifizierung") if necessary.

Note: the Commission does not itself withdraw the notification - it asks the
notifying Member State to act. For manufacturers this matters because a
conformity assessment relies on the notified body staying validly notified.
