# CRA Art. 47 - Operational obligations of notified bodies (Operative Pflichten der notifizierten Stellen)

## Summary
Article 47 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") sets how notified bodies
("notifizierte Stellen") operate. They perform conformity assessments under
Art. 32 and Annex VIII (para. 1), proportionately and without unnecessary burden
on economic operators, taking account of company size (notably micro/SMEs),
sector, structure, complexity, product cybersecurity risk and mass/series
production (para. 2), yet strictly enough to secure compliance (para. 3). If a
manufacturer fails Annex I or harmonised standards / common specifications under
Art. 27, no certificate is issued and corrective measures are requested
(para. 4); if a certified product no longer complies, the certificate may be
suspended or withdrawn (para. 5); without effective corrective measures, the
body restricts, suspends or withdraws certificates (para. 6). The captured page
is the German text as republished by Kunnus; only the Official Journal text is
binding.
