# CRA notified body certificate actions - refuse, restrict, suspend, withdraw

Art. 47(4)-(6) set the escalation path a notified body ("notifizierte Stelle")
follows when a manufacturer falls short of the CRA requirements:

1. **Before certification** (para. 4) - if the manufacturer does not meet the
   requirements of Annex I or of the relevant harmonised standards or common
   specifications under Art. 27, the body asks for "angemessene
   Korrekturmaßnahmen" (appropriate corrective measures) and issues no
   "Konformitätsbescheinigung" (certificate of conformity).
2. **After certification, during monitoring** (para. 5) - if, "im Rahmen der
   Überwachung der Konformität", the product no longer meets the requirements,
   the body asks for corrective measures and, where necessary, suspends or
   withdraws the certificate.
3. **Corrective measures missing or ineffective** (para. 6) - the body
   restricts, suspends or withdraws the certificates ("schränkt ... ein, setzt
   ... aus bzw. widerruft"), whichever is appropriate.

For a manufacturer this means a certificate is not permanent: ongoing
conformity monitoring can lead to its restriction, suspension or withdrawal.
