# CRA proportionate conformity assessment by notified bodies

Art. 47(1)-(3) define how notified bodies carry out conformity assessments
("Konformitätsbewertungen"):

- **Procedure** (para. 1) - in line with the conformity assessment procedures
  of Art. 32 and Annex VIII.
- **Proportionality** (para. 2) - "unter Wahrung der Verhältnismäßigkeit",
  avoiding unnecessary burdens on economic operators ("Wirtschaftsakteure").
  Bodies take due account of: company size (especially micro, small and
  medium-sized enterprises, "Kleinstunternehmen sowie kleine und mittlere
  Unternehmen"), sector, structure, degree of complexity and cybersecurity
  risk of the products with digital elements and technologies concerned, and
  whether production is mass or series manufacturing
  ("Massenfertigungs- oder Seriencharakter").
- **Rigour floor** (para. 3) - proportionality does not lower the bar: bodies
  must act as strictly ("so streng") and keep the level of protection needed
  for the products to comply with the regulation.
