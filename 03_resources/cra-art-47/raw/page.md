**Article 47: Operational obligations of notified bodies**

1. **Procedures:** Notified bodies carry out conformity assessments under Article 32 and Annex VIII.
2. **Proportionality:** Assessments should avoid unnecessary burdens on economic operators and account for company size (including micro and small/medium enterprises), sector, structure, complexity, cybersecurity risk, and whether production is mass or series manufacturing.
3. **Strictness:** Despite proportionality, notified bodies must act rigorously and maintain the level of protection needed for products to comply with the Regulation. The page quotes the German phrase "Die notifizierten Stellen gehen hierbei jedoch so streng vor" for this point.
4. **Non-compliance before certification:** If a body finds that a manufacturer does not meet the requirements of Annex I or the relevant harmonised standards, it asks the manufacturer to take corrective measures and does not issue a certificate.
5. **Non-compliance after certification:** If a certified product no longer meets the requirements during surveillance, the body asks for corrective measures and suspends or withdraws the certificate where necessary.
6. **Escalation:** If corrective measures are absent or ineffective, the body restricts, suspends, or withdraws certificates, as appropriate.
