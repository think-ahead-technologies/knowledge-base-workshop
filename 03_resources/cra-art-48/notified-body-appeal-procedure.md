# Appeal procedure against notified body decisions

Under CRA Art. 48 the Member States must ensure that an appeal procedure
("Einspruchsverfahren") exists against decisions of notified bodies
("Entscheidungen der notifizierten Stellen"). The obligation sits with the
Member States, not with the notified bodies or economic operators: the article
does not define the form, deadlines or competent instance of the procedure,
leaving that to national law. In practice it gives a party affected by a
notified body decision (typically the manufacturer whose product underwent
third-party conformity assessment) a route to contest it.

Verbatim legal text (German): "Die Mitgliedstaaten stellen sicher, dass ein
Einspruchsverfahren gegen die Entscheidungen der notifizierten Stellen
vorgesehen ist."

The note on who is typically affected is an interpretation, not part of the
legal text.
