# CRA Art. 49 - Information obligations on notified bodies (Meldepflichten der notifizierten Stellen)

## Summary
Article 49 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") obliges notified bodies to
inform the notifying authority of certificate refusals, restrictions,
suspensions and withdrawals, of changes affecting their notification, of
information requests from market surveillance authorities and, on request, of
their activities (incl. cross-border work and subcontracting). It also obliges
them to share negative (and on request positive) conformity assessment results
with other notified bodies assessing the same products with digital elements.
The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus; it carries no
FAQ or recitals list for this article. Only the Official Journal text is
binding.
