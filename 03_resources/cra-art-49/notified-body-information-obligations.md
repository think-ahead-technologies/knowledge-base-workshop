# Notified body information obligations (Meldepflichten der notifizierten Stellen)

CRA Art. 49 sets two reporting duties for "notifizierte Stellen" (notified
conformity assessment bodies).

**Towards the notifying authority ("notifizierende Behörde"), para. 1.** A
notified body must report:

- (a) every refusal, restriction, suspension and withdrawal of a certificate
  ("alle Verweigerungen, Einschränkungen, Aussetzungen und Aufhebung einer
  Bescheinigung");
- (b) every circumstance affecting the scope and conditions of its
  notification ("Geltungsbereich und die Bedingungen der Notifizierung");
- (c) every request for information about conformity assessment activities it
  has received from market surveillance authorities
  ("Marktüberwachungsbehörden");
- (d) on request, the conformity assessment activities performed within the
  scope of its notification and any other activities, including cross-border
  activities and subcontracting ("grenzüberschreitender Tätigkeiten und Vergabe
  von Unteraufträgen").

**Towards other notified bodies, para. 2.** A notified body must give other
bodies notified under the CRA that perform similar conformity assessment
activities for the same "Produkte mit digitalen Elementen" relevant information
on negative conformity assessment results, and on positive results on request.

Practical effect: a certificate refused or withdrawn by one notified body
becomes known to the notifying authority and to peer bodies assessing the same
products.
