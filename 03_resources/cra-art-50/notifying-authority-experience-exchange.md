# Exchange of experience between notifying authorities

Art. 50 CRA ("Erfahrungsaustausch") makes the European Commission responsible
for organising an exchange of experience among the national authorities of the
Member States that are responsible for notification policy. Full German text
as captured:

> "Die Kommission organisiert den Erfahrungsaustausch zwischen den für die
> Notifizierungspolitik zuständigen nationalen Behörden der Mitgliedstaaten."

Key points:

- **Who organises:** "Die Kommission" (the European Commission).
- **Who takes part:** "die für die Notifizierungspolitik zuständigen nationalen
  Behörden der Mitgliedstaaten" - the national authorities in charge of
  notification policy, i.e. the bodies that designate, notify and supervise
  conformity assessment bodies under Chapter IV ("Notifizierung von
  Konformitätsbewertungsstellen").
- **Purpose (implied by placement, not stated in the text):** consistent
  practice across Member States in notifying and monitoring notified bodies.
  The article sets no format, frequency or output for the exchange.
- It is the authority-level counterpart to Art. 51 (coordination of the
  notified bodies themselves, per the page's "next article" link).
