# CRA Art. 51 - Coordination of notified bodies (Koordinierung der notifizierten Stellen)

## Summary
Article 51 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV
"Notifizierung von Konformitätsbewertungsstellen") has two paragraphs: the
Commission ensures coordination and cooperation between notified bodies via a
cross-sectoral group of notified bodies ("sektorübergreifende Gruppe
notifizierter Stellen"), and Member States ensure their notified bodies take
part in that group directly or through designated representatives
("benannte Vertreter"). The captured page is the German text as republished by
Kunnus (last reviewed by Kunnus March 2026); it carries no FAQ and no recitals
for this article. Only the Official Journal text is binding.
