# CRA cross-sectoral group of notified bodies

Art. 51 CRA (Chapter IV, notification of conformity assessment bodies) sets up
the coordination mechanism among notified bodies ("notifizierte Stellen"):

- Para. 1 - the Commission ensures that appropriate coordination and
  cooperation between notified bodies is put in place and properly operated
  "in Form einer sektorübergreifenden Gruppe notifizierter Stellen"
  (a cross-sectoral group of notified bodies).
- Para. 2 - Member States ensure that the bodies they notified take part in
  the work of this group, "direkt oder über benannte Vertreter" (directly or
  through designated representatives).

So participation is an obligation the notifying Member State has to secure;
the Commission is responsible for establishing and running the group. The
purpose is consistent application of conformity assessment across sectors.
