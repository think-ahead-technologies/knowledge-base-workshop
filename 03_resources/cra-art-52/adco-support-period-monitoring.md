# ADCO and support-period monitoring

**ADCO (para 15).** For uniform application of the CRA, an ADCO (administrative
cooperation group) is set up under Art. 30(2) of Regulation (EU) 2019/1020. It is
made up of representatives of the designated market surveillance authorities and,
where appropriate, of the single liaison offices ("zentrale Verbindungsstellen").
It also handles specific questions on surveillance of open-source steward
obligations.

**Support-period monitoring (para 16).** Market surveillance authorities monitor
how manufacturers applied the criteria of Art. 13(8) when setting the
"Unterstützungszeitraum" (support period) of their products. The ADCO:

- publishes, publicly and in user-friendly form, statistics per product category,
  including the average support periods manufacturers set under Art. 13(8);
- provides guidance with indicative support periods per product category;
- may recommend that authorities focus on categories where the data indicates
  insufficient support periods.

Takeaway for manufacturers: a chosen support period becomes comparable against
category averages and ADCO indicative periods; a short one in a category flagged
as under-supported is a likely surveillance focus.
