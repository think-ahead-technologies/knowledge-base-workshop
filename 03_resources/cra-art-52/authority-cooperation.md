# Cooperation network of CRA market surveillance authorities

Art. 52 ties the CRA market surveillance authorities into a web of other bodies,
each with "regelmäßig Informationen austauschen" duties where appropriate:

- **Cybersecurity certification authorities (para 4)** - national authorities
  designated under Art. 58 of Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Cybersecurity Act).
- **CSIRTs and ENISA (paras 4, 5)** - when supervising the reporting obligations
  of Art. 14, authorities cooperate with the "als Koordinatoren benannten CSIRTs"
  and ENISA. They may ask the coordinator CSIRT or ENISA for technical advice on
  applying and enforcing the CRA, and, in an investigation under Art. 54, for an
  analysis supporting the conformity assessment of a product.
- **Other market surveillance authorities (para 6)** - those designated under
  other Union harmonisation legislation for other products.
- **Data protection authorities (para 7)** - authorities inform them of relevant
  findings, including guidance and advice under para 10 that concerns personal
  data processing. Data protection authorities may request and access any
  documentation drawn up or kept under the CRA when needed for their tasks, and
  must notify the designated market surveillance authority of each such request.
- **AI Act authorities (para 14)** - for products in CRA scope that are high-risk
  AI systems under Art. 6 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, the AI Act market
  surveillance authorities are also responsible for CRA market surveillance. They
  cooperate with the CRA authorities and, for Art. 14 reporting supervision, with
  the coordinator CSIRTs and ENISA, and inform CRA authorities of relevant
  findings.

Takeaway: documentation produced for the CRA can reach data protection
authorities; high-risk AI products face a single surveillance authority (the AI
Act one) for both regimes.
