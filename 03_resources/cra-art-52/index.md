# CRA Art. 52 - Market surveillance and control of products with digital elements in the Union market (Marktüberwachung und Kontrolle von Produkten mit digitalen Elementen auf dem Unionsmarkt)

## Summary
Article 52 opens Chapter V ("Marktüberwachung und Durchsetzung") of the EU Cyber
Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847). It plugs the CRA into the general EU
market surveillance framework (Regulation (EU) 2019/1020), obliges each Member
State to designate one or more "Marktüberwachungsbehörden", and sets out their
extra CRA duties: supervising open-source software stewards ("Verwalter
quelloffener Software", Art. 24), cooperating with certification authorities,
CSIRTs, ENISA, data protection and AI Act authorities, informing consumers and
operators, annual reporting to the Commission, and monitoring how manufacturers
set support periods ("Unterstützungszeitraum", Art. 13(8)). It also sets up an
ADCO (administrative cooperation group) for uniform application. The captured
page is the German text (16 paragraphs) as republished by Kunnus, with a list of
5 related recitals (107-111); the page has no article FAQ. Only the Official
Journal text is binding.
