# Market surveillance of open-source software stewards

The authorities designated under Art. 52(2) are also responsible for market
surveillance of the obligations that Art. 24 places on "Verwalter quelloffener
Software" (open-source software stewards) (para 3).

Enforcement is deliberately light: if an authority finds a steward not meeting
its Art. 24 obligations, it "fordert ... auf" - it requests the steward to ensure
that all appropriate corrective measures ("geeigneten Korrekturmaßnahmen") are
taken; the steward must then ensure this within its CRA obligations. Para 3 names
no other measure against stewards.

Para 15 adds that the CRA ADCO also deals with specific questions on market
surveillance of open-source steward obligations, giving a Union-level forum for
consistent treatment.
