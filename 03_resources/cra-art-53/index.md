# CRA Art. 53 - Access to data and documentation (Zugang zu Daten und zur Dokumentation)

## Summary
Article 53 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter V
"Marktüberwachung und Durchsetzung") lets market surveillance authorities, on a
reasoned request ("auf begründeten Antrag"), obtain access to the data needed to
assess the design, development, production and vulnerability handling of products
with digital elements, including the relevant internal documents of the economic
operator ("Wirtschaftsakteur"), in a language easily understood by the authority.
Access is limited to what is necessary to assess conformity of the products and
of the manufacturer's procedures with the essential cybersecurity requirements of
Annex I. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus, plus one
related recital (112); only the Official Journal text is binding.
