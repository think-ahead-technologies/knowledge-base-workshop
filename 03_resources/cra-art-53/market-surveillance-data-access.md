# Market surveillance authority access to data and internal documentation

Art. 53 CRA gives market surveillance authorities ("Marktüberwachungsbehörden")
a right of access to data held by economic operators. Elements of the rule:

- **Trigger** - a reasoned request ("auf begründeten Antrag"); access is not
  automatic or unconditional.
- **Purpose limit** - only "soweit dies ... erforderlich ist" to assess the
  conformity of products with digital elements and of the manufacturer's
  procedures with the essential cybersecurity requirements in Annex I.
- **Scope of data** - data needed to assess "Konzeption, Entwicklung, Herstellung
  und die Behandlung von Schwachstellen" (design, development, production and
  vulnerability handling) of the product, explicitly "einschließlich der
  betreffenden internen Unterlagen des betroffenen Wirtschaftsakteurs" (including
  the relevant internal documents of the economic operator concerned).
- **Language** - provided "in einer für sie leicht verständlichen Sprache", a
  language easily understood by the authority.

Practical takeaway: manufacturers should keep design, development, production and
vulnerability-handling records (not only the formal technical documentation) in a
form they can hand over and translate on request. This takeaway is an
interpretation, not legal text.
