**Article 53 CRA: Access to data and documentation**

- **Location:** Chapter V (Market surveillance and enforcement) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, published 10 December 2024. The page was last checked by Kunnus in March 2026.
- **Core rule:** On a reasoned request, market surveillance authorities can obtain data needed to assess a product's design, development, production, and vulnerability handling. This includes relevant internal documentation of the economic operator. The access must be in a language the authority can easily understand.
- **Condition:** It applies only where needed to assess whether products with digital elements, and the manufacturer's procedures, meet the essential cybersecurity requirements in Annex I.
- **Related recitals:** One recital (number 112) is listed. It concerns products that pose a significant cybersecurity risk or may not comply with the regulation.
- **Navigation:** The previous article is Article 52 (market surveillance and control of products with digital elements). The next is Article 54 (national procedures for such products).
