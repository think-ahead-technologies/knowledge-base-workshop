# National procedure for products with a significant cybersecurity risk

Art. 54 CRA defines what a national market surveillance authority does when it
has "hinreichenden Grund zu der Annahme" that a product with digital elements,
"einschließlich der Behandlung von Schwachstellen", presents an "erhebliches
Cybersicherheitsrisiko":

1. **Evaluation (para 1)** - the authority carries out, without delay, a
   conformity evaluation ("Konformitätsbewertung") against the CRA requirements,
   where appropriate together with the relevant CSIRT. Affected economic
   operators ("Wirtschaftsakteure") must cooperate as necessary.
2. **Corrective action (para 1)** - if the product does not comply, the authority
   requires the operator to take all appropriate "Korrekturmaßnahmen" within a
   deadline proportionate to the nature of the risk: bring it into conformity,
   withdraw it ("vom Markt nehmen") or recall it ("zurückrufen"). The relevant
   notified body is informed. Art. 18 of Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 applies.
3. **Cross-border (para 3)** - if the non-conformity is not limited to its
   territory, the authority informs the Commission and the other Member States
   of the results and the measures it required.
4. **Operator scope (para 4)** - corrective action must cover all affected
   products the operator made available on the Union market.
5. **Provisional measures (para 5)** - if the operator does not act within the
   deadline, authorities take "vorläufige Maßnahmen" to prohibit or restrict
   making the product available nationally, withdraw or recall it, and notify
   the Commission and other Member States without delay.
6. **Notification content (para 6)** - product identification data, origin,
   nature of the non-conformity and risk, nature and duration of the national
   measure, the operator's arguments, and whether the cause is (a) failure of the
   product or manufacturer processes to meet Annex I essential requirements, or
   (b) shortcomings in harmonised standards, European cybersecurity
   certification schemes or common specifications (Art. 27).
7. **Other Member States (para 7)** - report their own measures, additional
   information and any objections to the notified measure.
8. **Deemed justified (para 8)** - if neither a Member State nor the Commission
   objects within three months of the notification, the provisional measure is
   deemed justified ("gilt diese Maßnahme als gerechtfertigt"); the operator's
   procedural rights under Art. 18 Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 are unaffected.
9. **EU-wide follow-up (para 9)** - all Member States' authorities ensure
   appropriate restrictive measures without delay, e.g. withdrawal from their
   market.
