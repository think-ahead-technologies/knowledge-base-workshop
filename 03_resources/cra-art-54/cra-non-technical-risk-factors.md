# Non-technical risk factors in CRA market surveillance

Art. 54(2) CRA requires market surveillance authorities, when determining
whether a cybersecurity risk is significant under para 1, to also take into
account "nichttechnische Risikofaktoren" - in particular those established by
Union-level coordinated security risk assessments of supply chains
("koordinierte Risikobewertungen in Bezug auf die Sicherheit der Lieferketten auf
Unionsebene") under Art. 22 of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2).

If an authority has sufficient reason to believe a product presents a
significant cybersecurity risk because of non-technical risk factors, it informs
the competent authorities designated or established under Art. 8 NIS2 and
cooperates with them as necessary.

So a product can be treated as presenting a significant risk not only for
technical flaws but also for supply-chain or origin-related factors identified
at EU level.

## Mentions
- No other CRA article or annex is referenced in para 2.
- External: Arts. 8 and 22 Directive (EU) 2022/2555
