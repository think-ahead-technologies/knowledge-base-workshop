# CRA Art. 54 - National procedure for products presenting a significant cybersecurity risk

## Summary
Article 54 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter V
"Marktüberwachung und Durchsetzung") sets the national procedure a market
surveillance authority ("Marktüberwachungsbehörde") follows when it has
sufficient reason to believe a product with digital elements, including its
vulnerability handling, presents a "erhebliches Cybersicherheitsrisiko". It
covers the conformity evaluation (optionally with the CSIRT), corrective-action
demands with a deadline, the use of non-technical risk factors, notification of
the Commission and other Member States, provisional national measures
("vorläufige Maßnahmen"), a three-month objection window after which a measure
is deemed justified, and EU-wide follow-up restrictive measures. The captured
page is the German text as republished by Kunnus (9 paragraphs, 2 related
recitals); only the Official Journal text is binding.
