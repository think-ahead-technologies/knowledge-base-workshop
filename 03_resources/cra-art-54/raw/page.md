**Artikel 54: Nationale Verfahren für Produkte mit digitalen Elementen, die ein erhebliches Cybersicherheitsrisiko bergen**

*Kapitel V, Marktüberwachung und Durchsetzung. Verordnung (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act).*

**Summary of paragraphs**

1. **Assessment and corrective action:** If a national market surveillance authority has good reason to think a product with digital elements poses a significant cybersecurity risk, it must promptly assess the product's conformity, with the relevant CSIRT where needed. If the product is non-compliant, the operator must take corrective action within a deadline, or the product must be withdrawn or recalled. Article 18 of Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 applies.
2. **Non-technical risk factors:** Authorities may consider non-technical factors, such as coordinated EU-level supply chain risk assessments under Article 22 of NIS2 (Directive (EU) 2022/2555). They inform the competent authorities designated under NIS2 Article 8.
3. **Cross-border cases:** If the non-compliance is not limited to one member state, the authority informs the Commission and the other member states.
4. **Scope of operator duties:** The economic operator must ensure corrective measures cover all affected products it has placed on the EU market.
5. **Provisional measures:** If the operator fails to act adequately, authorities may ban or restrict the product, withdraw it, or recall it, and must notify the Commission and other states without delay.
6. **Notification content:** Notices must identify the product and its origin, describe the non-compliance and risk, and record the operator's arguments. They must also state whether the cause is (a) failure to meet the Annex I essential requirements or the manufacturer's processes, or (b) deficiencies in harmonised standards, certification schemes, or common specifications under Article 27.
7. **Information sharing:** Other member states' authorities report to the Commission and other states any further measures or evidence, and any objections to a notified national measure.
8. **Deemed justification:** If no member state or the Commission objects within three months, the provisional measure is considered justified. The operator's procedural rights under Article 18 of Regulation 2019/1020 remain unaffected.
9. **Effective restrictions across the EU:** All member states' authorities must promptly take appropriate restrictive measures for the product, such as withdrawing it from their markets.

**Related recitals (previews only):** 113 (joint activities where non-compliance appears in several member states) and 114 (coordinated sweeps).
