# CRA Art. 55 - Union safeguard procedure (Schutzklauselverfahren der Union)

## Summary
Article 55 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter V
"Marktüberwachung und Durchsetzung") sets the Union safeguard procedure: when a
Member State objects within three months to another Member State's national
measure notified under Art. 54(5), or the Commission deems it incompatible with
Union law, the Commission consults, evaluates and decides within nine months
whether the measure is justified. If justified, every Member State removes the
non-compliant product from its market; if not, the measure is withdrawn. Where
the non-compliance traces back to harmonised standards, an Art. 27 certification
scheme or Art. 27 common specifications, the Commission reviews that instrument.
The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus, with one related
recital; only the Official Journal text is binding.
