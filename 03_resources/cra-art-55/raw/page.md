# EU CRA Article 55 - Union safeguard procedure (Schutzklauselverfahren der Union)

- **Paragraph 1:** If a Member State objects to another Member State's national measure within three months of the notification under Article 54(5), or the Commission considers the measure incompatible with EU law, the Commission consults the parties and examines the measure. Within nine months, it decides whether the measure is justified.
- **Paragraph 2:** If the measure is justified, all Member States must remove the non-compliant product with digital elements from their markets. If it is not justified, the Member State that adopted it must withdraw it.
- **Paragraph 3:** If the non-compliance stems from deficiencies in harmonised standards, the Commission initiates the procedure under Article 11 of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012.
- **Paragraph 4:** If the cause is deficiencies in a European cybersecurity certification scheme under Article 27, the Commission reviews the delegated act that sets out the presumption of conformity for that scheme.
- **Paragraph 5:** If the cause is deficiencies in common specifications under Article 27, the Commission reviews the implementing act that lays them down.

The related recital (115) concerns ENISA's role in supporting implementation. The adjacent articles are Article 54 (national procedures) and Article 56 (Union-level procedure for products with digital elements).
