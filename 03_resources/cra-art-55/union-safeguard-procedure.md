# CRA Union safeguard procedure

Art. 55 CRA ("Schutzklauselverfahren der Union") is the EU-level review of a
national market-surveillance measure against a non-compliant product with
digital elements, triggered after the notification under Art. 54(5).

**Trigger (para. 1).** Either a Member State raises objections ("Einwände")
within **three months** of receiving the Art. 54(5) notification against a
measure taken by another Member State, or the Commission considers the measure
incompatible with Union law ("mit dem Unionsrecht unvereinbar").

**Procedure (para. 1).** The Commission without delay consults the Member State
or economic operator ("Wirtschaftsakteur") concerned and evaluates the national
measure. Within **nine months** of the Art. 54(5) notification it decides whether
the measure is justified ("gerechtfertigt") and informs the Member State.

**Outcome (para. 2).**
- Justified: all Member States take the necessary measures to ensure the
  non-compliant product is withdrawn from their market ("von ihrem Markt
  genommen wird") and inform the Commission.
- Not justified: the Member State concerned must withdraw its measure
  ("zurücknehmen").

**Root cause in the presumption-of-conformity instruments (paras. 3-5).** If the
measure is justified and non-compliance is attributed to shortcomings
("Mängel") in:
- harmonised standards ("harmonisierte Normen") - the Commission starts the
  procedure of Art. 11 of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 (formal objection to
  standards);
- a European cybersecurity certification scheme under Art. 27 - the Commission
  assesses whether to amend or repeal the delegated act under Art. 27(9) that
  set the presumption of conformity for that scheme;
- common specifications ("gemeinsame Spezifikationen") under Art. 27 - the
  Commission assesses whether to amend or repeal the implementing act under
  Art. 27(2) that laid them down.

So the procedure both settles the market fate of one product EU-wide and feeds
back into the standards / certification / common-specification instruments that
grant presumption of conformity.
