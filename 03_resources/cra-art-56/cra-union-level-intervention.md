# Commission Union-level intervention for significant-risk products

Art. 56 CRA lets the European Commission act on a product with digital elements
that presents a significant cybersecurity risk ("erhebliches
Cybersicherheitsrisiko") and does not comply with the regulation.

- **Trigger (para. 1)** - the Commission has "hinreichenden Grund zu der Annahme"
  of non-compliance, also based on ENISA information. It informs the relevant
  market surveillance authorities. If those authorities carry out a conformity
  assessment, the national procedures of Art. 54 and 55 apply - the default path
  stays national.
- **Escalation (para. 3)** - only "unter Umständen, die ein sofortiges Eingreifen
  rechtfertigen, um das reibungslose Funktionieren des Binnenmarkts zu bewahren",
  when the product still does not comply and the market surveillance authorities
  have taken no effective measures ("keine wirksamen Maßnahmen"). The Commission
  then performs the conformity assessment itself, may ask ENISA for a supporting
  analysis, and informs the authorities. Affected economic operators
  ("Wirtschaftsakteure") must cooperate with ENISA as necessary.
- **Decision and consultation (para. 4)** - based on that assessment the
  Commission may find that a corrective or restrictive measure ("Korrekturmaßnahme
  oder eine einschränkende Maßnahme auf Unionsebene") is needed, and consults the
  affected Member States and economic operators without delay.
- **Implementing acts (para. 5)** - after consultation the Commission may adopt
  implementing acts ("Durchführungsrechtsakte") on such measures, including
  requiring withdrawal ("Rücknahme vom Markt") or recall ("Rückruf") within a
  period proportionate to the nature of the risk; adopted under the examination
  procedure of Art. 62(2).
- **Execution (para. 6)** - the Commission informs the operators without delay;
  Member States implement the acts without delay and report back.
- **Duration (para. 7)** - paras. 3-6 apply only for the duration of the
  exceptional circumstances and as long as the product has not been brought into
  conformity.
