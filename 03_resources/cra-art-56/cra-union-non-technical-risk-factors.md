# Union-level handling of non-technical risk factors

Art. 56(2) CRA covers the case where the Commission has sufficient reason to
believe a product with digital elements presents a significant cybersecurity
risk "angesichts nichttechnischer Risikofaktoren" (because of non-technical risk
factors - e.g. who supplies or controls the product, rather than a technical
defect; that example is an interpretation, the article does not define the term).

The Commission then:

- informs the relevant market surveillance authorities and, where appropriate,
  the competent authorities designated or established under Art. 8 of Directive
  (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2), and cooperates with them as needed;
- examines the relevance of the identified risks for its tasks on the
  coordinated Union-level risk assessments of supply-chain security
  ("koordinierten Risikobewertungen in Bezug auf die Sicherheit der Lieferketten
  auf Unionsebene") under Art. 22 NIS2;
- consults, where necessary, the Cooperation Group ("Kooperationsgruppe") set up
  under Art. 14 NIS2 and ENISA.

Unlike para. 1, para. 2 does not route the case into the national Art. 54/55
procedures; it ties the CRA to the NIS2 supply-chain risk machinery.

## Mentions
- Directive (EU) 2022/2555 Art. 8, Art. 14, Art. 22 (NIS2, outside the CRA)
- No other CRA article is referenced in para. 2.
