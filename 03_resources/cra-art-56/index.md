# CRA Art. 56 - Union-level procedure for products with digital elements presenting a significant cybersecurity risk

## Summary
Article 56 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter V
"Marktüberwachung und Durchsetzung") gives the European Commission its own role
when a product with digital elements presents a significant cybersecurity risk
("erhebliches Cybersicherheitsrisiko"). Para. 1: if the Commission (also based on
ENISA information) suspects non-compliance, it informs the market surveillance
authorities, and the national procedures of Art. 54 and 55 apply. Para. 2: for
risks from non-technical risk factors ("nichttechnischer Risikofaktoren") it
involves NIS2 competent authorities and links to Union-level supply-chain risk
assessments. Paras. 3-7: in exceptional circumstances where national authorities
have not acted effectively, the Commission itself assesses conformity (with ENISA
analysis) and may adopt implementing acts ordering corrective or restrictive
measures, including withdrawal ("Rücknahme") or recall ("Rückruf"), at Union level.
The page is the German text republished by Kunnus; it carries no article FAQ, only
a related-recital excerpt. Only the Official Journal text is binding.
