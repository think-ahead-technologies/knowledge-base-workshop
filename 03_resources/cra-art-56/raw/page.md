**Artikel 56 - Verfahren auf Unionsebene für Produkte mit digitalen Elementen, die ein erhebliche Cybersicherheitsrisiko bergen**
*Kapitel V - Marktüberwachung und Durchsetzung*
Source: Verordnung (EU) 2024/2847

**Summary of paragraphs (1)-(7):**

1. **Risk from technical deficiencies:** If the Commission has sufficient grounds to believe a product with digital elements poses a significant cybersecurity risk and doesn't comply with the Regulation, it informs the market surveillance authorities. Where those authorities assess the product, the procedures in Articles 54 and 55 apply.
2. **Non-technical risk factors:** If the risk stems from non-technical factors, the Commission informs the relevant market surveillance authorities and, where applicable, the competent authorities under the NIS2 Directive (EU 2022/2555). It cooperates with them as needed, checks the risk's relevance to supply-chain risk assessments, and consults the NIS Cooperation Group and ENISA.
3. **Urgent intervention:** In urgent cases affecting the proper functioning of the internal market, where national authorities have not acted effectively, the Commission may carry out a conformity assessment and ask ENISA for supporting analysis. Economic operators must cooperate with ENISA to the necessary extent.
4. **Corrective measures:** Based on that assessment, the Commission may determine that a Union-level corrective or restrictive measure is needed and must promptly consult the affected Member States and operators.
5. **Implementing acts:** After consultation, the Commission may adopt implementing acts ordering measures such as withdrawal from the market or recall within a period suited to the risk. The examination procedure in Article 62(2) applies.
6. **Notification and enforcement:** The Commission informs the affected operators without delay. Member States implement the acts without delay and report back to the Commission.
7. **Duration:** Paragraphs 3-6 apply for as long as the exceptional circumstances persist and the product has not been brought into conformity.
