# Commission Union-level intervention for compliant risky products (CRA Art. 57(6)-(11))

Art. 57(6)-(11) let the Commission act on compliant products that present the
risks of Art. 57(1), escalating in steps:

- **Trigger national action (para. 6)** - with "hinreichenden Grund zu der Annahme",
  including from ENISA information, the Commission informs the relevant market
  surveillance authorities and may ask them to carry out an evaluation and apply
  Art. 54 and Art. 57(1)-(3).
- **Own risk assessment (para. 7)** - where immediate intervention is justified to
  preserve the functioning of the internal market, the product still presents the
  risks and the authorities have not taken effective measures, the Commission
  assesses the risks itself, may ask ENISA for a supporting analysis, and informs the
  authorities. Affected operators cooperate with ENISA as necessary.
- **Union-level measure (paras. 8-9)** - based on that assessment and after
  consulting the Member States and operators concerned, the Commission may adopt
  implementing acts ("Durchführungsrechtsakte") on corrective or restrictive measures
  at Union level, including withdrawal or recall within a period proportionate to the
  risk, under the examination procedure of Art. 62(2).
- **Execution (para. 10)** - the Commission informs the operators; Member States
  implement the acts without delay and inform the Commission.
- **Time limit (para. 11)** - paras. 6-10 apply only for the duration of the
  exceptional circumstances ("außergewöhnlichen Umstände") and as long as the product
  continues to present the risks.
