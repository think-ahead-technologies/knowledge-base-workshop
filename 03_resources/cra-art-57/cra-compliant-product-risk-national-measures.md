# Compliant products with significant cybersecurity risk - national procedure (CRA Art. 57(1)-(5))

Conformity with the CRA does not shield a product from intervention. Under
Art. 57(1) a Member State's "Marktüberwachungsbehörde" requires an economic
operator ("Wirtschaftsakteur") to take "alle geeigneten Maßnahmen" when, after an
evaluation under Art. 54, it finds that a product with digital elements and the
manufacturer's procedures comply with the regulation but present an "erhebliches
Cybersicherheitsrisiko" together with a risk to:

- (a) health or safety of persons,
- (b) compliance with Union or national obligations protecting fundamental rights,
- (c) availability, authenticity, integrity or confidentiality of services offered
  via an electronic information system by essential entities under Art. 3(1) of
  Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2), or
- (d) other aspects of public-interest protection.

The measures may ensure the product and procedures no longer present the risk when
made available on the market, or may mean withdrawal ("vom Markt rückgenommen") or
recall ("zurückgerufen"); they must be proportionate to the nature of the risks.

- **Operator duty (para. 2)** - the manufacturer or other operators ensure
  "Korrekturmaßnahmen" for all affected products they made available in the Union,
  within the deadline the authority sets.
- **Notification (para. 3)** - the Member State informs the Commission and the other
  Member States without delay, including product identification data, origin and
  supply chain, nature of the risk, and nature and duration of national measures.
- **Commission review (paras. 4-5)** - the Commission consults the Member States and
  the operator, evaluates the national measure, decides whether it is justified,
  proposes appropriate measures where needed, and addresses the decision to the
  Member States.
