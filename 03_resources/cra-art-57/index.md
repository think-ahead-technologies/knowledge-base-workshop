# CRA Art. 57 - Compliant products with digital elements that present a significant cybersecurity risk (Konforme Produkte mit digitalen Elementen, die ein erhebliches Cybersicherheitsrisiko bergen)

## Summary
Article 57 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter V
"Marktüberwachung und Durchsetzung") covers products with digital elements that
*do* comply with the regulation but still present an "erhebliches
Cybersicherheitsrisiko" plus a further risk (health/safety, fundamental rights,
services of essential entities under NIS2, or other public interests). Paragraphs
1-5 set the national procedure: the market surveillance authority, after an
evaluation under Art. 54, orders the economic operator to take appropriate
measures, notifies the Commission and other Member States, and the Commission
decides whether the measure is justified. Paragraphs 6-11 give the Commission
(optionally informed or supported by ENISA) a Union-level route, up to
implementing acts ordering withdrawal or recall, limited to exceptional
circumstances. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus,
with one related recital (117, truncated); it has no vendor FAQ. Only the
Official Journal text is binding.
