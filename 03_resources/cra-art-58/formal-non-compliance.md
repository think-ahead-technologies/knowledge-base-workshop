# CRA formal non-compliance - six formal defects and escalation

Art. 58 CRA ("Formale Nichtkonformität") covers paperwork and marking defects,
as opposed to substantive cybersecurity failures. When the market surveillance
authority ("Marktüberwachungsbehörde") of a Member State finds one of the
following, it requires the manufacturer ("fordert sie den betroffenen
Hersteller auf") to remedy the non-compliance:

- **(a)** CE marking affixed in breach of Art. 29 and 30 ("unter Nichteinhaltung
  der Artikel 29 und 30 angebracht").
- **(b)** CE marking not affixed ("nicht angebracht").
- **(c)** EU declaration of conformity ("EU-Konformitätserklärung") not drawn up.
- **(d)** EU declaration of conformity not drawn up correctly ("nicht
  ordnungsgemäß ausgestellt").
- **(e)** Identification number of the notified body ("Kennnummer der ...
  notifizierten Stelle") involved in the conformity assessment, where
  applicable, not affixed.
- **(f)** Technical documentation ("technische Dokumentation") either not
  available or not complete.

**Escalation (para. 2):** if the non-compliance persists ("Besteht ... weiter"),
the Member State takes all appropriate measures to restrict or prohibit making
the product with digital elements available on the market, or to ensure it is
recalled ("zurückgerufen") or withdrawn ("vom Markt genommen").

Practical reading: the six items form a checklist of formal deliverables a
manufacturer must have in order - CE marking, EU declaration of conformity,
notified-body number where applicable, and complete technical documentation.
