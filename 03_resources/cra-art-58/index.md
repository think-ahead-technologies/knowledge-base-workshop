# CRA Art. 58 - Formal non-compliance (Formale Nichtkonformität)

## Summary
Article 58 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter V
"Marktüberwachung und Durchsetzung") deals with formal defects: a missing or
wrongly affixed CE marking, a missing or incorrect EU declaration of
conformity, a missing notified-body identification number, or unavailable or
incomplete technical documentation. The market surveillance authority must
require the manufacturer to remedy the defect; if it persists, the Member State
restricts or prohibits making the product available, or has it recalled or
withdrawn. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus; only
the Official Journal text is binding.
