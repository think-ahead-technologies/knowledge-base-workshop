# CRA Art. 59 - Joint activities of market surveillance authorities (Gemeinsame Tätigkeiten der Marktüberwachungsbehörden)

## Summary
Article 59 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter V
"Marktüberwachung und Durchsetzung") allows market surveillance authorities to
agree with other relevant authorities on joint activities ("gemeinsame
Tätigkeiten") for cybersecurity and consumer protection of specific products
with digital elements, especially those where cybersecurity risks are often
found. The Commission or ENISA propose joint compliance checks when products
may be non-compliant in several Member States; agreements must not cause unfair
competition or impair impartiality, information gathered may be used in the
authority's own investigation, and agreements (with participant names) are made
public. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus; it has
no FAQ or recitals section. Only the Official Journal text is binding.
