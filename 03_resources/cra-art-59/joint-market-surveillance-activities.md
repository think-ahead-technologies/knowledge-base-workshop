# CRA joint activities of market surveillance authorities

Art. 59 CRA lets market surveillance authorities ("Marktüberwachungsbehörden")
agree with other relevant authorities on joint activities ("gemeinsame
Tätigkeiten") to ensure cybersecurity and consumer protection for specific
products with digital elements placed on or made available on the market -
in particular products where cybersecurity risks are frequently found.

- **Initiative (para. 2):** the Commission or ENISA propose joint activities to
  check compliance, to be carried out by market surveillance authorities, based
  on indications or information that in-scope products may not comply with the
  regulation in several Member States.
- **Safeguards (para. 3):** authorities (and, where applicable, the Commission)
  ensure the agreement causes no unfair competition between economic operators
  ("Wirtschaftsakteure") and does not impair the objectivity, independence or
  impartiality of the parties.
- **Use of evidence (para. 4):** an authority may use all information obtained
  in joint activities that were part of an investigation it carried out.
- **Transparency (para. 5):** the agreement on joint activities, including the
  names of the participants, is made publicly available by the authority
  concerned and, where applicable, the Commission.

Practical relevance for manufacturers: products in categories with frequent
cybersecurity findings, or suspected non-compliance across several Member
States, can become the target of cross-border coordinated checks, and evidence
gathered there feeds national investigations.
