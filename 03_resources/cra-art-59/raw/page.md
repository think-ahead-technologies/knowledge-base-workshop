**Article 59: Joint activities of market surveillance authorities**
**Chapter V: Market surveillance and enforcement** (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Cyber Resilience Act)

1. Market surveillance authorities may agree with other relevant authorities to run joint activities that protect cybersecurity and consumers for products with digital elements. These activities may focus on product types where cybersecurity risks are often found. The quoted phrase is "gemeinsame Tätigkeiten zur Gewährleistung der Cybersicherheit und des Verbraucherschutzes."
2. The Commission or ENISA may propose joint compliance checks. Authorities carry them out when there are signs that in-scope products may not meet the Regulation's requirements in several Member States.
3. Authorities, and the Commission where relevant, must make sure these joint activities do not distort competition between economic operators or undermine the objectivity, independence, or impartiality of the parties.
4. An authority may use any information it obtains through joint activities that form part of its own investigation.
5. The authority concerned, and the Commission where relevant, must make the joint activity agreement publicly available, including the names of the participants.
