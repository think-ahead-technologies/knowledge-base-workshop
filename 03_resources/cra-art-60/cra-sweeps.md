# CRA coordinated controls (sweeps)

Art. 60 CRA establishes "gleichzeitige koordinierte Kontrollen („Sweeps“)" as an
enforcement instrument of market surveillance:

- **Purpose and object (para. 1)** - market surveillance authorities carry out
  sweeps on specific products with digital elements "zur Prüfung der Einhaltung
  dieser Verordnung oder zur Feststellung von Verstößen". Sweeps may include
  inspecting products "die unter einer falschen Identität erworben wurden"
  (mystery shopping / covert purchases).
- **Coordination (para. 2)** - unless the authorities agree otherwise, the
  Commission coordinates sweeps. The coordinator publishes aggregated results
  where appropriate ("gegebenenfalls").
- **ENISA proposals (para. 3)** - where ENISA, in performing its tasks (also based
  on notifications received under Art. 14(1) and (3)), identifies categories of
  products for which sweeps may be organised, it submits a sweep proposal to the
  coordinator for the authorities' consideration.
- **Powers (para. 4)** - participating authorities may use the investigative
  powers of Arts. 52 to 58 and further powers granted under national law.
- **Participants (para. 5)** - authorities may invite Commission officials and
  other persons authorised by the Commission to take part.

Practical reading for manufacturers (ingester interpretation, not legal text): Art. 14 vulnerability/incident reports can
feed into which product categories get swept, and products bought anonymously
on the open market may be tested.
