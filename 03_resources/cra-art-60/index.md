# CRA Art. 60 - Coordinated controls (sweeps) (Koordinierte Kontrollen (Sweeps))

## Summary
Article 60 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter V
"Marktüberwachung und Durchsetzung") obliges market surveillance authorities
("Marktüberwachungsbehörden") to carry out simultaneous coordinated controls
("Sweeps") on specific products with digital elements, to check compliance or
detect infringements - including inspection of products acquired under a false
identity ("unter einer falschen Identität erworben"). The Commission coordinates
sweeps unless the authorities agree otherwise; ENISA may propose product
categories for sweeps, partly based on Art. 14 notifications; authorities may use
their investigative powers under Arts. 52 to 58 and national law, and may invite
Commission officials. The captured page is the German text as republished by
Kunnus (no FAQ or recitals on this page); only the Official Journal text is
binding.
