# CRA delegated acts - delegation of power and objection procedure

Art. 61 sets the rules under which the European Commission may adopt delegated
acts ("delegierte Rechtsakte") that amend or supplement the CRA. It applies to
the empowerments in Art. 2(5) subpara. 2, Art. 7(3), Art. 8(1) and (2),
Art. 13(8) subpara. 4, Art. 14(9), Art. 25, Art. 27(9), Art. 28(5) and
Art. 31(5).

- **Duration** - the power is conferred for five years from 10 December 2024.
  The Commission reports on it no later than nine months before the period
  ends. It extends tacitly ("verlängert sich stillschweigend") for periods of
  equal length unless the European Parliament or the Council opposes the
  extension no later than three months before the period ends.
- **Revocation** ("Widerruf") - Parliament or Council may revoke the delegation
  at any time. The decision takes effect the day after publication in the
  Official Journal, or a later date it names. Delegated acts already in force
  stay valid.
- **Consultation** - before adopting a delegated act the Commission consults
  experts designated by each Member State, following the Interinstitutional
  Agreement on Better Law-Making of 13 April 2016.
- **Notification** - an adopted act is sent to Parliament and Council
  simultaneously.
- **Entry into force** - only if neither Parliament nor Council objects
  ("Einwände erhoben") within two months of notification, or both inform the
  Commission earlier that they will not object. The period is extended by two
  months at the initiative of Parliament or Council.

Practical consequence: CRA content that sits behind these empowerments (for
example the product category lists or reporting details) can change by
delegated act, but only after this scrutiny window has passed.
