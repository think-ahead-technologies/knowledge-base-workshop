# CRA Art. 61 - Exercise of the delegation (Ausübung der Befugnisübertragung)

## Summary
Article 61 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter VI
"Übertragene Befugnisse und Ausschussverfahren") is the standard EU clause on
how the Commission exercises its power to adopt delegated acts ("delegierte
Rechtsakte") under the CRA. It lists the nine empowering provisions, confers the
power for five tacitly renewable years from 10 December 2024, lets Parliament or
Council revoke it at any time, requires consultation of Member State experts,
and gives Parliament and Council a two-month (extendable by two) objection
window before a delegated act enters into force. The captured page is the German
text as republished by Kunnus, with two related recitals (119, 120) shown only as
truncated previews; it has no FAQ. Only the Official Journal text is binding.
