**Article 61: Exercise of the delegation of power**
**Chapter VI: Delegated powers and committee procedure**
**Source:** Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act), published 10 December 2024

**Paragraph summaries:**

1. **Delegation:** The Commission receives the power to adopt delegated acts, subject to the conditions in this article.
2. **Duration:** The power applies for five years from 10 December 2024 for the listed articles. The Commission must report on it at least nine months before expiry. It renews automatically for equal periods unless Parliament or the Council objects at least three months before expiry.
3. **Revocation:** Parliament or the Council can revoke the delegation at any time. Revocation takes effect the day after publication in the Official Journal, or on a later date if specified. Delegated acts already in force are unaffected.
4. **Consultation:** Before adopting a delegated act, the Commission consults national experts, following the 2016 Interinstitutional Agreement on Better Law-Making.
5. **Transmission:** The Commission sends each delegated act to Parliament and the Council at the same time.
6. **Entry into force:** A delegated act enters into force only if neither Parliament nor the Council objects within two months of transmission. That period can be extended by two months at their initiative. Early written confirmation of no objections also allows entry into force.
