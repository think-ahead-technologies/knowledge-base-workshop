# CRA Committee Procedure (Ausschussverfahren)

Art. 62 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 sets up the comitology framework under
which the Commission adopts CRA implementing acts.

- **The committee (Abs. 1)** - the Commission is assisted by a committee, "ein
  Ausschuss im Sinne der Verordnung (EU) Nr. 182/2011", i.e. a committee of
  Member State representatives under the general comitology regulation.
- **Examination procedure (Abs. 2)** - wherever another CRA provision refers to
  Art. 62(2), Article 5 of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 applies. Article 5 of that
  regulation is the examination procedure (Pruefverfahren): the committee votes by
  qualified majority on the draft implementing act (this characterisation of
  Art. 5 comes from Regulation 182/2011 itself, not from the captured page).
- **Written procedure (Abs. 3)** - when the committee's opinion is obtained by
  written procedure ("im schriftlichen Verfahren"), the procedure is terminated
  without result ("ohne Ergebnis abgeschlossen") if, within the deadline for
  delivering the opinion, the committee chair so decides or a committee member so
  requests.

## Mentions
- Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 (comitology regulation), Art. 5
- No other CRA article or annex is referenced in the legal text of Art. 62.
