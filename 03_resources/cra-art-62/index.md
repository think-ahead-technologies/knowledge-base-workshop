# CRA Art. 62 - Committee procedure (Ausschussverfahren)

## Summary
Article 62 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847), in
Chapter VI "Übertragene Befugnisse und Ausschussverfahren", German text as
published by Kunnus. It provides that the Commission is assisted by a committee
within the meaning of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 (Abs. 1), that references to
Art. 62(2) trigger the examination procedure of Article 5 of that regulation
(Abs. 2), and that a written procedure for the committee's opinion ends without
result if the chair decides so or a member requests it within the deadline
(Abs. 3). Useful when tracing how CRA implementing acts are adopted.
