# CRA confidentiality obligations of parties applying the regulation

Art. 63 CRA ("Vertraulichkeit") binds "Alle an der Anwendung dieser Verordnung
beteiligten Parteien" (authorities, the Commission, notified bodies and anyone
else involved in applying the act) to keep confidential the information and data
they learn while performing their tasks. It names four protected interests (para. 1):

- **(a) IP and trade secrets** - "Rechte des geistigen Eigentums, vertrauliche
  Geschäftsinformationen oder Geschäftsgeheimnisse", explicitly "auch Quellcode",
  except the cases in Art. 5 of Directive (EU) 2016/943 (Trade Secrets Directive).
- **(b) Effective enforcement** - especially for "Inspektionen, Untersuchungen oder Audits".
- **(c) Public and national security interests.**
- **(d) Integrity of criminal or administrative proceedings.**

Further rules:

- **Para. 2 - originator consent** - information that market surveillance
  authorities ("Marktüberwachungsbehörden") exchange on a confidential basis among
  themselves or with the Commission is not passed on without the prior consent
  ("vorherige Zustimmung") of the originating authority.
- **Para. 3 - carve-out** - paras. 1-2 do not affect the rights and duties of the
  Commission, Member States and notified bodies ("notifizierte Stellen") to exchange
  information and disseminate warnings, nor duties to provide information under
  Member States' criminal law.
- **Para. 4 - third countries** - the Commission and Member States may exchange
  sensitive information with third-country authorities where bilateral or
  multilateral confidentiality agreements guarantee an adequate level of protection.

Takeaway for manufacturers: technical documentation, source code and other material
handed to authorities or notified bodies during conformity assessment or market
surveillance is protected as confidential, but this does not block authorities from
sharing warnings or cooperating across the EU.
