# CRA Art. 63 - Confidentiality (Vertraulichkeit)

## Summary
Article 63 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter VII
"Vertraulichkeit und Sanktionen") obliges every party involved in applying the
regulation to keep confidential the information they obtain, protecting in
particular IP, trade secrets and source code, effective enforcement (inspections,
investigations, audits), public and national security, and the integrity of
criminal or administrative proceedings. Confidentially exchanged information
between market surveillance authorities needs the originator's consent before
onward disclosure; warnings and criminal-law information duties are unaffected;
exchange with third countries is allowed under confidentiality agreements. The
captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus, with 2 related recitals;
only the Official Journal text is binding.
