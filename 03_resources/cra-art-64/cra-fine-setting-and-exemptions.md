# CRA fine-setting criteria, enforcement and exemptions

**Setting the amount (para. 5).** Each fine is decided case by case, taking into
account all relevant circumstances and in particular:
- (a) nature, gravity and duration of the infringement and its consequences
  ("Art, Schwere und Dauer des Verstoßes und dessen Folgen");
- (b) whether the same or other market surveillance authorities have already
  fined the same economic operator ("Wirtschaftsakteur") for a similar
  infringement;
- (c) size - in particular micro, small and medium-sized enterprises including
  start-ups ("Kleinstunternehmen sowie kleine und mittlere Unternehmen,
  einschließlich Start-up-Unternehmen") - and market share of the operator.

**Enforcement mechanics.**
- Para. 6: market surveillance authorities that impose a fine inform the
  authorities of the other Member States via the information and communication
  system of Art. 34 of Regulation (EU) 2019/1020.
- Para. 7: each Member State decides whether and to what extent fines can be
  imposed on its own public authorities and public bodies.
- Para. 8: depending on the national legal system, fines may be imposed by
  competent national courts or other bodies, with equivalent effect.
- Para. 9: fines may be imposed in addition to other corrective or restrictive
  measures that market surveillance authorities take for the same infringement.

**Exemptions (para. 10).** "Abweichend von den Absätzen 3 bis 9" (as captured),
the fines do not apply to:
- (a) manufacturers that qualify as micro or small enterprises
  ("Kleinst- oder Kleinunternehmen"), as regards failure to meet the deadline in
  Art. 14(2)(a) or Art. 14(4)(a) (the early-warning notification deadlines);
- (b) open-source software stewards ("Verwalter quelloffener Software") for any
  infringement of the regulation.

Note: the captured page text derogates from "paras. 3 to 9", while the Art. 14
obligations themselves fall under the para. 2 tier; check the Official Journal
wording before relying on the exact scope.

Vendor interpretation (Kunnus FAQ, non-binding): lists further factors - number
of affected users, intent or negligence, cooperation, mitigation measures - and
says SME size is a mitigating factor (citing recital 102). Only (a)-(c) above are
in the legal text.
