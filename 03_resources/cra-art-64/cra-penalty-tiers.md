# CRA administrative fine tiers

Art. 64 obliges Member States to lay down rules on penalties ("Sanktionen") for
infringements of the CRA, which must be "wirksam, verhältnismäßig und
abschreckend" (effective, proportionate and dissuasive), and to notify the
Commission of these rules and any later amendment without delay (para. 1). It
then fixes three maximum fine tiers ("Geldbußen"). In each tier the cap is a fixed
amount or - for undertakings ("im Falle von Unternehmen") - a share of the
"gesamten weltweiten Jahresumsatzes des vorangegangenen Geschäftsjahres" (total
worldwide annual turnover of the preceding financial year), "je nachdem, welcher
Betrag höher ist" (whichever is higher):

| Tier | Infringement | Max. fixed | Max. turnover share |
|------|--------------|-----------|---------------------|
| Para. 2 | Non-compliance with the essential cybersecurity requirements of Annex I, or breach of the obligations in Art. 13 and Art. 14 | EUR 15 000 000 | 2.5 % |
| Para. 3 | Breach of the obligations in Art. 18 to 23, Art. 28, Art. 30(1)-(4), Art. 31(1)-(4), Art. 32(1)-(3), Art. 33(5), Art. 39, 41, 47, 49 and 53 | EUR 10 000 000 | 2 % |
| Para. 4 | Supplying incorrect, incomplete or misleading information ("falsche, unvollständige oder irreführende Angaben") to notified bodies or market surveillance authorities in reply to a request | EUR 5 000 000 | 1 % |

The turnover percentage is an alternative ceiling that applies when higher than
the fixed amount; it raises the ceiling for large undertakings rather than
lowering it for small ones.

Vendor interpretation (Kunnus FAQ, non-binding): the page describes the 2.5 %
limit as having a "capping" effect for smaller companies, and says accompanying
measures (sales ban, recall, reputational damage) can be more threatening than
the fine itself. The first claim does not follow from the "whichever is higher"
wording of the legal text.
