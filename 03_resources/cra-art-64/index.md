# CRA Art. 64 - Penalties (Sanktionen)

## Summary
Article 64 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter VII
"Vertraulichkeit und Sanktionen") obliges Member States to lay down effective,
proportionate and dissuasive penalties and sets three maximum fine tiers: EUR 15
million or 2.5 % of worldwide annual turnover (Annex I, Art. 13, Art. 14), EUR 10
million or 2 % (a list of further obligations), and EUR 5 million or 1 % (false
or misleading information to authorities) - whichever is higher. It also sets
fine-setting criteria, cross-border notification, national procedure, and
exemptions for micro/small manufacturers (certain Art. 14 deadlines) and
open-source software stewards. The captured page is the German text as
republished by Kunnus, with a vendor quick answer and FAQ (non-binding), an
excerpt of the Commission guidance, and two related recitals; only the Official
Journal text is binding.
