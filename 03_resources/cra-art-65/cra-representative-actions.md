# CRA representative actions for consumers (Verbandsklagen)

Art. 65 CRA opens a private-enforcement route next to market surveillance and
fines: the Representative Actions Directive, Directive (EU) 2020/1828, applies
to representative actions ("Verbandsklagen") against infringements by economic
operators ("Zuwiderhandlungen durch Wirtschaftsakteure") of CRA provisions that
harm or threaten to harm "die Kollektivinteressen der Verbraucher".

Verbatim legal text (German, as captured):

> Richtlinie (EU) 2020/1828 findet Anwendung auf Verbandsklagen gegen
> Zuwiderhandlungen durch Wirtschaftsakteure gegen Bestimmungen dieser
> Verordnung, die die Kollektivinteressen der Verbraucher beeinträchtigen oder
> zu beeinträchtigen drohen.

Practical reading:
- Who can be sued: any economic operator (manufacturer, importer, distributor)
  that breaches a CRA provision.
- Trigger: the breach affects, or threatens, the collective interests of
  consumers - an actual damage event is not required ("zu beeinträchtigen
  drohen").
- Mechanism: qualified entities under Directive (EU) 2020/1828 (e.g. consumer
  associations) bring the action; the directive allows injunctive and redress
  measures. (The scope of those measures comes from the directive, not from the
  captured page.)
