# CRA Art. 65 - Representative actions (Verbandsklagen)

## Summary
Article 65 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter
VII "Vertraulichkeit und Sanktionen") makes Directive (EU) 2020/1828 on
representative actions applicable to infringements of the CRA by economic
operators ("Wirtschaftsakteure") that harm or threaten to harm "die
Kollektivinteressen der Verbraucher". In effect, consumer associations can bring
collective actions for CRA breaches, in addition to public enforcement (Art. 64
penalties, market surveillance). The captured page is the German text as
republished by Kunnus with one related recital (125); it has no FAQ. Only the
Official Journal text is binding.
