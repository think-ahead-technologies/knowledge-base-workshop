**Artikel 65: Verbandsklagen** (Kapitel VII, Vertraulichkeit und Sanktionen)

Source: Verordnung (EU) 2024/2847, published 10 December 2024. The page was last reviewed by Kunnus in March 2026.

**Legal content (paraphrased):** Article 65 states that the representative actions framework in Directive (EU) 2020/1828 applies to collective actions against economic operators that breach this Regulation in ways that harm, or threaten to harm, the collective interests of consumers.

**Related recital:** Recital 125 says the Commission should regularly evaluate and review the Regulation in consultation with relevant stakeholders, including whether it still fits changing social and political conditions. The recital text on the page is truncated.
