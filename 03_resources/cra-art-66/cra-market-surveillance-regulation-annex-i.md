# CRA listed in Annex I of the Market Surveillance Regulation (EU) 2019/1020

Art. 66 CRA amends Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 on market surveillance and
compliance of products by appending point 72 to its Annex I: "72. Verordnung
(EU) 2024/2847 des Europäischen Parlaments und des Rates". Annex I of
Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 is the list of Union harmonisation legislation to
which that regulation applies (inferred from general knowledge of 2019/1020,
not stated on the captured page). Effect: the general EU market surveillance
rules (powers of market surveillance authorities, cooperation, controls at
external borders) become applicable to products with digital elements under
the CRA, alongside the CRA's own market surveillance chapter.

The captured page gives only the amending sentence; footnote (1) after the
regulation name is not reproduced there.
