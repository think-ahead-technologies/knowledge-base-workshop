# CRA Art. 66 - Amendment to Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 (Änderung der Verordnung (EU) 2019/1020)

## Summary
Article 66 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter
VIII "Übergangs- und Schlussbestimmungen") is a one-sentence amending provision:
it appends a new point 72 to Annex I of the EU Market Surveillance Regulation
(EU) 2019/1020, naming Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 ("In Anhang I der Verordnung
(EU) 2019/1020 wird folgende Nummer angefügt: 72. Verordnung (EU) 2024/2847 des
Europäischen Parlaments und des Rates"). This puts the CRA on the list of Union
harmonisation legislation covered by the general market surveillance framework.
The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus, with one related
recital (126, truncated); it has no article FAQ. Only the Official Journal text
is binding.
