# CRA Art. 67 - Amendment to Directive (EU) 2020/1828 (Änderung der Richtlinie (EU) 2020/1828)

## Summary
Article 67 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter VIII
"Übergangs- und Schlussbestimmungen") is a one-sentence amendment: it appends
point (69) "Verordnung (EU) 2024/2847 des Europäischen Parlaments und des Rates"
to Annex I of Directive (EU) 2020/1828. This brings the CRA into the scope of
the EU representative actions (collective consumer redress) framework. The
captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus (last reviewed by
Kunnus March 2026); it carries no FAQ, vendor guidance or recitals for this
article. Only the Official Journal text is binding.
