# CRA added to Annex I of the Representative Actions Directive (EU) 2020/1828

Art. 67 CRA ("Änderung der Richtlinie (EU) 2020/1828") amends Directive (EU)
2020/1828 by appending a new point to its Annex I. The captured legal text reads:
"In Anhang I der Richtlinie (EU) 2020/1828 wird folgende Nummer angefügt:
„(69) Verordnung (EU) 2024/2847 des Europäischen Parlaments und des Rates (1)."

Effect: the Cyber Resilience Act becomes item (69) in the list of Union law
covered by Directive (EU) 2020/1828. Background (not stated on the captured
page; general knowledge of that directive): Directive (EU) 2020/1828 is the EU
directive on representative actions for the protection of the collective
interests of consumers, and its Annex I lists the Union acts whose infringement
qualified entities may pursue through representative actions. Consequently,
infringements of CRA obligations that harm consumers' collective interests can
be the subject of such actions. The footnote "(1)" refers to the Official
Journal citation of the CRA; its text is not present in the captured page.

## Mentions
- Directive (EU) 2020/1828, Annex I (amended; not a CRA article)
- No other CRA article or annex is referenced in the legal text of Art. 67.
