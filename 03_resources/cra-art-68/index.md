# CRA Art. 68 - Amendment to Regulation (EU) No 168/2013 (Änderungen der Verordnung (EU) Nr. 168/2013)

## Summary
Article 68 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter VIII
"Übergangs- und Schlussbestimmungen") amends another EU act: it appends one entry
to the table in Annex II Part C1 ("Anhang II Teil C1") of Regulation (EU)
No 168/2013. The new row is numbered "16 18" and reads "Schutz des Fahrzeugs
gegen Cyberangriffe" (protection of the vehicle against cyberattacks), marked
with "x" across all columns of the table. The article itself contains no further
substantive text. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus;
it carries no FAQ and no recital list. Only the Official Journal text is binding.

Context (not stated on the page, background knowledge): Regulation (EU)
No 168/2013 governs approval and market surveillance of two- or three-wheel
vehicles and quadricycles (L-category vehicles); the columns of the Annex II
table correspond to vehicle categories. Verify against EUR-Lex before relying on it.
