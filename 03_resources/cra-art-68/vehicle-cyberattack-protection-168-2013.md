# Vehicle cyberattack protection in Regulation (EU) No 168/2013

CRA Art. 68 adds an entry to the table in Annex II Part C1 of Regulation (EU)
No 168/2013: row "16 18" with the requirement "Schutz des Fahrzeugs gegen
Cyberangriffe" (protection of the vehicle against cyberattacks), marked "x" in
every column. The effect is that protection against cyberattacks becomes a listed
requirement in that regulation's table of requirements. The page gives no further
detail (no date of application, no delegated act reference) beyond this row.

Background (inferred, not from the page): Regulation (EU) No 168/2013 covers
type-approval and market surveillance of two- or three-wheel vehicles and
quadricycles; the columns are the L-category vehicle classes, so the "x" in every
column would mean the requirement applies to all of them.
