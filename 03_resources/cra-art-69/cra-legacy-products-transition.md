# CRA legacy products and substantial modification

Art. 69(2) keeps products with digital elements that were placed on the market
("in den Verkehr gebracht") before 11 December 2027 outside the CRA requirements -
unless they undergo a substantial modification ("wesentliche Änderung") after that
date. Art. 69(3) derogates from this: the obligations of Art. 14 (reporting of
actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents) apply to all in-scope
products placed on the market before 11 December 2027 as well.

Interpretation on the page (non-binding): the Commission guidance (section 4.4) says
a substantially modified product counts as newly placed on the market and the party
making the modification becomes manufacturer, limited to the modified parts while
overall cybersecurity is unaffected; the original manufacturer's obligations
continue. The Kunnus FAQ states that security updates and bugfixes are not
substantial modifications, and that stock made available after the cut-off date is
covered because the exemption depends on placing on the market of each unit.
