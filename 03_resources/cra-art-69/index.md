# CRA Art. 69 - Transitional provisions (Übergangsbestimmungen)

## Summary
Article 69 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter VIII
"Übergangs- und Schlussbestimmungen") governs the transition from the old to the
new legal regime in three paragraphs: (1) EU type-examination certificates and
approvals ("EU-Baumusterprüfbescheinigungen und Zulassungen") issued for
cybersecurity requirements under other Union harmonisation legislation stay valid
until 11 June 2028 unless they expire earlier or that other legislation says
otherwise; (2) products with digital elements placed on the market before
11 December 2027 are subject to the CRA requirements only if they undergo a
substantial modification ("wesentliche Änderung") after that date; (3) by way of
derogation, the Art. 14 obligations (reporting) apply to all in-scope products
placed on the market before 11 December 2027. The captured page is the German text
as republished by Kunnus, with a vendor quick answer, summaries of the Commission
guidance and a vendor FAQ (all non-binding); only the Official Journal text is
binding.

- **Commission guidance C(2026) 5252 final of 27 July 2026 (as summarised by Kunnus, non-binding)** - section 4.4: a substantially modified product counts as newly placed on the market; a third party modifying it becomes manufacturer (Art. 21/22), limited to the modified part if overall cybersecurity is unaffected; integrating components into an own product is regular manufacturing (example 51). Section 9.1: Art. 14 reporting applies since 11 September 2026 to all in-scope products incl. legacy and end-of-support products; deadlines run from "Bewusstwerden" (24 h early warning, 72 h notification, 14 days / 1 month final report); no retroactive reporting. Section 9.3: vehicle-component exclusion depends on objective distribution channels; RED delegated act / Machinery Regulation certificates serve as CRA evidence only for the risks they cover and at most until 11 June 2028.
- **Vendor FAQ (Kunnus, non-binding)** - legacy products stay outside the CRA except Art. 14 reporting; security updates and bugfixes are said not to be substantial modifications, functional changes or new interfaces are; stock ("Lagerware") made available after 11 December 2027 is claimed to fall under the CRA because the exemption is tied to "Inverkehrbringen", per unit. Verify against the legal text.
