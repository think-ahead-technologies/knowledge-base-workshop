# CRA evaluation and review reports

Art. 70 obliges the European Commission to report on the CRA to the European
Parliament and the Council in two ways:

- **General evaluation and review** ("Bericht über die Bewertung und Überprüfung
  dieser Verordnung") - first by 11 December 2030, then every four years. The
  reports are published ("Die Berichte werden veröffentlicht").
- **Single reporting platform report** - by 11 September 2028, after consulting
  ENISA and the CSIRT network. It assesses the effectiveness of the "einheitliche
  Meldeplattform" (Art. 16) and the impact of coordinator CSIRTs ("als
  Koordinatoren benannten CSIRTs") invoking the cybersecurity grounds of Art. 16(2)
  on the timely forwarding of received notifications to other relevant CSIRTs.

Practical relevance: the 2028 report is the first formal checkpoint on how the
vulnerability / incident reporting pipeline works; changes to the reporting
platform may follow from it (inferred, not stated in the text).
