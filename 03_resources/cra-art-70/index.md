# CRA Art. 70 - Evaluation and review (Bewertung und Überprüfung)

## Summary
Article 70 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter VIII
"Übergangs- und Schlussbestimmungen") sets the Commission's review duties. (1) By
11 December 2030 and every four years thereafter, the Commission submits a report
on the evaluation and review of the regulation to the European Parliament and the
Council; the reports are published. (2) By 11 September 2028, after consulting
ENISA and the CSIRT network ("CSIRT-Netz"), the Commission reports on the
effectiveness of the single reporting platform ("einheitliche Meldeplattform")
under Art. 16, and on how coordinator CSIRTs invoking the cybersecurity grounds of
Art. 16(2) affect the timely forwarding of received notifications to other
relevant CSIRTs. The captured page is the German text as republished by Kunnus;
only the Official Journal text is binding.
