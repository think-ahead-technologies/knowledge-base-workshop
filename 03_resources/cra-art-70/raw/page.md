**Article 70: Bewertung und Überprüfung (Evaluation and Review)**

- **Chapter:** VIII, Übergangs- und Schlussbestimmungen (Transitional and Final Provisions)
- **Regulation:** (EU) 2024/2847, published 10 December 2024
- **Last checked by Kunnus:** March 2026

**Paragraph 1:** The Commission must report to Parliament and Council on the evaluation and review of the Regulation by 11 December 2030, and every four years after that. The reports are published.

**Paragraph 2:** By 11 September 2028, the Commission must report on the single reporting platform under Article 16. The report, prepared after consulting ENISA and the CSIRT network, assesses how well the platform works and how cybersecurity grounds invoked by coordinator CSIRTs (Article 16(2)) affect timely forwarding of reports to other relevant CSIRTs.
