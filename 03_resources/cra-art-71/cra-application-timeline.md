# CRA entry into force and staged application dates

Art. 71 separates "Inkrafttreten" (entry into force) from "Geltungsbeginn"
(date of application) and stages the application in three steps:

| Date | What applies (per Art. 71(2)) |
|------|-------------------------------|
| 20th day after publication in the Official Journal | Regulation enters into force (Art. 71(1): "tritt am zwanzigsten Tag nach ihrer Veröffentlichung im Amtsblatt der Europäischen Union in Kraft") |
| 11 June 2026 | Chapter IV (Art. 35 to 51) - "Kapitel IV (Artikel 35 bis 51) gilt ab dem 11. Juni 2026" |
| 11 September 2026 | Art. 14 - "Artikel 14 gilt jedoch ab dem 11. September 2026" |
| 11 December 2027 | The rest of the regulation - "Diese Verordnung gilt ab dem 11. Dezember 2027" |

The regulation is "in allen ihren Teilen verbindlich und gilt unmittelbar in
jedem Mitgliedstaat" - binding in its entirety and directly applicable in every
Member State, so no national transposition is needed (it is a regulation, not
a directive). Signed in Strasbourg on 23 October 2024.

Practical reading: the article text itself does not name the content of
Art. 14 or Chapter IV. On the vendor page, Art. 14 is presented as the
reporting obligations ("Die ersten Pflichten ... für Hersteller gelten seit dem
11. September 2026" - vendor banner, not legal text).
