# CRA Art. 71 - Entry into force and application (Inkrafttreten und Geltungsbeginn)

## Summary
Article 71 is the last article of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU)
2024/2847, Chapter VIII "Übergangs- und Schlussbestimmungen"). Paragraph 1 sets
entry into force on the twentieth day after publication in the Official
Journal. Paragraph 2 sets the general date of application to 11 December 2027,
with two earlier dates: Art. 14 from 11 September 2026 and Chapter IV
(Art. 35 to 51) from 11 June 2026. It closes with the standard clause that the
regulation is binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member
States, signed in Strasbourg on 23 October 2024 (R. Metsola for the Parliament,
Zsigmond B. P. for the Council). The captured page is the German text as
republished by Kunnus; only the Official Journal text is binding.
