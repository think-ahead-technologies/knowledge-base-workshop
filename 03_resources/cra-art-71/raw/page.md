**Article 71: Entry into force and application**

- **Entry into force:** The regulation takes effect on the twentieth day after its publication in the Official Journal of the EU. It was published on 10 December 2024.
- **General application:** The regulation applies from 11 December 2027. The page quotes this as "Diese Verordnung gilt ab dem 11. Dezember 2027."
- **Early application:** Article 14 (reporting obligations) applies from 11 September 2026. Chapter IV (Articles 35 to 51) applies from 11 June 2026.
- **Binding nature:** The regulation is binding in all its parts and applies directly in every Member State.
- **Adoption:** It was signed in Strasbourg on 23 October 2024.
